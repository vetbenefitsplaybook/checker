Virginia Disabled Veteran Benefits by Rating (2026)

8 Virginia benefits to look into, with the rating each one starts at. Use the checker to filter by your rating.

Virginia state benefits

Last verified 2026-10-29

  • Real Estate Tax Exemption for 100% P&T Veterans

    100% + P&T
    • VA rating of 100% service-connected, permanent and total. Per DVS, veterans paid at 100% for individual unemployability and rated P&T also qualify
    • You own the home and live in it as your principal residence (joint property with your spouse counts)
    • Exempts the dwelling and up to one acre of land (more if your locality exempts more acres for its elderly/disabled program); a garage or shed for personal use on that land is also exempt
    • Surviving spouse who doesn't remarry keeps it (veteran died on or after Jan. 1, 2011), even after moving to another home

    Official source: DVS tax exemptions · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • Personal Property Tax Exemption on One Vehicle (100% P&T)

    100% + P&T
    • Veteran rated 100% service-connected, permanent and total by VA
    • One passenger car or pickup/panel truck registered for personal use, owned and used primarily by or for the veteran (a vehicle owned by the veteran's spouse can qualify)
    • Ends at the veteran's death; not available to a surviving spouse

    Official source: DVS tax exemptions · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • Free DV Plates + No Registration Fee (One Vehicle)

    P&T
    • Veteran who has lost, or lost the use of, a leg, arm or hand; is blind; or is permanently and totally disabled, as certified by VA
    • Free: one set of Disabled Veteran or standard plates, with no annual registration fee, on one vehicle you own and use personally. Personalization and emissions fees still apply
    • Unremarried surviving spouse can also get one free set

    Official source: DMV Disabled Veteran plates · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • Disabled Veteran (DV) Plates for Any Service-Connected Rating

    Any rating
    • Any veteran with a service-connected disability certified by VA (any rating)
    • Cost: a $10 yearly special plate fee plus regular registration fees; personalization fees apply if you personalize, and emissions fees, if applicable

    Official source: DMV Disabled Veteran plates · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • Lifetime Hunting & Freshwater Fishing License (30%+)

    30%+
    • Virginia resident with a VA service-connected rating of 30% or more
    • Price per license (fishing and hunting are separate): P&T $0 · 70%+ $50 · 50–69% $75 · 30–49% $100. Lifetime trapping is $15 (P&T only)
    • Doesn't cover trout-stocked waters during the stocking season or National/State Forest permits; buy those separately

    Official source: DWR veterans · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • Free Veteran Disability Passport for State Parks (100%)

    100%+
    • Veteran with a 100% service-connected disability rating
    • Free (per DCR): parking and admission for you and anyone needed to assist you, plus boat launch and horse trailer parking
    • 50% off camping, swimming, shelters, park-run equipment rentals and 2+ night standard yurt stays (25% off 1-night yurts)

    Official source: Virginia State Parks passes · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • Military Benefits Subtraction: Up to $40,000

    Other rules
    • Military retirement income, Survivor Benefit Plan payments to a surviving spouse, or other military benefits paid to a surviving spouse
    • Up to $40,000 a year (tax year 2025 and later), and only income that's taxable on your federal return. Each eligible spouse can take up to the limit
    • Not for TSP withdrawals or CSRS/FERS pensions

    Official source: Virginia Tax military benefits FAQ · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • VMSDEP: Tuition Waiver for Spouses and Children (90%+ or P&T)

    90%+ or P&T
    • Veteran rated by VA P&T or at least 90% permanently disabled due to service, with a discharge other than dishonorable
    • Veteran meets the Virginia domicile rule (for example, lived in Virginia at entry into service, or for the 5 years right before the student applied to college)
    • Current spouse, or a biological/adopted child aged 16–29, admitted to a Virginia public college or university
    • Waives tuition and mandatory fees for up to 8 semesters. A stipend is only for combat-related cases (Tier 2)

    Official source: DVS VMSDEP · Last verified 2026-10-29

Federal benefits to look into

  • CHAMPVA: health coverage for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    It's for family members, not the veteran: the spouse or dependent child of a veteran rated P&T from a service-connected disability. It's a cost-sharing program: VA shares the cost of covered care with the family. Not free. Family members eligible for TRICARE can't get CHAMPVA.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DEA (Chapter 35): school money for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    Chapter 35 / DEA education benefits are available to the spouse and children of a veteran who is P&T due to a service-connected disability. For school or training that started on or after Aug 1, 2018, DEA pays up to 36 months of benefits.

    Official source: VA: DEA · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Social Security: faster disability decisions

    100% + P&T

    SSA expedites disability claims from veterans rated 100% P&T. Say "veteran rated 100% P&T" (or enter "Veteran 100% P&T" in the online Remarks). It doesn't guarantee approval; SSA's own rules still apply.

    Official source: SSA: Veterans · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Dental insurance for CHAMPVA families (VADIP)

    P&T

    CHAMPVA covers dental only in limited medical cases; enrollees can buy dental insurance at reduced cost through VADIP.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Your Benefit Summary Letter (proof of your rating)

    All veterans

    You can download your Benefit Summary Letter (sometimes called an award letter) on VA.gov.

    Official source: VA: Download VA letters · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • VA dental care (Class IV)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Per VA, you qualify for any needed dental care if you have a service-connected disability rated 100% disabling, or you're paid at the 100% rate because of TDIU. A temporary 100% rating does not qualify.

    Official source: VA: Dental care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DoD ID card and base privileges (100%)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    A 100% disabled veteran (DAV) and eligible family members can get a DoD Uniformed Services ID card. It gives commissary, exchange and MWR privileges and base access. It is not TRICARE.

    Official source: eCFR: 32 CFR part 161 · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Base retail access for all service-connected veterans

    Any rating

    All service-connected veterans can use commissaries, exchanges and MWR retail facilities, in person and online, per VA's matrix.

    Official source: VA: Derivative benefits matrix · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Federal student loan discharge (TPD)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Veterans whom VA has determined have a service-connected disability rated 100% or are rated totally disabled based on individual unemployability (TDIU) can have eligible federal student loans discharged.

    Official source: Federal Student Aid: TPD discharge · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • No VA home loan funding fee

    10%+

    Veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability are exempt from the VA home loan funding fee, at any compensable rating, not just 100%.

    Official source: VA: Funding fee · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • America the Beautiful Access Pass

    Other rules

    A free, lifetime pass for U.S. citizens or permanent residents with a permanent disability. It doesn't have to be 100% or service-connected.

    Official source: NPS: Access Pass · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Military Lifetime Pass (federal lands)

    All veterans

    Veterans (and Gold Star families) can get a free lifetime pass to federal recreation lands. No disability is needed.

    Official source: NPS: Veterans free access · Last verified 2026-11-01
