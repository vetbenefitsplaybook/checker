Georgia Disabled Veteran Benefits by Rating (2026)

8 Georgia benefits to look into, with the rating each one starts at. Use the checker to filter by your rating.

Georgia state benefits

Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Disabled Veteran Homestead Exemption: Up to $126,526 (2026)

    100%+
    • Honorably discharged Georgia veteran who is VA-rated 100%, or rated less but paid at the 100% rate due to unemployability
    • Or entitled to a VA statutory award for loss or loss of use of one or both feet or hands, loss of sight in one or both eyes, or permanent impairment of both eyes
    • Exempts up to $126,526 of assessed value for 2026 from state, county, city and school taxes; assessed value above that stays taxable (the amount tracks VA's yearly housing-grant maximum)
    • You own the home and live in it as your primary residence. An unremarried surviving spouse or minor children keep it while they live there

    Official source: GDVS homestead page · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Free Disabled Veteran Plates + Vehicle Tax Exemption

    100%+
    • Georgia resident discharged under honorable conditions who is VA-rated 100% (permanent, or non-permanent with yearly recertification), or rated less but paid at the 100% rate due to unemployability, or entitled to a VA statutory award for loss or loss of use of feet or hands, loss of sight, or the vision impairment above
    • No registration, manufacturing or annual fee for the plates. An unremarried surviving spouse is also eligible
    • VA-rated 100% totally disabled, or paid at the 100% rate due to unemployability: no ad valorem tax (annual or title tax) on one vehicle you own
    • With the plate displayed, you may park in disability spaces (no extra permit)

    Official source: Ga. DOR veteran plates · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Free Veteran Driver's License

    All veterans
    • Georgia resident veteran with an honorable or medical discharge (some National Guard service also qualifies)
    • The license or ID shows a U.S. flag and the word VETERAN
    • The spouse of a veteran whose disabilities keep them from driving, or an unremarried surviving spouse, can get a free honorary license

    Official source: DDS veteran license · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Discounted Hunting & Fishing Licenses (P&T)

    P&T
    • Georgia resident veteran with a permanent and total disability rating from VA (or another government agency) for a physical or mental impairment
    • Eligible for a 3-year disability sportsman's license at a steep discount (mental impairment: a discounted disability fishing license)
    • Separately: a free one-time 1-year sportsman's license for resident veterans honorably discharged on or after July 1, 2005 with 90+ days of active federal duty
    • Hunter education is required for hunting privileges if born on or after Jan 1, 1961

    Official source: GDVS hunting & fishing page · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • 25% Off State Park Fees

    Any rating
    • Georgia veteran with a discharge under honorable conditions and a VA service-connected disability rating
    • 25% off individual accommodations, recreation fees (like mini-golf and boat rental), golf fees, parking fees and historic site admission at Georgia state parks and historic sites; show your Certificate of Eligibility card

    Official source: GDVS 2026 Resource Guide · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Military & Disability Retirement Income Exclusion

    Other rules
    • Under 62: exclude up to $17,500 of military retirement income, plus up to $17,500 more if you have over $17,500 of earned income in Georgia. Spouses each qualify separately
    • 62 to 64: up to $35,000 of retirement income; 65+: up to $65,000
    • If you are permanently and totally disabled, you may claim the general retirement exclusion at any age

    Official source: Ga. DOR retirement income exclusion · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • 10-Point Preference for State Jobs (10%+)

    10%+
    • Honorably discharged veteran with wartime (armed-conflict) service and a VA service-connected rating of 10% or more: 10 points (instead of 5) added to a passing score on a scored State of Georgia entrance exam; where no scored exam is used, preference applies among equally qualified applicants
    • Also: veterans over 55 receiving disability pension or compensation, spouses of disabled veterans who can't work due to the disability, and unmarried surviving spouses of wartime veterans
    • Other wartime veterans get 5 points

    Official source: GDVS special state provisions · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Business Certificate of Exemption (10%+)

    10%+
    • Georgia veteran discharged under honorable conditions with a 10%+ service-connected rating; the probate court confirms any other eligibility conditions
    • Exempts you for 10 years from local occupation taxes and administrative or regulatory fees for running a business or practicing a profession

    Official source: GDVS business certificate · Last verified 2026-10-30

Federal benefits to look into

  • CHAMPVA: health coverage for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    It's for family members, not the veteran: the spouse or dependent child of a veteran rated P&T from a service-connected disability. It's a cost-sharing program: VA shares the cost of covered care with the family. Not free. Family members eligible for TRICARE can't get CHAMPVA.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DEA (Chapter 35): school money for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    Chapter 35 / DEA education benefits are available to the spouse and children of a veteran who is P&T due to a service-connected disability. For school or training that started on or after Aug 1, 2018, DEA pays up to 36 months of benefits.

    Official source: VA: DEA · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Social Security: faster disability decisions

    100% + P&T

    SSA expedites disability claims from veterans rated 100% P&T. Say "veteran rated 100% P&T" (or enter "Veteran 100% P&T" in the online Remarks). It doesn't guarantee approval; SSA's own rules still apply.

    Official source: SSA: Veterans · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Dental insurance for CHAMPVA families (VADIP)

    P&T

    CHAMPVA covers dental only in limited medical cases; enrollees can buy dental insurance at reduced cost through VADIP.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Your Benefit Summary Letter (proof of your rating)

    All veterans

    You can download your Benefit Summary Letter (sometimes called an award letter) on VA.gov.

    Official source: VA: Download VA letters · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • VA dental care (Class IV)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Per VA, you qualify for any needed dental care if you have a service-connected disability rated 100% disabling, or you're paid at the 100% rate because of TDIU. A temporary 100% rating does not qualify.

    Official source: VA: Dental care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DoD ID card and base privileges (100%)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    A 100% disabled veteran (DAV) and eligible family members can get a DoD Uniformed Services ID card. It gives commissary, exchange and MWR privileges and base access. It is not TRICARE.

    Official source: eCFR: 32 CFR part 161 · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Base retail access for all service-connected veterans

    Any rating

    All service-connected veterans can use commissaries, exchanges and MWR retail facilities, in person and online, per VA's matrix.

    Official source: VA: Derivative benefits matrix · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Federal student loan discharge (TPD)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Veterans whom VA has determined have a service-connected disability rated 100% or are rated totally disabled based on individual unemployability (TDIU) can have eligible federal student loans discharged.

    Official source: Federal Student Aid: TPD discharge · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • No VA home loan funding fee

    10%+

    Veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability are exempt from the VA home loan funding fee, at any compensable rating, not just 100%.

    Official source: VA: Funding fee · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • America the Beautiful Access Pass

    Other rules

    A free, lifetime pass for U.S. citizens or permanent residents with a permanent disability. It doesn't have to be 100% or service-connected.

    Official source: NPS: Access Pass · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Military Lifetime Pass (federal lands)

    All veterans

    Veterans (and Gold Star families) can get a free lifetime pass to federal recreation lands. No disability is needed.

    Official source: NPS: Veterans free access · Last verified 2026-11-01
