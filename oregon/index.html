Oregon Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Rating guide

Any service-connected rating: free state park camping and day-use parking, permanent DV registration, 10-point hiring preference (items 4, 5, 7) · 25%+: free hunting, fishing and shellfish license, reduced elk tag (items 3, 8) · 40%+: property tax exemption (item 1) · 50%+: Wounded Warrior parking placard (item 6) · Surviving spouses: property tax exemption (item 2). (page 1 rating guide; each part matches the item it points to)

Last verified 2026-11-01. Official sources are on each benefit below.

Oregon state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Disabled Veteran Property Tax Exemption

    • Oregon resident veteran, honorably discharged, rated 40%+ service-connected by the VA or a military branch: $32,512 off your home's assessed value (2026 amount)
    • Veteran certified 40%+ disabled but not service-connected (or certified yearly by a doctor, with an income limit): $27,092
    • You must own and live in the home. The amount rises 3% a year. You don't refile yearly if your VA rating backs the claim
    • File with your county assessor: claim form · DOR guide
    What to gather (4)
    • Form 150-303-086 (claim)
    • DD-214
    • VA rating certificate showing 40%+
    • Property account number

    Official source: claim form · Official source: DOR guide · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Surviving Spouse Property Tax Exemption

    • Surviving spouse or registered domestic partner of a veteran who hasn't remarried or entered a new partnership
    • $32,512 if the veteran died from a service-connected injury or illness or got at least one year of the maximum exemption; otherwise $27,092
    • You must own and live in the home; the exemption doesn't move to a new home without a new claim
    • File with your county assessor: claim form · DOR guide
    What to gather (4)
    • Form 150-303-086
    • Veteran's DD-214
    • Death certificate
    • Proof of the service-connected death or prior exemption, if claiming $32,512

    Official source: claim form · Official source: DOR guide · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Free Hunting, Angling, and Shellfish Licenses at 25%+

    • A resident who files written proof that the latest official certification from VA, or from a branch of the U.S. armed forces, shows a disability of at least 25 percent
    • The resident disabled veteran hunting license, angling license, and shellfish license are free

    Official source: ORS 497.061, 497.102, and 497.121 · Last verified 2026-11-09

  • State Parks Special Access Pass

    • Oregon veteran with any service-connected disability
    • Free day-use parking and free RV, tent and standard horse campsites, up to 4 nights at a time or 4 nights a calendar month
    • Not covered: cabins, yurts, the $10 reservation fee, a second vehicle or dump stations. Pass is good 10 years; spouses and survivors don't qualify
    • Apply with the online form before you reserve: Oregon State Parks
    What to gather (2)
    • VA letter stating you have a service-connected disability
    • Oregon driver's license or ID, or mail with an Oregon address

    Official source: Oregon State Parks · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Permanent Disabled Veteran Registration

    • Veteran with a service-connected disability, honorably discharged, with 90+ consecutive days of service or discharged early for a service-connected injury or illness
    • Pay a one-time registration fee; no renewals or renewal fees after that
    • One vehicle per qualifying veteran in the household; commercial vehicles excluded. Regular plate fees still apply
    • Apply through Oregon DMV: ODVA plates page
    What to gather (3)
    • VA or military letter certifying you are a disabled veteran
    • DD-214
    • Vehicle title and registration

    Official source: ODVA plates page · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Oregon Wounded Warrior Parking Placard

    • Veteran with a VA service-connected rating of 50%+, discharged under other than dishonorable conditions
    • Lets you park at public meters without paying and stay past time limits (not in 30-minutes-or-less or no-parking zones)
    • Must hold an Oregon driver license, permit or ID card; the placard is good 8 years
    • Apply through Oregon DMV: DMV parking permits
    What to gather (3)
    • Form 735-265
    • VA letter showing 50%+
    • DD-214 or other discharge document

    Official source: DMV parking permits · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • 10-Point Public Job Hiring Preference

    • Disabled veteran: any VA disability rating, a discharge for a line-of-duty disability, or a Purple Heart
    • Public employers add 10 percentage points to your screening and exam scores (5 points for other veterans)
    • Applies to state, county and local civil service jobs and promotions when you meet the minimum qualifications
    • Claim it when you apply for the job: ODVA employment page
    What to gather (3)
    • DD-214
    • VA rating letter
    • Any preference form the job posting asks for

    Official source: ODVA employment page · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Reduced-Price Elk Tag

    • Holder of the resident disabled veteran hunting license (at least 25 percent disabled)
    • Special annual elk tag for $29

    Official source: ORS 497.061 and 497.112 · Last verified 2026-11-09
