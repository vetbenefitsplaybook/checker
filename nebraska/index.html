Nebraska Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Where to get help

The NDVA State Service Office's accredited veterans service officers help you apply for state and federal benefits, and its services are 100% free (NDVA State Service Office; 402-420-4021). Your County Veterans Service Officer can also help.

Official source: County Veterans Service Officer · Official source: NDVA State Service Office · Last verified 2026-11-03

Rating guide

100% permanent (or paid at 100% for unemployability): full homestead exemption, no income limit (item 1) · 100% temporary: homestead exemption (item 3) · Nebraska residents at 50%+: free lifetime hunt/fish/fur permit and park entry permit (items 6, 7) · 10%+: Disabled Veteran plates with no plate fee (item 5) · Any service-connected rating: vehicle tax and fee exemption on one vehicle (item 4). (page 1 rating guide; each part matches the item it points to)

Nebraska state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Full Homestead Exemption at 100%

    • Veteran discharged honorable or general, paid by VA for a 100% service-connected permanent disability or a 100% unemployability rating, effective by January 1
    • Exempts 100% of the exempt amount on your home, with no income or home value limit (Category 4V)
    • You must own and live in the home from January 1 through August 15
    • File with your county assessor after February 1 and by June 30: Form 458 (PDF) · Homestead guide (PDF)
    What to gather (2)
    • Form 458, Homestead Exemption Application (first year only, and again if your status changes)
    • VA certification of 100% permanent or unemployability

    Official source: Neb. Rev. Stat. 77-3506 · Official source: Form 458 (PDF) · Official source: Homestead guide (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Surviving Spouse Homestead Exemption

    • Unremarried surviving spouse (or one who remarried at 57 or older) of a veteran paid at 100% permanent or for unemployability, or of a veteran who died of a service-connected disability
    • Also the surviving spouse of a service member who died on active duty from a service-connected cause, or during wartime
    • No income or home value limit (Category 4S); you apply once, and again only if your status changes
    • File with your county assessor after February 1 and by June 30: 2026 category table (PDF) · Homestead guide (PDF)
    What to gather (3)
    • Form 458 (first year only, and again if your status changes)
    • VA certification (with your first application)
    • Death certificate, if your assessor asks

    Official source: Neb. Rev. Stat. 77-3506 · Official source: 2026 category table (PDF) · Official source: Homestead guide (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Homestead Exemption at 100% Temporary

    • Veteran discharged honorable or general, paid by VA for a 100% service-connected temporary disability effective by January 1, or that veteran's unremarried surviving spouse (or one who remarried at 57 or older)
    • No income or home value limit (Category 7)
    • Unlike item 1, you must apply every year with a new VA certification
    • File with your county assessor after February 1 and by June 30: Form 458 (PDF) · 2026 category table (PDF)
    What to gather (2)
    • Form 458
    • VA certification of the 100% temporary rating

    Official source: Neb. Rev. Stat. 77-3506 · Official source: 2026 category table (PDF) · Official source: Form 458 (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Vehicle Tax and Fee Exemption (One Vehicle)

    • Veteran with an honorable discharge and any service-connected rating (including 0%), or one paid VA compensation or pension, or a VA-recognized blind veteran
    • Since January 1, 2026: no Motor Vehicle Tax or Motor Vehicle Fee on one personal vehicle; plate fees, sales tax and wheel tax still apply
    • Ask within 30 days of buying, or before the end of your renewal month; it then carries over automatically. Surviving spouses don't qualify
    • Claim it at the county treasurer, by mail, or online at renewal: NDVA vehicle tax exemption · NDVA registry
    What to gather (2)
    • Join the NDVA Military & Veterans' Registry with your VA benefit summary
    • Social Security number when you register or renew

    Official source: NDVA registry · Official source: NDVA vehicle tax exemption · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Disabled Veteran Plates (10%+)

    • Veteran discharged honorable or general with a VA service-connected rating of 10% or more
    • No license plate fee for the standard plates; registration and other fees still apply. Personalized plates cost $40 when issued and $40 at each renewal
    • Plates with the wheelchair symbol need a permanent disability certification
    • Mail to Nebraska DMV, PO Box 94789, Lincoln, NE 68509: DMV application (PDF)
    What to gather (3)
    • Join the NDVA Military & Veterans' Registry
    • DMV Application for Disabled Veteran License Plate
    • Nebraska title number and VIN

    Official source: DMV application (PDF) · Official source: Neb. Rev. Stat. 60-3,124 · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Lifetime Hunt, Fish and Fur Permit

    • Nebraska resident veteran discharged honorable or general, rated 50% or more service-connected (or on a VA pension for total and permanent disability)
    • A free, perpetual small game hunt, fish and fur harvest permit, including the habitat, aquatic habitat and state waterfowl stamps
    • Big game permits aren't included
    • Mail the application to the VA office at 3800 Village Drive, Lincoln, NE 68516, for certification: Game and Parks veteran permits
    What to gather (2)
    • Disabled Veteran Permit application
    • VA proof of your rating

    Official source: Neb. Rev. Stat. 37-420 · Official source: Game and Parks veteran permits · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Lifetime Park Entry Permit

    • Nebraska resident veteran rated 50% or more service-connected (honorable or general), or receiving a VA pension for total and permanent disability
    • One free, perpetual Disabled Veteran's Lifetime Annual Park Entry Permit for your vehicle
    • Lost or stolen permits can't be replaced; damaged ones can
    • Mail to the VA office at 3800 Village Drive, Lincoln, NE 68516, or to the Game and Parks Permit Section if you already hold item 6: Game and Parks veteran permits
    What to gather (2)
    • Disabled Veteran Permit application (same form as item 6)
    • VA proof of your rating

    Official source: Game and Parks veteran permits · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Military Retirement Pay Not Taxed

    • All military retirees, for tax years starting January 1, 2022 or later
    • 100% of military retirement benefits included in federal AGI can be excluded
    • No election or separate form is needed
    • Claim it on your Nebraska return: DOR military retirement FAQ
    What to gather (2)
    • Your DFAS (or OPM) 1099-R
    • Nebraska Form 1040N and its instructions

    Official source: DOR military retirement FAQ · Last verified 2026-11-03
