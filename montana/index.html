Montana Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Where to get help

the Montana Veterans Affairs Division has Veteran Service Offices across the state that help veterans and families find and apply for federal, state and local benefits (Army benefits summary; MVAD state benefits; 406-324-3742).

Official source: MVAD state benefits · Last verified 2026-11-03

Rating guide

100% (or paid at the 100% rate): property tax rate cut if under the income limit, DV plate, no registration fees (items 1 to 3) · Purple Heart and 50%+: DV plate and fee waiver (items 2, 3) · Combat-connected injury that leaves you non-ambulatory or with substantially impaired mobility (provider-certified): half-price deer and antelope (item 4) · Any service-connected rating: 10-point hiring preference (item 5) · Veterans: retired pay break, tuition waiver (items 6 to 8). Montana state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Property Tax Rate Cut (MDV)

    • Honorably discharged veteran rated 100% (or paid at the 100% rate) for a service-connected disability who owns and lives in the home at least 7 months a year; or an unmarried surviving spouse
    • Tax rate cut by 100%, 80%, 70% or 50% by income; tax year 2027 limits: single up to $64,551, married or head of household up to $74,482 (2025 federal AGI)
    • Unmarried surviving spouse (income limit $56,276) of a veteran who was 100% or paid at 100% at death, died on active duty, or died of a service-connected disability
    • Apply to the Department of Revenue by April 15: MDV program · MDV application
    What to gather (4)
    • Form MDV (online or paper)
    • VA letter showing 100% service-connected
    • Your 2025 federal return if you did not file a 2025 Montana return (including new residents)
    • Income proof if only nontaxable income

    Official source: MDV program · Official source: MDV application · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Disabled Veteran (DV) License Plate

    • Honorably discharged veteran currently rated 100% or paid at the 100% rate for a service-connected disability
    • Or awarded the Purple Heart and rated 50% or more from a service-connected injury
    • DV plates allow disabled parking; $12 production cost, no renewal fee; cannot pass to a surviving spouse
    • Apply at your County Treasurer's office: MVD Disabled Veteran plate · Veteran plates
    What to gather (2)
    • VA letter showing your rating or payment status
    • DD214 (and Purple Heart proof if using that route)

    Official source: Veteran plates · Official source: MVD Disabled Veteran plate · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • No Registration Fees for Two Vehicles

    • Veteran currently rated 100% or paid at the 100% rate for a service-connected disability (or an unremarried surviving spouse)
    • Exempt from all motor vehicle registration fees and the veterans' cemetery fee for two special veteran plates on two non-commercial vehicles
    • Purple Heart with 50%+ also qualifies, but pays the cemetery fee
    • Show the VA letter at your County Treasurer's office: MVD reduced fees for disabled veterans · MCA 61-3-460
    What to gather (3)
    • VA letter showing 100% or paid at the 100% rate
    • DD214
    • Vehicle registration

    Official source: MCA 61-3-460 · Official source: MVD reduced fees for disabled veterans · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Half-Price Deer and Antelope (Combat Disabled)

    • Veteran (or disabled service member) whose combat-connected injury leaves them non-ambulatory, with substantially impaired mobility, or with another condition meeting MCA 87-2-803(9), certified by a Montana health care provider
    • Half-price deer A, deer B and special antelope licenses (resident deer A $8, deer B $5, antelope $7)
    • Up to 50 licenses a year; conservation and base hunting licenses still needed
    • Mail to FWP Licensing (406-444-2950): Combat Disabled Application (PDF) · FWP disabled licenses
    What to gather (4)
    • 2026 Combat Disabled Application
    • VA service-connected disability letter
    • Montana medical provider certification (first time)
    • Hunter education card if born after Jan 1, 1985

    Official source: Combat Disabled Application (PDF) · Official source: FWP disabled licenses · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • 10-Point Public Hiring Preference

    • Disabled veteran applying to a Montana public employer from outside its current staff (initial hiring)
    • 10 percentage points added in a scored hiring process (5 for other veterans); first preference where jobs are not scored
    • In layoffs, veterans rated 30% or more are kept ahead of other preferred employees with the same duties and service
    • Claim preference on each job application: MCA 39-29-102 · MCA 39-29-111
    What to gather (3)
    • DD214
    • VA letter showing your service-connected rating
    • Preference claim with your application

    Official source: MCA 39-29-102 · Official source: MCA 39-29-111 · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Half of Military Retired Pay Exempt (if Working)

    • Military retiree (or Survivor Benefit Plan beneficiary) who became a Montana resident after June 30, 2023, or lived here before the pay started
    • Exempt the lesser of 50% of military retired pay or your Montana wages plus business and farm income
    • For 5 consecutive years; lost if you move away
    • Attach Form WMRE to your Montana return: Working military retirement exemption
    What to gather (3)
    • DFAS 1099-R
    • Montana W-2 or business income records
    • Montana income tax return

    Official source: Working military retirement exemption · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Help Applying From a Veteran Service Office

    • Veterans, retired service members and their families
    • Veteran Service Offices across Montana help you find and apply for federal, state and local benefits
    • Can help you get the VA letter that items 1 to 5 need
    • Call 406-324-3742 or email mvad@mt.gov: MVAD state benefits · Army benefits summary
    What to gather (3)
    • DD214
    • Any VA letters you already have
    • List of the items above you want to apply for

    Official source: MVAD state benefits · Last verified 2026-11-03
