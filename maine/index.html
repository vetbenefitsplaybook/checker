Maine Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Where to get help

the Maine Bureau of Veterans' Services has offices statewide that help veterans and families find and apply for federal, state and local benefits, free of charge (Army benefits summary; BVS, 207-287-7020).

Official source: BVS · Last verified 2026-11-03

Rating guide

100% permanent and total: Disabled Veteran plate with no excise, registration or title fees, free state park camping, dependents' free tuition (items 4, 5, 8) · Total service-connected disability: excise tax exemption on up to 3 cars (item 3) · 60% to 100%: new homestead exemption of $10,000 to $50,000 from April 1, 2027 (item 1) · 50%+: free lifetime hunting and fishing license (item 6) · Any rating: military retired pay not taxed (item 7). (page 1 rating guide; each part matches the item it points to)

Last verified 2026-11-03. Official sources are on each benefit below.

Maine state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-03

  • New Homestead Exemption by Rating (from 2027)

    • Maine veteran with a VA service-connected rating of 60% or more, living in a homestead you've owned for 12 months
    • From April 1, 2027: an extra exemption of $10,000 (60%) up to $50,000 (100%) of value, on top of the $25,000 homestead exemption
    • Below 60%: $5,000 ($6,000 at 62+). Replaces the old $6,000 veterans' exemption; an unremarried surviving spouse can claim what the veteran would get
    • Apply to your local assessor by April 1, 2027; current holders reapply only for a higher amount: MRS homestead changes (PDF) · property tax forms
    What to gather (3)
    • Application from Maine Revenue Services (property tax forms)
    • VA letter showing your rating
    • DD214

    Official source: MRS homestead changes (PDF) · Official source: property tax forms · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • $50,000 Exemption for Adapted Housing

    • Veteran who received a VA grant for a specially adapted housing unit (38 U.S.C. 2101)
    • From April 1, 2027: an extra $50,000 of value exempt on your homestead
    • You get only one veterans' tier: this one or item 1, not both
    • Apply to your local assessor by April 1, 2027: 36 M.R.S. 683-A · property tax forms
    What to gather (2)
    • Proof of your VA specially adapted housing grant
    • Application from Maine Revenue Services

    Official source: 36 M.R.S. 683-A · Official source: property tax forms · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Excise Tax Exemption on Up to 3 Cars

    • Veteran disabled in the line of duty who receives VA pension or compensation for total, service-connected disability
    • Up to 3 automobiles you own are exempt from the excise tax (leased cars can qualify too)
    • Your name must be on each car's title
    • Claim it where you pay excise tax when you register: 36 M.R.S. 1483
    What to gather (2)
    • VA letter showing total service-connected disability
    • Vehicle title in your name

    Official source: 36 M.R.S. 1483 · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Disabled Veteran Plate: No Excise, Registration or Title Fees

    • Veteran rated 100% service-connected, paid at 100%, and permanent and total; or with loss of use of both legs
    • Exempt from excise tax, registration and title fees (up to 26,000 lbs.); loss of both legs also skips sales tax
    • You can show another plate, but then the registration fee applies
    • Apply at a BMV branch: BMV available veteran plates
    What to gather (2)
    • VA letter showing 100% permanent and total
    • Your vehicle title or registration papers

    Official source: BMV available veteran plates · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free State Park Camping at 100%

    • Maine resident 100% disabled veteran with a Disabled Veteran plate
    • You, your spouse and unmarried children skip reservation and camping fees at state parks (family site, up to 6 people)
    • You must be there for the whole stay
    • Ask to join the Disabled Veterans' Camping List: mail 22 State House Station, Augusta, ME 04330, email dacf@maine.gov or fax 207-287-2400: BVS free camping
    What to gather (2)
    • Written request
    • BMV letter stating you have a Disabled Veteran plate for 100% service connection

    Official source: BVS free camping · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Lifetime Hunting and Fishing License (50%+)

    • Honorably discharged veteran with a service-connected rating of 50% or more; residents of Maine or of NH, VT, RI, TX and other states with a reciprocal agreement
    • A complimentary license to fish, trap and hunt, with archery, muzzleloader, bear, turkey and other permits included
    • Good for life while you meet the residency rule; lotteries (moose, antlerless deer) still need an application
    • Apply as the BVS page directs: BVS license page · application
    What to gather (3)
    • Disabled Veteran Hunting and Fishing License application
    • VA letter showing 50%+
    • Copy of your driver's license or proof of residency

    Official source: application · Official source: BVS license page · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Military Retired Pay Not Taxed

    • Military retirees (any rating), and survivors receiving military retirement plan benefits
    • Military retirement benefits are fully exempt from Maine income tax
    • Social Security doesn't reduce it (it does reduce the separate pension deduction)
    • Take the pension income deduction on your Maine return: MRS income tax FAQ (no. 14)
    What to gather (1)
    • Your DFAS 1099-R

    Official source: MRS income tax FAQ (no. 14) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Tuition for Spouse and Children

    • Spouse and dependents of a veteran with a 100% permanent and total service-connected rating (or who died of service causes)
    • A 100% waiver of tuition and mandatory fees at the University of Maine System, community colleges and Maine Maritime Academy
    • The veteran entered service from Maine or lived here 5 years before you apply, and (if living) still lives in Maine; the student must apply for a Pell Grant
    • Apply to the Bureau of Veterans' Services: BVS dependents education page · application
    What to gather (4)
    • Dependents Educational Benefits application
    • VA letter showing 100% P&T
    • DD214
    • FAFSA (Pell Grant application)

    Official source: BVS dependents education page · Official source: application · Last verified 2026-11-03
