Kansas Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Rating guide

100% permanent or TDIU: sales tax exemption card (item 1) · 50%+: property tax refunds (items 2, 3) and a free DV plate (item 6) · 100% permanent: extra $2,320 income tax exemption (item 5) · 30%+: free hunting and fishing license (item 7) · Surviving spouses: item 4 · Military retirees: retired pay not taxed (item 8). (page 1 rating guide; each part matches the item it points to)

Last verified 2026-11-03. Official sources are on each benefit below.

Kansas state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Sales Tax Exemption Card ($24,000 a Year)

    • Kansas resident, honorably discharged, with a permanent VA rating of 100% or TDIU
    • Purchases on or after July 1, 2026: no Kansas sales tax on up to $24,000 a year of personal-use items and services
    • Not for motor vehicles, alcohol, tobacco, e-cigarettes or business use. Show the card at checkout; renew every 3 years
    • Apply online or on Form K-97: KDOR disabled veteran exemption · K.S.A. 79-3606h
    What to gather (3)
    • DD214 (Member 4 copy)
    • VA award letter or benefit summary letter showing 100% or TDIU
    • Form K-97 if applying on paper

    Official source: KDOR disabled veteran exemption · Official source: K.S.A. 79-3606h · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Homestead Property Tax Refund (K-40H)

    • Kansas resident all year with a service-connected rating of 50% or more (honorable or general discharge)
    • Household income of $43,389 or less (2025 claims) and a home appraised at $350,000 or less. VA disability pay does not count as income
    • Refund of part of the property tax you paid, based on income, up to $700
    • File after Dec. 31 and by April 15 for the prior year: KDOR Homestead FAQ · KDOR claim booklet
    What to gather (3)
    • Form K-40H
    • VA letter showing your disability date and percentage
    • Pages 1–2 of your federal 1040 and income statements

    Official source: KDOR Homestead FAQ · Official source: KDOR claim booklet · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Property Tax Freeze Refund (K-40SVR)

    • Disabled veteran rated 50% or more for the entire base year, Kansas resident all year
    • Household income of $58,041 or less (2025 claims, Kansas AGI); home appraised at $350,000 or less in the base year
    • Refund = this year's homestead property tax minus your base-year tax. Only one refund per household (K-40H, K-40PT or K-40SVR)
    • File by April 15 for the prior year: KDOR SVR FAQ · KDOR's free WebFile picks the larger refund for you
    What to gather (3)
    • Form K-40SVR
    • VA letter showing your disability date and percentage
    • Pages 1–2 of your federal 1040

    Official source: KDOR SVR FAQ · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Surviving Spouse Benefits

    • Surviving spouse of a disabled veteran (50%+): can claim the K-40H or K-40SVR refund (items 2, 3) until remarriage
    • Surviving spouse of a service member who died in the line of duty on active service: K-40H until remarriage
    • Sales tax card (item 1): if the veteran was receiving the exemption when they died, until remarriage. Refunds use the same limits as items 2 and 3
    • File refunds by April 15 for the prior year: KDOR Homestead FAQ · KDOR SVR FAQ · sales tax card
    What to gather (2)
    • The claim form (K-40H or K-40SVR), or KDOR's sales tax card application
    • Copy of the veteran's original VA disability letter showing date and percentage

    Official source: KDOR SVR FAQ · Official source: KDOR Homestead FAQ · Official source: sales tax card · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Extra $2,320 Income Tax Exemption at 100%

    • Honorably discharged veteran receiving VA compensation at the 100% rate
    • The disability must be permanent and service-connected
    • Adds a $2,320 Kansas exemption (tax year 2025 and later)
    • Count the disabled veteran in the exemptions box on your K-40: KDOR income tax booklet
    What to gather (2)
    • VA letter showing 100% compensation and permanent status
    • Kansas K-40 return

    Official source: KDOR income tax booklet · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Disabled Veteran Plate

    • Kansas resident with a service-connected rating of 50% or more (honorable or general discharge)
    • Car, motorcycle or truck up to 20,000 lbs. in your name. No registration fee for the first DV plate; vehicle property tax still applies
    • Plate with the wheelchair symbol needs a medical signature and allows disability parking
    • Apply at your county treasurer's motor vehicle office: KDOR DV plate · K.S.A. 8-161
    What to gather (2)
    • Form TR-103 signed by you and the VA regional office
    • Proof of insurance

    Official source: K.S.A. 8-161 · Official source: KDOR DV plate · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Permanent Hunting and Fishing License

    • Kansas resident with an honorable or general discharge
    • Service-connected rating of 30% or more
    • License to hunt and fish issued free of charge. All hunting and fishing rules still apply
    • Apply to the Kansas Department of Wildlife and Parks: K.S.A. 32-934
    What to gather (2)
    • VA letter showing your rating (30%+)
    • Proof of Kansas residency

    Official source: K.S.A. 32-934 · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Military Retired Pay Not Taxed

    • Retirees receiving retirement pay for service in the U.S. Armed Forces
    • Listed by KDOR as retirement benefits exempt from Kansas income tax
    • Federal civil service retirement is on the same list
    • Subtract it on Schedule S, line A14: KDOR income tax booklet
    What to gather (2)
    • Your DFAS 1099-R
    • Kansas K-40 and Schedule S

    Official source: KDOR income tax booklet · Last verified 2026-11-03
