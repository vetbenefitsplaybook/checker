Hawaii Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Where to get help

The Hawaii Office of Veterans' Services helps veterans, retirees and families find and apply for federal, state and local benefits, free of charge (Army benefits summary; OVS, 808-433-0420).

Official source: OVS · Last verified 2026-11-03

Rating guide

Hawaii has no state property tax: each county sets its own disabled veteran rule (items 1 to 5). Totally disabled: home exempt except the minimum tax in Honolulu (item 1) and except half the minimum tax in Hawaii County (item 2) · 70%+: $150 a year on Maui (item 3) · 80%+: home exempt except the $150 minimum on Kauai (item 4), plus a $50,000 exemption for disabled veterans (item 5) · 100%: $46 state registration fee waived (item 7) · Any rating: military retired pay not taxed (item 6). (page 1 rating guide; each part matches the item it points to)

Last verified 2026-11-03. Official sources are on each benefit below.

Hawaii state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Honolulu: Home Exempt Except Minimum Tax

    • Veteran totally disabled from injuries sustained on active duty, who owns and lives in the home (alone or with a spouse) on O'ahu
    • The home is exempt from real property tax except the minimum tax
    • It lasts as long as you stay totally disabled; an unremarried widow(er) can keep it
    • File by June 30 (first payment) or December 31 (second payment), in person or by mail with the Real Property Assessment Division, 808-768-3799: Honolulu RPAD page · Form E-8-10.5 (PDF)
    What to gather (4)
    • Form E-8-10.5
    • Physician's certificate of disability (on the City's form)
    • VA certification (listed as an attachment on the form)
    • Official ID (plus marriage/death certificate if you're a spouse or surviving spouse)

    Official source: Form E-8-10.5 (PDF) · Official source: Honolulu RPAD page · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Hawaii County: Home Exempt Except Half the Minimum Tax

    • Real property owned and occupied as a home by a person who is 100 percent disabled or 100 percent unemployable, or both, because of injuries received while on duty with the U.S. armed forces, including a home owned with a spouse and occupied by either spouse
    • The home is exempt from property taxes, other than special assessments, except for 50 percent of the minimum tax
    • A portion used for commercial purposes is not exempt
    • The exemption lasts while the veteran remains 100 percent disabled or 100 percent unemployable, or both. An unmarried widow or widower who keeps owning and living in the home can keep it
    • Proof of disability or unemployable status from the Veterans Administration may be used

    Official source: Hawaiʻi County Code § 19-73 · Last verified 2026-11-09

  • Maui County: $150 a Year at 70%+

    • Veteran with a VA rating of 70% or higher from a service-connected injury on duty, who owns and lives in the home on Maui, Moloka'i or Lāna'i
    • The home is levied a real property tax of $150 per year; one home only, and no business portion
    • The unmarried surviving spouse of a veteran who died on duty also pays $150 a year
    • File by December 31 for the next assessment year; email RPA@co.maui.hi.us or call 808-270-7297: Form DFT-475 (PDF) · Ordinance 5410 (PDF)
    What to gather (3)
    • Form DFT-475
    • Proof of age (driver's license, state ID or birth certificate)
    • The county verifies your rating with VA

    Official source: Form DFT-475 (PDF) · Official source: Ordinance 5410 (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Kauai: Home Exempt at 80%+

    • Veteran rated at least 80% disabled from injuries received on active duty, with a home on Kaua'i
    • The home is exempt from all property taxes except the $150 minimum tax
    • It lasts while you stay disabled; an unremarried widow(er) who lives in the home can keep it
    • File by September 30 with Kaua'i Real Property Assessment: Kauai exemption information
    What to gather (3)
    • RP Form P-6 (disability claim)
    • Claim for Home Exemption (Form P-3)
    • VA rating letter

    Official source: Kauai exemption information · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Kauai: $50,000 Exemption Below 80%

    • Disabled veteran, or an owner who is blind, deaf or totally disabled, who owns property on Kaua'i
    • A $50,000 real property tax exemption, on top of the regular home exemption
    • Your condition must be certified by a Hawaii-licensed physician or a commissioned military medical officer
    • File with Kaua'i Real Property Assessment: Kauai exemption information
    What to gather (2)
    • RP Form P-6
    • Physician's written certification

    Official source: Kauai exemption information · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Military Retired Pay Not Taxed

    • Military retirees (any rating) with pension or annuity pay from a public retirement system
    • Hawaii doesn't tax qualifying distributions from a government plan, including a military pension
    • Not if you made voluntary employee contributions under an elective right
    • Exclude it when you file your Hawaii return: Form N-11 instructions (PDF)
    What to gather (2)
    • Your DFAS 1099-R
    • Form N-11, line 13 (pensions)

    Official source: Form N-11 instructions (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • $46 State Registration Fee Waived at 100%

    • Hawaii resident, discharged other than dishonorably, with a 100% service-connected VA rating
    • Waives the $46 state registration fee on one non-commercial vehicle; you still pay the rest of the bill
    • Ask OVS for a new exemption letter every year; the DMV takes only the original letter
    • Send to OVS by fax (808-433-0385) or email (ovs@hawaii.gov), then take the letter to your county DMV: OVS benefits and services
    What to gather (4)
    • Copy of your driver's license
    • Copy of your current registration (one vehicle)
    • Current VA 100% disability letter
    • Current registration and safety check

    Official source: OVS benefits and services · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Vehicle Tax Exemption for a VA-Furnished Car

    • Veteran who was furnished a passenger car by the federal government because of a service disability
    • The car is exempt from the county vehicle tax, as certified by VA
    • One vehicle only; not for commercial use
    What to gather (2)
    • VA certification that the car was furnished for your service disability
    • Vehicle registration papers

    Official source: OVS benefits and services · Official source: HRS 249-6 · Last verified 2026-11-03
