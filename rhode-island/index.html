Rhode Island Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Where to get help

the Rhode Island Office of Veterans Services (RIVETS) runs a Veterans Resource Center for benefits navigation and has an online contact form for help with federal, state and local benefits (RIVETS; Army benefits summary; 401-921-2119).

Official source: RIVETS · Last verified 2026-11-03

Rating guide

Totally disabled (service-connected): local property tax exemptions (items 1, 2) · 100%, individual unemployability, or 70% at age 75+: no registration or license fee, Disabled Veteran plate (item 3) · 100%: parking placard, free fishing and combination license, free state beach and park entry (items 4 to 6) · 10% or more: free tuition at public colleges (item 7) · Wartime veteran with a VA rating: 10 hiring points (item 8). (page 1 rating guide; each part matches the item it points to)

Last verified 2026-11-03. Official sources are on each benefit below.

Rhode Island state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Property Tax Exemption, Totally Disabled

    • Rhode Island resident veteran rated totally disabled (service-connected) by VA; some town exemptions also require wartime or listed-conflict service and an honorable discharge
    • Many cities and towns grant an extra exemption on top of the regular veterans' exemption; amounts vary by town
    • Unmarried widows or widowers of veterans can qualify for veterans' exemptions
    • Apply at your city or town tax assessor: RIVETS property tax page · Exemptions by town (PDF) · RIGL 44-3-4
    What to gather (3)
    • DD214
    • VA certificate that you are totally disabled
    • Your town's exemption application

    Official source: Exemptions by town (PDF) · Official source: RIVETS property tax page · Official source: RIGL 44-3-4 · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Specially Adapted Home: Up to Full Exemption

    • Veteran rated totally disabled who got VA help to acquire specially adapted housing: a $10,000 exemption on the home you live in (some towns differ)
    • Rated permanently and totally disabled and own a home bought or modified with a VA adaptive housing grant: exempt from all taxation on it (Westerly: $46,500)
    • An unmarried widow or widower or surviving spouse can keep it
    • Give the proof to your city or town tax assessor: RIGL 44-3-4(b) and (i) · RIVETS property tax page
    What to gather (3)
    • VA letter showing total (or P&T) service-connected disability
    • Proof of the VA specially adapted housing grant
    • DD214

    Official source: RIVETS property tax page · Official source: RIGL 44-3-4(b) and (i) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • No Registration or License Fee, DV Plate

    • Honorably discharged wartime veteran rated 100% combined or individually unemployable, or 75 or older with a 70% combined rating (or lost a limb or its use in service, or got a VA vehicle grant)
    • No fee for the annual registration of and license to operate one vehicle
    • "Disabled Veteran" plates (one set); your spouse can take them over if you are certified unable to drive, and a surviving spouse can keep them until remarrying
    • Bring them to a DMV office: DMV veteran plates · RIGL 31-6-8
    What to gather (3)
    • DD214 or other proof of service
    • Executive Certification letter from the VA Regional Office (380 Westminster St., Providence) showing 100%, individual unemployability, or 70% at age 75+
    • Application for Registration (TR-1)

    Official source: DMV veteran plates · Official source: RIGL 31-6-8 · Official source: P.L. 2026, ch. 248 · Official source: P.L. 2026, ch. 251 · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Veteran Disability Parking Placard

    • Veteran with a 100% service-connected disability
    • No physician's approval needed (others need one)
    • You need an existing Rhode Island license or ID
    • Fax to (401) 462-0829 or mail to RI DMV, Disability Parking Placard Office, 600 New London Ave., Cranston, RI 02920: DMV veteran placard page
    What to gather (3)
    • Veteran Disability Parking Placard Application
    • VA War Related Disabled Declaration Letter
    • Your Rhode Island credential

    Official source: DMV veteran placard page · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Fishing or Combination License

    • Veteran rated 100% disabled by VA (a permanent-and-total rating isn't required)
    • No fee for the fishing or combination (hunt and fish) disability license; for veterans it is permanent once issued
    • Permits and stamps (turkey, game bird, waterfowl) still cost extra
    • Mail to RI DEM, 235 Promenade St., Room 360, Providence, RI 02908: Application (PDF) · Army benefits summary
    What to gather (3)
    • Application for 100% Permanent Disability Fishing/Combination License
    • Original VA final decision letter on VA letterhead (benefit explanation letters don't count)
    • For hunting: a prior hunting license, hunter safety card or DD214; a copy of photo ID

    Official source: Application (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free State Beach and Park Entry

    • Veteran determined by VA to be 100% disabled through a service-connected disability
    • No parking, admission or golf fees at state-owned recreation sites (camping and other special fees still apply)
    • Renew yearly unless you are no longer re-evaluated; a car carrying you as a non-driver isn't charged
    • Apply in person at DEM Parks Headquarters, 1100 Tower Hill Rd., North Kingstown (401-667-6200): Disability pass (PDF) · RIGL 32-1-17
    What to gather (2)
    • VA letter for the current year stating you are 100% disabled from a service-connected injury
    • Valid photo ID

    Official source: Disability pass (PDF) · Official source: RIGL 32-1-17 · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Tuition at Public Colleges

    • Permanent Rhode Island resident veteran with a VA rating of 10% to 100% from military service
    • No tuition at any public college or university in the state (fees and books not covered)
    • You must apply for financial aid; any aid is applied to tuition first
    • Ask your school's financial aid office: RIGL 30-30.1-1 · Army benefits summary
    What to gather (3)
    • Recent VA letter showing your disability rating
    • FAFSA
    • Proof of Rhode Island residency

    Official source: RIGL 30-30.1-1 · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Civil Service Hiring Preference

    • Disabled veteran: a war veteran (campaign ribbon or expeditionary medal) with an honorable or general discharge and a VA service-connected rating
    • 10 points added to a passing entrance exam score (5 for other veterans)
    • For entry to the classified service only, not promotions
    • Claim it when you apply for competitive state jobs: RIVETS hiring preference · RIGL 36-4-19
    What to gather (2)
    • DD214 showing your campaign ribbon or medal
    • VA letter showing your rating

    Official source: RIVETS hiring preference · Official source: RIGL 36-4-19 · Last verified 2026-11-03
