California Disabled Veteran Benefits by Rating (2026)

8 California benefits to look into, with the rating each one starts at. Use the checker to filter by your rating.

California state benefits

Last verified 2026-10-29

  • Disabled Veterans' Property Tax Exemption (Basic): $180,671 for 2026

    100%+
    • Rated 100% service-connected, or paid at the 100% rate for unemployability, or blind in both eyes (visual acuity 5/200 or less, or a 5° field or less), or lost use of 2 or more limbs
    • The home is your principal place of residence in California
    • Discharge other than dishonorable, and service during a period listed in Rev. & Tax. Code § 205.5
    • Unmarried surviving spouses can qualify (e.g., the veteran died of a service-connected cause)
    • 2026 amount: $180,671 off the home's full value

    Official source: BOE DV Exemption page · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • Low-Income Level of the Same Exemption: $271,009 for 2026

    100%+
    • Meet the basic exemption rules (item 1)
    • Household income at or under $81,131 (2026 limit). The BOE counts all VA and Social Security benefits as household income
    • 2026 amount: $271,009 instead of $180,671

    Official source: BOE DV Exemption page · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • Disabled Veteran (DV) License Plates: No Registration or License Fees

    Other rules
    • Permanently disabled from an injury or disease during active service, and any one of:
    • 100% rating for a disease or disorder that substantially impairs or interferes with mobility · can't move without an assistive device · lost use of 1+ limbs · permanently blind (W&I Code § 19153)
    • Fee exemption on one vehicle you own: passenger vehicle, motorcycle, or commercial vehicle of 8,000 lbs or less unladen not used for hire
    • You can't hold both DV and Ex-POW plates

    Official source: DMV DV plates page · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • DV Plate Parking Privileges

    Other rules
    • Comes with valid DV plates (item 3)
    • Park in wheelchair-symbol spaces and at blue curbs
    • Park at green curbs for as long as you wish, and at on-street meters at no charge
    • Park in resident or merchant permit areas
    • Full-service stations must refuel your vehicle at self-service rates (unless only one employee is on duty)

    Official source: DMV DV plates FAQ · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • Reduced-Fee Fishing and Hunting Licenses

    50%+
    • Honorably discharged veteran with a 50%+ service-connected disability rating
    • Fishing: resident or nonresident. Fee $10.04 at CDFW offices, $10.54 from license agents; valid 365 days from purchase
    • Hunting: resident or nonresident. You also need to meet California hunter education rules

    Official source: CDFW Fishing · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • Distinguished Veteran Pass: Free State Park Day Use, Camping and Boating

    50%+
    • Honorably discharged veteran with California residency, and one of:
    • 50%+ combined service-connected rating with service during wartime or operations against a foreign power (State Parks lists the periods) · former POW · Medal of Honor
    • Free vehicle day use, family camping and boat use at 100+ State Parks-operated units

    Official source: State Parks Distinguished Veteran Pass · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • College Fee Waiver for Veteran Dependents (CCC, CSU, UC)

    Any rating
    • Waives mandatory system-wide tuition and fees at California Community Colleges, CSU and UC (not books, parking or room and board)
    • Plan A (no income limit): an unmarried child over 14 and under 27 (under 30 if the child is a veteran) of a veteran who is totally service-connected disabled; the spouse of a wartime veteran who is totally service-connected disabled. Needs wartime service or a campaign or expeditionary medal, and the rating must come before the child turns 21. Can't be combined with VA Chapter 35
    • Plan B: a child of a veteran with any service-connected disability; the child's income (AGI plus any parental support) can't exceed $22,941 for 2026–27. No age or wartime rule
    • All applicants must meet the school's California residency rules

    Official source: CalVet College Fee Waiver · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • State Income Tax: VA Disability Pay Not Taxed + Military Retirement Exclusion

    Any rating
    • VA disability benefits and lump-sum retroactive VA payments are tax-exempt in California. CRSC is tax-exempt; CRDP is taxable
    • Tax years 2025–2029: exclude up to $20,000 of uniformed-services retirement pay, and separately up to $20,000 of Survivor Benefit Plan annuity payments
    • Federal AGI limit: $250,000 joint/surviving spouse; $125,000 others

    Official source: FTB Military page · Last verified 2026-10-29

Federal benefits to look into

  • CHAMPVA: health coverage for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    It's for family members, not the veteran: the spouse or dependent child of a veteran rated P&T from a service-connected disability. It's a cost-sharing program: VA shares the cost of covered care with the family. Not free. Family members eligible for TRICARE can't get CHAMPVA.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DEA (Chapter 35): school money for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    Chapter 35 / DEA education benefits are available to the spouse and children of a veteran who is P&T due to a service-connected disability. For school or training that started on or after Aug 1, 2018, DEA pays up to 36 months of benefits.

    Official source: VA: DEA · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Social Security: faster disability decisions

    100% + P&T

    SSA expedites disability claims from veterans rated 100% P&T. Say "veteran rated 100% P&T" (or enter "Veteran 100% P&T" in the online Remarks). It doesn't guarantee approval; SSA's own rules still apply.

    Official source: SSA: Veterans · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Dental insurance for CHAMPVA families (VADIP)

    P&T

    CHAMPVA covers dental only in limited medical cases; enrollees can buy dental insurance at reduced cost through VADIP.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Your Benefit Summary Letter (proof of your rating)

    All veterans

    You can download your Benefit Summary Letter (sometimes called an award letter) on VA.gov.

    Official source: VA: Download VA letters · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • VA dental care (Class IV)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Per VA, you qualify for any needed dental care if you have a service-connected disability rated 100% disabling, or you're paid at the 100% rate because of TDIU. A temporary 100% rating does not qualify.

    Official source: VA: Dental care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DoD ID card and base privileges (100%)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    A 100% disabled veteran (DAV) and eligible family members can get a DoD Uniformed Services ID card. It gives commissary, exchange and MWR privileges and base access. It is not TRICARE.

    Official source: eCFR: 32 CFR part 161 · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Base retail access for all service-connected veterans

    Any rating

    All service-connected veterans can use commissaries, exchanges and MWR retail facilities, in person and online, per VA's matrix.

    Official source: VA: Derivative benefits matrix · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Federal student loan discharge (TPD)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Veterans whom VA has determined have a service-connected disability rated 100% or are rated totally disabled based on individual unemployability (TDIU) can have eligible federal student loans discharged.

    Official source: Federal Student Aid: TPD discharge · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • No VA home loan funding fee

    10%+

    Veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability are exempt from the VA home loan funding fee, at any compensable rating, not just 100%.

    Official source: VA: Funding fee · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • America the Beautiful Access Pass

    Other rules

    A free, lifetime pass for U.S. citizens or permanent residents with a permanent disability. It doesn't have to be 100% or service-connected.

    Official source: NPS: Access Pass · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Military Lifetime Pass (federal lands)

    All veterans

    Veterans (and Gold Star families) can get a free lifetime pass to federal recreation lands. No disability is needed.

    Official source: NPS: Veterans free access · Last verified 2026-11-01
