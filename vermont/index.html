Vermont Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Where to get help

The Vermont Office of Veterans Affairs runs state benefits and helps veterans file federal VA claims; call 802-828-3379 or 888-666-9844 in Vermont (OVA; Army benefits summary).

Official source: OVA · Last verified 2026-11-03

Rating guide

60% or more (now or ever): free hunting, fishing or combination license with big game tags (item 4) · 50% or more (VA compensation): $10,000 to $40,000 property tax exemption (item 1) · VA-funded vehicle (approved VA Form 21-4502): no purchase-and-use tax or DMV fees (item 2) · Any service-connected disability: DV plate, hiring preference (items 3, 5) · Any Vermont resident veteran: $2 park passport (item 6) · Discharged other than dishonorably: Veterans' Home (item 7). (page 1 rating guide; each part matches the item it points to)

Last verified 2026-11-03. Official sources are on each benefit below.

Vermont state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-03

  • $10,000+ Veterans Property Tax Exemption

    • Veteran receiving VA disability compensation at 50% or higher (also Improved Pension or a permanent military medical retirement)
    • Takes $10,000 off the appraised value of your owned primary home; your town can vote to raise it up to $40,000
    • Unremarried surviving spouses of a veteran who had the exemption can qualify, and so can a child who owns the home and gets VA death compensation or DIC; renters and second homes do not
    • Mail, email or fax to the Office of Veterans Affairs before May 1 each year (once only if permanently disabled): Application (PDF) · OVA tax exemptions
    What to gather (3)
    • Property Tax Exemption application
    • Current VA Summary of Benefits letter
    • Your SPAN number from your tax bill

    Official source: OVA tax exemptions · Official source: Application (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • No Tax or DMV Fees on a VA-Funded Vehicle

    • Vermont resident veteran who got the vehicle with VA financial assistance (approved VA Form 21-4502), or a replacement for that vehicle
    • No purchase-and-use tax, registration fees or title fee; the driver's license fee is also waived if you were honorably discharged
    • Other veterans are not exempt, even at 100%
    • Ask the DMV (802-828-2000) when you title and register: 23 V.S.A. 378 · 32 V.S.A. 8911
    What to gather (2)
    • A copy of your approved VA Form 21-4502
    • Vehicle title and registration paperwork

    Official source: 23 V.S.A. 378 · Official source: 32 V.S.A. 8911 · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Disabled Veteran License Plate

    • Vermont veteran disabled during active service
    • For cars, motorcycles and trucks registered under 26,001 lbs
    • A licensed physician, PA or APRN must sign a medical report (unless one was filed in the last 4 years)
    • Apply to the DMV: DMV military plates · VG-168 (PDF) · VS-113 (PDF)
    What to gather (2)
    • Vermont Certificate of Veteran Status (VG-168)
    • Universal Medical Evaluation/Progress Report (VS-113)

    Official source: DMV military plates · Official source: VG-168 (PDF) · Official source: VS-113 (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Hunting, Fishing or Combination License

    • Vermont resident veteran who is, or ever has been, 60% disabled from a service-connected disability
    • Free fishing, hunting or combination license that includes all big game licenses except moose
    • Hunting and combination licenses still require meeting Vermont hunting license requirements
    • Mail or email Fish and Wildlife, 1 National Life Drive, Dewey Building, Montpelier (ANR.FWlicensing@vermont.gov; 802-828-1190): 10 V.S.A. § 4255 · Army benefits summary
    What to gather (2)
    • Fish and Wildlife license application
    • VA certificate showing the 60% rating

    Official source: 10 V.S.A. § 4255 · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • State Job Preference

    • Veteran separated under honorable conditions with a service-connected disability; extra preference points over other veterans
    • Lets you apply for jobs normally open only to current state employees ("State promotional only")
    • The spouse of a service-connected disabled veteran who can't qualify for a state job because of the disability can also claim preference
    • Claim it on each state job application at humanresources.vermont.gov/careers: DHR veterans preference · 20 V.S.A. § 1543
    What to gather (2)
    • DD214
    • VA letter showing your service-connected disability

    Official source: DHR veterans preference · Official source: 20 V.S.A. § 1543 · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • $2 Green Mountain Passport

    • Vermont resident veteran (no rating needed)
    • Free day entry to state parks, historic sites and fully state-sponsored events for life
    • One-time $2 fee
    • Only available at your local town clerk's office: Town clerk directory · 31 V.S.A. 1002
    What to gather (3)
    • DD214, in case the clerk asks
    • Proof of Vermont residency, in case the clerk asks
    • $2; you swear to your eligibility on the town clerk's application

    Official source: 31 V.S.A. 1002 · Official source: Town clerk directory · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Vermont Veterans' Home

    • Veteran discharged under conditions other than dishonorable, with 90+ days of service or less if discharged for a line-of-duty disability
    • Skilled nursing, rehab, dementia care and respite care in Bennington
    • Spouses and Gold Star parents can also apply; Vermont residents get priority
    • Send to 325 North Street, Bennington, VT 05201 (admissions 802-447-6539): VVH admissions · Army benefits summary
    What to gather (3)
    • Application for admission
    • DD214 or VA HINQ
    • Current medical information and VA Form 10-10EZ

    Official source: VVH admissions · Official source: VVH eligibility · Official source: Vermont Veterans' Home bylaws (Art. X) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Help From the Office of Veterans Affairs

    • Vermont veterans, survivors and families
    • OVA runs state benefits like item 1 and helps veterans file federal VA claims
    • Independent from the federal VA system
    • 118 State Street, Montpelier; 802-828-3379: Vermont OVA · Army benefits summary
    What to gather (3)
    • DD214
    • Any VA letters you already have
    • List of the items above you want

    Official source: Vermont OVA · Last verified 2026-11-03
