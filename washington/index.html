Washington Disabled Veteran Benefits by Rating (2026)

8 Washington benefits to look into, with the rating each one starts at. Use the checker to filter by your rating.

Washington state benefits

Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Property Tax Exemption for Veterans with Disabilities (40%+)

    40%+
    • Veteran receiving VA compensation at a combined rating of 40% or more, or a total disability rating (such as IU) at any percentage
    • Your combined disposable income must be under your county's threshold. VA disability compensation and DIC don't count as income; military retired pay does
    • You own and live in the home as your principal residence
    • Lower income means a bigger exemption; at every level, the taxable value of your home is frozen

    Official source: Wash. DOR exemption page · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Free Disabled American Veteran Plates (One Vehicle)

    100%+
    • Veteran paid VA service-connected compensation at the 100% rate, expected to last more than one year
    • Or lost the use of both hands or one foot, became blind in both eyes from service, or is a former POW with the POW medal
    • No vehicle license fees, plate fees or excise tax on one personal-use vehicle. You can get the same exemption on regular or other qualifying special plates instead
    • No renewal needed if you take the next plate number in the series (a special background must renew yearly)

    Official source: DOL disabled veteran plates · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Lifetime Disabled Veteran Pass: Free Camping (30%+)

    30%+
    • Washington resident veteran with a combined service-connected rating of 30% or more
    • Free day-use parking (no Discover Pass), boat launch, trailer dump, and free camping or moorage at state parks
    • Free day-use parking on WDFW and DNR lands too. Since Oct. 1, 2025, camping reservations and changes cost $8 online or $10 by phone

    Official source: Lifetime Disabled Veteran Pass · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Reduced-Fee Hunting & Fishing Licenses (30%+)

    30%+
    • Veteran with a qualifying discharge (honorable, general under honorable conditions and certain others defined in RCW 73.04.005) and a VA service-connected rating of 30% or more. Residents get the reduced rate; non-residents pay the regular resident price
    • Or age 65+ with any service-connected rating
    • Reduced fees on combination fishing and hunting licenses (see the WDFW disability fee schedule)
    • A rating from Social Security or another agency doesn't count

    Official source: WDFW veterans reduced fees · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Tuition Waivers for Your Family (100%)

    100%+
    • The child (Washington resident, age 17 to 26), spouse or surviving spouse of a Washington veteran rated 100% disabled by VA from active federal service (or POW/MIA), who served in a war or conflict on foreign soil or in international waters, or in support of it, and has a qualifying discharge
    • State colleges and universities must waive all tuition and fees, up to 250 quarter credits (or the semester equivalent), full- or part-time
    • A $500-a-year textbook stipend if the Legislature funds it
    • You, the veteran, may also get a full or partial waiver: each college runs its own veteran waiver program

    Official source: WDVA education benefits · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Sales Tax Refund on Adapted Housing

    Other rules
    • Veteran who received a VA adapted housing grant
    • Refund of up to $2,500 of state sales tax paid on labor and materials for the project
    • The statewide limit for all refunds is $125,000 per fiscal year (July to June)

    Official source: Wash. DOR adaptive housing refund · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Regional Reduced Fare Permit (40%+)

    40%+
    • Veteran with a VA rating of 40% or more
    • Reduced fares on participating transit, including Washington State Ferries, Sound Transit, Metro Transit and other regional systems; each agency sets its own fares

    Official source: WDVA benefits by rating · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • No State Income Tax on Retired Pay

    All veterans
    • Washington does not currently have an individual income tax, so military retired pay and wages aren't taxed by the state
    • A new 9.9% tax on income over $1 million (ESSB 6346) is set to start Jan. 1, 2028. Initiative IP26-645, on the Nov. 3, 2026 ballot, would repeal it

    Official source: Wash. DOR income tax page · Last verified 2026-10-30

Federal benefits to look into

  • CHAMPVA: health coverage for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    It's for family members, not the veteran: the spouse or dependent child of a veteran rated P&T from a service-connected disability. It's a cost-sharing program: VA shares the cost of covered care with the family. Not free. Family members eligible for TRICARE can't get CHAMPVA.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DEA (Chapter 35): school money for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    Chapter 35 / DEA education benefits are available to the spouse and children of a veteran who is P&T due to a service-connected disability. For school or training that started on or after Aug 1, 2018, DEA pays up to 36 months of benefits.

    Official source: VA: DEA · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Social Security: faster disability decisions

    100% + P&T

    SSA expedites disability claims from veterans rated 100% P&T. Say "veteran rated 100% P&T" (or enter "Veteran 100% P&T" in the online Remarks). It doesn't guarantee approval; SSA's own rules still apply.

    Official source: SSA: Veterans · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Dental insurance for CHAMPVA families (VADIP)

    P&T

    CHAMPVA covers dental only in limited medical cases; enrollees can buy dental insurance at reduced cost through VADIP.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Your Benefit Summary Letter (proof of your rating)

    All veterans

    You can download your Benefit Summary Letter (sometimes called an award letter) on VA.gov.

    Official source: VA: Download VA letters · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • VA dental care (Class IV)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Per VA, you qualify for any needed dental care if you have a service-connected disability rated 100% disabling, or you're paid at the 100% rate because of TDIU. A temporary 100% rating does not qualify.

    Official source: VA: Dental care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DoD ID card and base privileges (100%)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    A 100% disabled veteran (DAV) and eligible family members can get a DoD Uniformed Services ID card. It gives commissary, exchange and MWR privileges and base access. It is not TRICARE.

    Official source: eCFR: 32 CFR part 161 · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Base retail access for all service-connected veterans

    Any rating

    All service-connected veterans can use commissaries, exchanges and MWR retail facilities, in person and online, per VA's matrix.

    Official source: VA: Derivative benefits matrix · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Federal student loan discharge (TPD)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Veterans whom VA has determined have a service-connected disability rated 100% or are rated totally disabled based on individual unemployability (TDIU) can have eligible federal student loans discharged.

    Official source: Federal Student Aid: TPD discharge · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • No VA home loan funding fee

    10%+

    Veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability are exempt from the VA home loan funding fee, at any compensable rating, not just 100%.

    Official source: VA: Funding fee · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • America the Beautiful Access Pass

    Other rules

    A free, lifetime pass for U.S. citizens or permanent residents with a permanent disability. It doesn't have to be 100% or service-connected.

    Official source: NPS: Access Pass · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Military Lifetime Pass (federal lands)

    All veterans

    Veterans (and Gold Star families) can get a free lifetime pass to federal recreation lands. No disability is needed.

    Official source: NPS: Veterans free access · Last verified 2026-11-01
