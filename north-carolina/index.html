North Carolina Disabled Veteran Benefits by Rating (2026)

7 North Carolina benefits to look into, with the rating each one starts at. Use the checker to filter by your rating.

North Carolina state benefits

Last verified 2026-10-29

  • $45,000 Disabled Veteran Homestead Exclusion

    P&T
    • Honorable or under-honorable-conditions discharge, and either a VA (or federal) certification of a service-connected permanent and total disability, or VA specially adapted housing benefits (38 U.S.C. § 2101), as of January 1
    • North Carolina resident who owns and lives in the home as a permanent residence
    • The first $45,000 of appraised value is excluded from tax. No age or income limit
    • Surviving spouse who has never remarried can qualify, including when the veteran died of a service-connected condition

    Official source: Form AV-9 · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • Free 100% Disabled Veteran (DV) License Plate

    100%+
    • VA combined rating of 100%, or Individual Unemployability (IU) paid at the 100% rate
    • Free: one plate each year for a motor vehicle and one for a motorcycle, with the plate fee waived. Vehicles registered over 6,000 lbs pay regular registration fees

    Official source: NC DMVA plates page · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • Partially Disabled Veteran Plate (Under 100%)

    Under 100%
    • Veteran with a service-connected disability of less than 100%
    • No special-plate fee; the regular registration fee still applies

    Official source: NC DMVA plates page · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • Vehicle Property Tax Exemption (VA-Adapted Vehicle)

    Other rules
    • A motor vehicle you own that is altered with special equipment for a service-connected disability
    • You must be entitled to VA special automotive equipment (38 U.S.C. § 3901)
    • Covers the adapted vehicle only, not every vehicle a disabled veteran owns

    Official source: Form AV-10V · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • Lifetime Hunting & Fishing License for Disabled Veterans (50%+)

    50%+
    • North Carolina resident rated 50% or more disabled by VA
    • Good for life as long as you stay rated 50%+. If you move away, you can still use it whenever you hunt or fish in North Carolina (it doesn't cover other states)
    • Fees: $15 each for inland fishing, coastal fishing, or the hunting + inland fishing combo · $131 Sportsman · $144 Sportsman + Coastal, plus a $5 transaction fee. Bear e-stamp (annual fee for lifetime licenses bought on or after July 1, 2014) and federal duck stamp cost extra; HIP certification is free but required for migratory birds

    Official source: application · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • Military Retirement Pay Deduction (Medically Retired or 20+ Years)

    Other rules
    • Retired members of the uniformed services who served at least 20 years, or were medically retired under 10 U.S.C. Chapter 61
    • Deduct that retirement pay from NC taxable income (tax years 2022 and later)
    • Also covers Survivor Benefit Plan payments to an eligible retiree's beneficiary. Not for Chapter 61 severance pay

    Official source: NCDOR military retirement · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • Scholarship for Children of Wartime Veterans

    20%+
    • Child of a veteran with wartime service (honorable conditions): Class I-B if the parent is (or was at death) rated 100% wartime service-connected; Class II (competitive, limited awards each year) for 20%–99% wartime ratings or a Purple Heart. Class I-A covers deaths from wartime service
    • Child must be under 25 when applying, an NC domiciliary and resident, and finish high school (or the equivalent) before the scholarship starts
    • Residency link: the parent was an NC legal resident when entering the qualifying service, or the child was born in NC and has lived here continuously
    • At a state school, Class I-B pays tuition and required fees; full scholarships add room and board allowances. Private schools get a set allowance. Up to 4 academic years, used within 8 years; keep a 2.0 GPA

    Official source: eligibility · Last verified 2026-10-29

Federal benefits to look into

  • CHAMPVA: health coverage for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    It's for family members, not the veteran: the spouse or dependent child of a veteran rated P&T from a service-connected disability. It's a cost-sharing program: VA shares the cost of covered care with the family. Not free. Family members eligible for TRICARE can't get CHAMPVA.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DEA (Chapter 35): school money for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    Chapter 35 / DEA education benefits are available to the spouse and children of a veteran who is P&T due to a service-connected disability. For school or training that started on or after Aug 1, 2018, DEA pays up to 36 months of benefits.

    Official source: VA: DEA · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Social Security: faster disability decisions

    100% + P&T

    SSA expedites disability claims from veterans rated 100% P&T. Say "veteran rated 100% P&T" (or enter "Veteran 100% P&T" in the online Remarks). It doesn't guarantee approval; SSA's own rules still apply.

    Official source: SSA: Veterans · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Dental insurance for CHAMPVA families (VADIP)

    P&T

    CHAMPVA covers dental only in limited medical cases; enrollees can buy dental insurance at reduced cost through VADIP.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Your Benefit Summary Letter (proof of your rating)

    All veterans

    You can download your Benefit Summary Letter (sometimes called an award letter) on VA.gov.

    Official source: VA: Download VA letters · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • VA dental care (Class IV)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Per VA, you qualify for any needed dental care if you have a service-connected disability rated 100% disabling, or you're paid at the 100% rate because of TDIU. A temporary 100% rating does not qualify.

    Official source: VA: Dental care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DoD ID card and base privileges (100%)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    A 100% disabled veteran (DAV) and eligible family members can get a DoD Uniformed Services ID card. It gives commissary, exchange and MWR privileges and base access. It is not TRICARE.

    Official source: eCFR: 32 CFR part 161 · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Base retail access for all service-connected veterans

    Any rating

    All service-connected veterans can use commissaries, exchanges and MWR retail facilities, in person and online, per VA's matrix.

    Official source: VA: Derivative benefits matrix · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Federal student loan discharge (TPD)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Veterans whom VA has determined have a service-connected disability rated 100% or are rated totally disabled based on individual unemployability (TDIU) can have eligible federal student loans discharged.

    Official source: Federal Student Aid: TPD discharge · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • No VA home loan funding fee

    10%+

    Veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability are exempt from the VA home loan funding fee, at any compensable rating, not just 100%.

    Official source: VA: Funding fee · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • America the Beautiful Access Pass

    Other rules

    A free, lifetime pass for U.S. citizens or permanent residents with a permanent disability. It doesn't have to be 100% or service-connected.

    Official source: NPS: Access Pass · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Military Lifetime Pass (federal lands)

    All veterans

    Veterans (and Gold Star families) can get a free lifetime pass to federal recreation lands. No disability is needed.

    Official source: NPS: Veterans free access · Last verified 2026-11-01
