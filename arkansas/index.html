Arkansas Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Where to get help

The Arkansas Department of Veterans Affairs (ADVA) works with County Veteran Service Officers who help veterans and families file claims and access benefits; ADVA says its Veteran Service Officers help you apply for state benefits at no cost (find your county VSO; 501-683-2382).

Official source: find your county VSO · Last verified 2026-11-03

Rating guide

100% permanent and total (P&T), or special monthly compensation for loss of a limb or blindness: no state property tax on your homestead and personal property (item 1) · 100% P&T: $1.50 lifetime licenses and half-price camping (items 4, 6) · 70%+ (or 50%+ with a Purple Heart): $52.50 lifetime combination license (item 5) · VA auto grant: no sales tax on the vehicle (item 7) · Surviving spouses and minor children: item 2 · Military retirees: retired pay not taxed (item 3). (page 1 rating guide; each part matches the item it points to)

Arkansas state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Homestead and Personal Property Tax Exemption

    • Exempt from all state taxes on your homestead and personal property
    • You file the VA letter with the county collector once; you don't need to resubmit it every year, but you must tell the collector if your eligibility or the property changes, and file a new letter if you move to a new homestead (Act 876 of 2025)
    • Apply at your county collector's office: ADVA property tax guide (PDF) · ADVA state benefits
    What to gather (2)
    • VA benefit letter showing 100% P&T or the special monthly compensation award
    • Proof the home is your homestead

    Official source: Act 876 of 2025 · Official source: ADVA property tax guide (PDF) · Official source: ADVA state benefits · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Surviving Spouse and Minor Children Exemption

    • Same exemption from state taxes on the homestead and personal property
    • Apply at your county collector's office: ADVA property tax guide (PDF)
    What to gather (3)
    • Veteran's VA award letter
    • Death certificate (or casualty/MIA report)
    • Marriage certificate; children's birth certificates

    Official source: ADVA property tax guide (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Military Retired Pay Not Taxed

    • Retired members of the uniformed services, including Guard and reserve retirees
    • Military retired pay is exempt from Arkansas income tax (since tax year 2018)
    • Survivor benefits funded by military retired pay are exempt too. If you claim this, the separate $6,000 retirement exemption is limited
    • Claim the exemption on your state return: DFA AR1000F instructions (PDF) · Act 141 of 2017
    What to gather (2)
    • Your 1099-R for military retired pay
    • Your Arkansas income tax return (AR1000F or AR1000NR)

    Official source: Act 141 of 2017 · Official source: DFA AR1000F instructions (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • $1.50 Lifetime Hunting or Fishing License at 100% P&T

    • Arkansas resident veteran with a 100% total and permanent service-connected disability rating from VA
    • Lifetime fishing license $1.50, lifetime hunting license $1.50, or lifetime combination hunting and fishing license $3
    • A lifetime trout permit ($10) and a lifetime state waterfowl permit ($7) are separate from those three licenses
    • Apply by mail to the Arkansas Game and Fish Commission, 2 Natural Resources Drive, Little Rock, AR 72205 (833-345-0325)

    Official source: ADVA state benefits · Official source: AGFC Code fee table (15 CAR) · Last verified 2026-11-09

  • $52.50 Lifetime Combination License at 70%+

    • Arkansas resident veteran with a service-connected rating of 70% or higher, or 50% or higher with a Purple Heart
    • One lifetime combination license and permit, including hunting, fishing, trout, and state waterfowl, for $52.50
    • Apply by mail to the Arkansas Game and Fish Commission, 2 Natural Resources Drive, Little Rock, AR 72205 (833-345-0325)

    Official source: ADVA state benefits · Official source: AGFC Code fee table (15 CAR) · Last verified 2026-11-09

  • Half-Price State Park Camping at 100% P&T

    • Veteran rated 100% permanent and total
    • Discounted campground rates at Arkansas state parks; ADVA describes it as half-price camping year-round
    • Show your proof when you book or check in at the park: 22 CAR 50-103 · ADVA state benefits
    What to gather (1)
    • VA award letter dated within the last 2 years, or an Arkansas plate starting with "DV" or "DAV", or an America the Beautiful Access Pass

    Official source: 22 CAR 50-103 · Official source: ADVA state benefits · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • No Sales Tax on a VA-Assisted Vehicle

    • Disabled veteran who buys a vehicle or adaptive equipment with the help of a VA automobile grant (38 U.S.C. 3902)
    • The sale is exempt from Arkansas sales tax
    • A veteran blinded by a service-connected injury may buy one new automobile every two years tax-free
    • Show the VA letter when you register the vehicle at a state revenue office: 26 CAR 30-1109
    What to gather (2)
    • Official VA letter showing the VA paid toward the vehicle or equipment (or certifying service-connected blindness)
    • Bill of sale

    Official source: 26 CAR 30-1109 · Last verified 2026-11-03
