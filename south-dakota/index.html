South Dakota Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Where to get help

County and Tribal Veterans Service Officers help veterans and families find and apply for federal, state and local benefits, free of charge (SDDVA contacts).

Official source: SDDVA contacts · Last verified 2026-11-03

Rating guide

Permanent and total: $200,000 property tax exemption (item 1) · Paraplegic veteran: no property tax on a wheelchair-designed home (item 2) · Total service-connected disability allotment, a K award or a loss-of-use award, from a wartime or armed-conflict injury: DV plate with no registration fee (item 3) · Residents at 40% or more: $10 four-year hunt and fish license (item 4) · Residents at 100% total (or a K award): free lifetime park entry (item 5) · Wartime veterans (10%+ if you count as a veteran only by a service-connected disability): free tuition (item 6) · Any service-connected rating: hiring preference (item 7). (page 1 rating guide; each part matches the item it points to)

South Dakota state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-03

  • $200,000 Property Tax Exemption (P&T)

    • Veteran rated permanently and totally disabled from a service-connected disability who owns and lives in the home
    • Exempts $200,000 of the full and true value of the home, garage and lot (owner-occupied)
    • An unremarried surviving spouse can qualify; once approved, no reapplying until the home is sold or its use changes
    • File with your county director of equalization by November 1: DOR brochure (PDF) · Relief programs
    What to gather (2)
    • County application form
    • VA statement that you are permanently and totally disabled from service (call 1-800-827-1000)

    Official source: Relief programs · Official source: DOR brochure (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • No Property Tax for Paraplegic Veterans

    • Paraplegic veteran (or loss or loss of use of both lower extremities) who owns and lives in a home specially designed for wheelchair use for the full calendar year
    • Exempts the home, garage and lot (up to one acre) from all property taxes; no income limit
    • The injury does not have to be service-connected; an unremarried surviving spouse can qualify
    • Once approved, you don't reapply
    • File with your county director of equalization by November 1: DOR paraplegic brochure (PDF) · Relief programs
    What to gather (3)
    • County application form
    • Proof of paraplegia and veteran status
    • DD214

    Official source: Relief programs · Official source: DOR paraplegic brochure (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Disabled Veteran Plate, No Registration Fee

    • Resident veteran with a VA K award, a loss-of-use award, or a total service-connected disability allotment
    • Injury incurred on active duty in wartime or in a mission involving armed conflict
    • No registration fee; $10 a year for the plate plus a $12 mailing fee
    • Apply to the Motor Vehicle Division; status is checked with the Sioux Falls VA: Specialty military plates
    What to gather (2)
    • Application for SD Military License Plates (MV-4000)
    • VA award letter

    Official source: Specialty military plates · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • $10 Hunt and Fish License for 4 Years

    • South Dakota resident receiving VA pay for a 40% or more service-connected disability, or holding a K award
    • Also former POWs, and service members 40%+ disabled for Social Security
    • One $10 license replaces the resident small game and fishing licenses for 4 license years
    • Mail to GFP Disability License, 20641 SD Hwy 1806, Fort Pierre, SD 57532: Application (PDF) · SDCL 41-6-10.2
    What to gather (3)
    • Reduced Fee Hunting and Fishing License application
    • Current VA benefits letter (send it even when renewing)
    • $10 fee

    Official source: Application (PDF) · Official source: SDCL 41-6-10.2 · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Lifetime State Park Entry

    • South Dakota resident with a VA 100% total service-connected rating or a K award (also POWs and Purple Heart recipients)
    • Free vehicle entry to state parks and recreation areas for life
    • 50% off camping and electric fees for campsites and camping cabins
    • Apply online in Go Outdoors South Dakota and upload your letter: Military park entrance licenses · SDCL 41-17-13.4
    What to gather (2)
    • K award or VA letter showing 100% total service-connected
    • Go Outdoors South Dakota profile

    Official source: Military park entrance licenses · Official source: SDCL 41-17-13.4 · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Tuition for Veterans

    • Honorably discharged South Dakota resident who served during a qualifying war period or campaign (SDCL 33A-2-2) and qualifies for in-state tuition; you must use any GI Bill or other federal tuition benefits first
    • One month of free tuition per month of qualifying service (1 to 4 academic years): undergraduate courses at Board of Regents universities, or any course at a technical college
    • If you count as a veteran only because of a service-connected disability, you need a VA rating of 10% or more
    • Send to the SD Department of Veterans Affairs, 425 E. Capitol Ave., Pierre: State education programs · SDCL 13-55-2
    What to gather (3)
    • Free Tuition application
    • DD214
    • VA rating letter if using the disability route

    Official source: SDCL 13-55-2 · Official source: State education programs · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Public Hiring Preference

    • Veteran (state definition) and US citizen applying to the state, a county, city or school district
    • Preference for appointment, hiring and promotion; a service-connected disability puts you ahead of non-disabled veterans
    • Age or a non-incapacitating impairment cannot disqualify you
    • Claim it on each public job application: DLR veterans' preference · SDCL 3-3-1
    What to gather (3)
    • DD214
    • VA letter showing your service-connected rating
    • Answer the veteran question on the application

    Official source: DLR veterans' preference · Official source: SDCL 3-3-1 · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Help From a Veterans Service Officer

    • Veterans, retired service members and their families
    • Trained County and Tribal Veterans Service Officers help you find and apply for benefits, free of charge
    • Can help you get the VA letters items 1 to 6 need
    • Find your county or tribal officer: SDDVA contacts
    What to gather (3)
    • DD214
    • Any VA letters you already have
    • List of the items above you want

    Official source: SDDVA contacts · Last verified 2026-11-03
