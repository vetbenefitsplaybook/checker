Florida Disabled Veteran Benefits by Rating (2026)

8 Florida benefits to look into, with the rating each one starts at. Use the checker to filter by your rating.

Florida state benefits

Last verified 2026-10-29

  • Total Homestead Exemption for 100% P&T Veterans

    100% + P&T
    • Honorably discharged with a service-connected total and permanent disability, with a letter from VA (or the U.S. government) certifying it
    • You own the home and use it as your homestead, and you're a permanent Florida resident on January 1 of the tax year
    • Exempts the home from property tax (all ad valorem taxes)
    • Surviving spouse keeps it while living there and not remarried, and can move it (up to the same amount) to a new home

    Official source: Fla. Stat. § 196.081 · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • $5,000 Disabled Veteran Property Exemption (10%+)

    10%+
    • Florida resident, discharged under honorable conditions, and disabled 10% or more by misfortune or during wartime service
    • $5,000 off the property's assessed value. Not limited to your homestead (DOR PT-109)
    • Unremarried surviving spouses also qualify

    Official source: Fla. Stat. § 196.24 · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • Combat-Related Disability Discount, Age 65+

    Other rules
    • Age 65 or older, honorably discharged, with a permanent service-connected disability (partial or total) that was combat-related
    • Discount on the homestead's property tax equal to your disability percentage (e.g., 40% rating = 40% off)
    • Carries over to a surviving spouse who lives there and doesn't remarry

    Official source: Fla. Stat. § 196.082 · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • Tax-Free Disabled Veteran (DV) License Plate

    100%+
    • Honorably discharged, and a Florida resident for the last 5 years in a row (or with a filed Declaration of Domicile)
    • And one of: VA rated you 100% service-connected for compensation · 100% service-connected and receiving military disability retirement pay · your vehicle was bought with VA auto-grant help
    • One tax-free plate on a vehicle you own or lease (no annual use tax). FLHSMV still lists reflectorization, decal, service and branch fees. Extra DV plates pay normal tax and fees

    Official source: Fla. Stat. § 320.084 · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • No-Fee Driver License or ID Card + "Veteran" Designation

    100% + P&T
    • Honorably discharged, and one of: VA rated you 100% P&T service-connected for compensation · an FDVA disabled veteran ID card · 100% service-connected P&T and receiving military disability retirement pay
    • Exempt from all fees under § 322.21 for a license (with endorsements) or ID card.
    • The "Veteran" designation is also no-fee for these veterans

    Official source: FLHSMV Military & Veterans · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • Free 5-Year Hunting & Fishing License (Persons with Disabilities)

    50%+
    • Florida resident, honorably discharged, with a 50%+ service-connected rating (VA or a military branch). T&P-certified veterans also qualify under § 379.353(1)(a)
    • No cost, valid 5 years: saltwater and freshwater fishing and hunting licenses, plus deer, WMA, archery, muzzleloading, crossbow, turkey, Florida waterfowl, snook and lobster permits
    • Doesn't include the Federal Duck Stamp, tarpon tags or some special hunts

    Official source: Fla. Stat. § 379.353 · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • Free Lifetime Military Entrance Pass to Florida State Parks

    Any rating
    • Honorably discharged U.S. veteran with a service-connected disability (no minimum rating is listed)
    • Same benefits as the Family Annual Entrance Pass: park entry, not camping, tours or other special-use fees
    • Also free for surviving spouses and parents of service members who fell in combat

    Official source: Fla. Stat. § 258.0145 · Last verified 2026-10-29

  • CSDDV Scholarship for Spouses and Children of 100% P&T Veterans

    100% + P&T
    • Education at state expense for a dependent child or spouse of a veteran rated 100% P&T service-connected for compensation (or with an FDVA ID card, or 100% P&T with military disability retirement), or who died of service-connected causes on active duty
    • Residency: Florida was the servicemember's DEERS home of record right before the disability (or death), or the student is a Florida resident for tuition (and, for a living veteran, the veteran lives in Florida)
    • Spouse of a disabled vet: married 1+ year; eligible while married. Unremarried spouse of a deceased servicemember: apply within 5 years of the death. FDVA: usable with Chapter 35 (DEA)

    Official source: Fla. Stat. § 295.01 · Last verified 2026-10-29

Federal benefits to look into

  • CHAMPVA: health coverage for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    It's for family members, not the veteran: the spouse or dependent child of a veteran rated P&T from a service-connected disability. It's a cost-sharing program: VA shares the cost of covered care with the family. Not free. Family members eligible for TRICARE can't get CHAMPVA.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DEA (Chapter 35): school money for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    Chapter 35 / DEA education benefits are available to the spouse and children of a veteran who is P&T due to a service-connected disability. For school or training that started on or after Aug 1, 2018, DEA pays up to 36 months of benefits.

    Official source: VA: DEA · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Social Security: faster disability decisions

    100% + P&T

    SSA expedites disability claims from veterans rated 100% P&T. Say "veteran rated 100% P&T" (or enter "Veteran 100% P&T" in the online Remarks). It doesn't guarantee approval; SSA's own rules still apply.

    Official source: SSA: Veterans · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Dental insurance for CHAMPVA families (VADIP)

    P&T

    CHAMPVA covers dental only in limited medical cases; enrollees can buy dental insurance at reduced cost through VADIP.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Your Benefit Summary Letter (proof of your rating)

    All veterans

    You can download your Benefit Summary Letter (sometimes called an award letter) on VA.gov.

    Official source: VA: Download VA letters · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • VA dental care (Class IV)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Per VA, you qualify for any needed dental care if you have a service-connected disability rated 100% disabling, or you're paid at the 100% rate because of TDIU. A temporary 100% rating does not qualify.

    Official source: VA: Dental care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DoD ID card and base privileges (100%)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    A 100% disabled veteran (DAV) and eligible family members can get a DoD Uniformed Services ID card. It gives commissary, exchange and MWR privileges and base access. It is not TRICARE.

    Official source: eCFR: 32 CFR part 161 · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Base retail access for all service-connected veterans

    Any rating

    All service-connected veterans can use commissaries, exchanges and MWR retail facilities, in person and online, per VA's matrix.

    Official source: VA: Derivative benefits matrix · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Federal student loan discharge (TPD)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Veterans whom VA has determined have a service-connected disability rated 100% or are rated totally disabled based on individual unemployability (TDIU) can have eligible federal student loans discharged.

    Official source: Federal Student Aid: TPD discharge · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • No VA home loan funding fee

    10%+

    Veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability are exempt from the VA home loan funding fee, at any compensable rating, not just 100%.

    Official source: VA: Funding fee · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • America the Beautiful Access Pass

    Other rules

    A free, lifetime pass for U.S. citizens or permanent residents with a permanent disability. It doesn't have to be 100% or service-connected.

    Official source: NPS: Access Pass · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Military Lifetime Pass (federal lands)

    All veterans

    Veterans (and Gold Star families) can get a free lifetime pass to federal recreation lands. No disability is needed.

    Official source: NPS: Veterans free access · Last verified 2026-11-01
