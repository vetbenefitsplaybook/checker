West Virginia Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Where to get help

The West Virginia Department of Veterans Assistance (WVDVA) has Veterans Service Officers (VSOs) at benefit offices around the state who can help with these benefits (WVDVA state benefits; 304-558-3661).

Official source: WVDVA state benefits · Last verified 2026-11-03

Rating guide

90%+ service-connected or individual unemployability: property tax paid back as a refundable credit (items 1, 2) · VA-certified total and permanent: $20,000 homestead exemption (item 3) · 100% P&T or VA auto grant: no registration fee (item 4) · West Virginia residents with a total permanent service-connected veterans pension, or exempt from the registration fee under 17A-10-8: free hunting, trapping and fishing (item 5) · Military retirees: retired pay not taxed (item 6) · All veterans: 10% off state park stays (item 7) · Medically certified disability: income deduction (item 8). (page 1 rating guide; each part matches the item it points to)

West Virginia state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Disabled Veteran Real Property Tax Credit

    • Honorably discharged veteran rated by VA at 90% or more service-connected, or eligible for individual unemployability (state law, W. Va. Code 11-13MM-2; the Tax Division's TSD-455 still says 90 to 100% permanent and total)
    • A refundable income tax credit for the property tax you actually paid on your homestead
    • Pay on time (first half before October 1, second half before April 1). You can't also take the Senior Citizen or Homestead Excess credits
    • Claim it on your WV income tax return: TSD-455 (PDF) · DV-1 instructions
    What to gather (3)
    • Form DV-1 with your IT-140 (amount goes on line 21(B))
    • Property tax receipts
    • VA disability rating letter (first year you claim)

    Official source: DV-1 instructions · Official source: TSD-455 (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Surviving Spouse Tax Credit

    • Widow or widower of a veteran who claimed the credit in a prior year
    • You haven't remarried, and the homestead hasn't been sold or reclassified
    • Same refundable credit for property tax paid on time
    • Claim it on your WV income tax return: TSD-455 (PDF)
    What to gather (3)
    • Form DV-1 and IT-140
    • Property tax receipts
    • Proof of the veteran's death, if the Tax Division asks

    Official source: TSD-455 (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • $20,000 Homestead Exemption

    • Homeowner certified as permanently and totally disabled; a written VA certification counts (or age 65+)
    • The first $20,000 of your home's assessed value is exempt
    • Two years of WV residency required. File once; no need to refile unless you move
    • File with your county assessor by December 1: W. Va. Code 11-6B-4 · 11-6B-3
    What to gather (3)
    • VA written certification of total and permanent disability
    • Proof of residency (WV voter or vehicle registration)
    • Sworn statement that you get no similar exemption in another state

    Official source: 11-6B-3 · Official source: W. Va. Code 11-6B-4 · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Disabled Veteran Plate with No Registration Fee

    • Veteran with a 100% total and permanent service-connected rating, or who got a VA auto grant
    • No registration fee on one vehicle at 100% P&T (auto grant vehicles also qualify); DV plate included; small DMV fees ($3 to $15, depending on the year of the 10-year plate cycle) still apply
    • Not for vehicles used for hire or commercial purposes. Personalizing adds $15 a year
    • Send to DMV Special Plates, PO Box 17120, Charleston, WV 25317, or a DMV regional office: DMV-48-D (PDF) · WVDVA state benefits
    What to gather (2)
    • Form DMV-48-D, certified by WVDVA (section F)
    • Vehicle title and insurance information; county personal property tax receipt (or assessor's affidavit DMV-178-TR)

    Official source: WVDVA state benefits · Official source: DMV-48-D (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Hunting, Trapping and Fishing

    • A West Virginia resident honorably discharged from the U.S. armed forces who receives a veterans pension for a total permanent service-connected disability, certified by the Veterans Administration
    • Also a resident disabled veteran whom the Division of Motor Vehicles certifies as exempt from the motor-vehicle registration fee under W. Va. Code 17A-10-8
    • May hunt, trap, or fish without a license. The Division of Natural Resources sets the certification and identification rules

    Official source: W. Va. Code § 20-2-28 · Last verified 2026-11-09

  • Military Retired Pay Not Taxed

    • Military retirees from the regular Armed Forces, Reserves and National Guard
    • West Virginia doesn't tax military retired pay, including survivorship annuities
    • Recently separated: active duty pay may also be subtracted (line 37)
    • Subtract it on Schedule M when you file: IT-140 instructions (PDF)
    What to gather (2)
    • Your DFAS 1099-R (include it even with no withholding)
    • Schedule M, line 32

    Official source: IT-140 instructions (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • 10% Off State Park Lodging and Camping

    • Veterans (any rating) and active duty service members
    • 10% off lodge rooms, cabins and campsites year-round ("Veterans' Salute"); 15% off greens fees at three park golf courses
    • Not at Stonewall Resort State Park
    • Use code Vets10 when you book: WVDVA state benefits · Army benefits summary
    What to gather (1)
    • Valid military ID at check-in

    Official source: WVDVA state benefits · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Up to $8,000 Disability Income Deduction

    • Taxpayers under 65 medically certified as unable to work (any substantial gainful activity) due to a physical or mental impairment
    • Reduces your WV taxable income by up to $8,000 (line 47), reduced by any retirement subtractions on lines 29 to 34 (including military retired pay)
    • Surviving spouses may qualify for a similar deduction
    • Claim it on Schedule M when you file: IT-140 instructions (PDF)
    What to gather (2)
    • WV Schedule H or federal Schedule R (first year of disability)
    • The year your disability began

    Official source: IT-140 instructions (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03
