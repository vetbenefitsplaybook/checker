Massachusetts Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Massachusetts state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-01

  • $400 Property Tax Exemption (Clause 22)

    • Veteran with a 10%+ service-connected rating, discharged under other than dishonorable conditions, and a Massachusetts legal resident
    • Lived in Massachusetts 6 months before entering service, or 2 straight years before filing. You must own the home and live in it as your domicile
    • Base amount $400 (or $2,000 of value, whichever saves more). Your city or town may double it or raise it with inflation. You get only one Chapter 59 exemption a year: the largest you qualify for
    • File with your local board of assessors: EOVS page · DOR guide
    What to gather (4)
    • Your city or town's exemption application
    • VA letter showing your rating
    • DD-214
    • Proof of residency and ownership

    Official source: DOR guide · Official source: EOVS page · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • $1,000 Exemption at 100% (Clause 22E)

    • Veteran rated 100% service-connected by the VA, with the same residency, domicile and discharge rules as item 1
    • Base amount $1,000 (or $6,000 of value, whichever saves more); your city or town may double or index it
    • Continues for a surviving spouse who still owns and lives in the home. Assessors require a yearly statement that the rating is still 100%
    • File with your local board of assessors: EOVS page · M.G.L. c. 59, sec. 5
    What to gather (4)
    • Exemption application
    • VA letter showing 100%
    • DD-214
    • Proof of residency and ownership

    Official source: M.G.L. c. 59, sec. 5 · Official source: EOVS page · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Full Exemption for Surviving Spouses (Clause 22D)

    • Surviving spouse of a service member, Guard member or veteran whose death was caused by an injury or illness from active duty service, or who is missing in action and presumed dead
    • You lived in Massachusetts 2 straight years before filing, or the service member lived here 6 months before entering service
    • Full exemption on the home you live in, until you die or remarry; the state reimburses your city or town
    • File with your local board of assessors: EOVS page
    What to gather (3)
    • Exemption application
    • Death certificate and marriage certificate
    • VA or military documentation linking the death to active duty service

    Official source: EOVS page · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • $2,500 Yearly Veteran Annuity

    • Massachusetts resident when you apply and while you receive it; must meet the requirements of M.G.L. c. 115, secs. 6B and 6C
    • One $2,500 payment each year in August. Certain surviving spouses and parents also qualify
    • Apply online, by email or mail, or through your local VSO: EOVS annuity page
    What to gather (3)
    • Veteran Annuity Application, EFT direct deposit form and IRS W-9
    • DD-214 (Member 4 copy)
    • VA rating decision showing 100%

    Official source: EOVS annuity page · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • No RMV Registration or License Fees

    • Veteran the VA rates 100% or finds unemployable due to service-connected disability
    • No registration fees on any vehicle where you are listed as an owner, and no driver's license transaction fees (all classes)
    • Show your VA letter at an RMV Service Center or ask the RMV: RMV disabled veteran exemptions
    What to gather (1)
    • VA letter showing 100% or individual unemployability

    Official source: RMV disabled veteran exemptions · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Motor Vehicle Excise Tax Exemption

    • Veteran the VA rates 100% combined or finds individually unemployable due to service-connected disability
    • No excise tax on one passenger vehicle or pickup truck you own
    • The vehicle must be for non-commercial use
    What to gather (2)
    • VA letter showing 100% or IU
    • Vehicle registration

    Official source: RMV disabled veteran exemptions · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Disabled Veteran Plates

    • Massachusetts resident veteran who qualifies for permanent disabled parking, with a VA rating of 60%+ where conditions totaling 60% affect walking
    • Primary owner of a registered passenger vehicle
    • DV plates bring a sales tax exemption on one vehicle and waived daily parking fees at all DCR state parks
    • Apply through the RMV: RMV disabled veteran exemptions · DCR parking
    What to gather (3)
    • VA disability breakdown letter
    • Disabled parking application
    • Vehicle registration

    Official source: DCR parking · Official source: RMV disabled veteran exemptions · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Military Pension Not Taxed

    • U.S. military pensions are excluded from Massachusetts gross income
    • For residents and nonresidents; no age or rating requirement
    • Also covers NOAA and Public Health Service Commissioned Corps pensions
    • Exclude it on your Massachusetts return: DOR military tax page
    What to gather (1)
    • Your 1099-R for military retired pay

    Official source: DOR military tax page · Last verified 2026-11-01

Other cleared points

  • Deadline: file property tax exemptions with your local assessors each fiscal year by April 1 or 3 months after actual tax bills are mailed, whichever is later; you must qualify as of July 1. Annuity applications must be received and approved by June 30. Help: every city and town has a Veterans' Service Officer who takes applications for state benefits, including the annuity (find your local VSO; EOVS 617-210-5480).
