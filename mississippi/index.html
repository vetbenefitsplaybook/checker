Mississippi Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Where to get help

the Mississippi Veterans Affairs Board (MSVA) has Veterans Benefits Specialists in offices across the state who help with VA claims and state benefits (MSVA benefits specialists; 601-576-4850).

Official source: MSVA benefits specialists · Last verified 2026-11-03

Rating guide

Service-connected total disability: no property tax on your homestead (item 1) · Mississippi residents with a service-connected total disability: no hunting or fishing license needed (item 4) · 100% permanent, or 70%+ not permanent: $1 DAV car tags (item 3) · Any service-connected disability: no firearm permit license fee (item 6) · Surviving spouses: item 2 · Military retirees: retired pay not taxed (item 5) · All veterans: 15% off state park stays, free records for VA claims (items 7, 8). (page 1 rating guide; each part matches the item it points to)

Mississippi state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Total Homestead Exemption for Total Disability

    • Honorably discharged veteran classified as having a service-connected, total disability
    • Exempt from all property taxes on your qualifying homestead (Homestead Tier 3)
    • You must own and live in the home as your primary residence on January 1, and file Mississippi income tax as a resident
    • Apply in person at your county tax assessor's office between January 1 and April 1: Dept. of Revenue homestead page · MSVA state benefits
    What to gather (3)
    • VA letter showing your service-connected total disability
    • Social Security numbers and dates of birth for all applicants
    • Closing statement or deed, and tag numbers of all your vehicles

    Official source: Dept. of Revenue homestead page · Official source: MSVA state benefits · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Surviving Spouse Homestead Exemption

    • Unremarried surviving spouse of an honorably discharged veteran with a service-connected total disability
    • Unremarried surviving spouse of a service member killed or who died on active duty (or a Guard or reserve member who died on active duty for training)
    • Same total exemption from property taxes on the homestead
    • Reapply with your county tax assessor January 1 to April 1 after a death or other change: Dept. of Revenue homestead page
    What to gather (2)
    • Proof of the veteran's death and your marriage (ask your county tax assessor what they accept)
    • Homestead application documents (see item 1)

    Official source: Dept. of Revenue homestead page · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • $1 Disabled American Veteran Car Tags

    • Mississippi resident rated 100% permanent service-connected, or at least 70% not permanent
    • Up to 2 vehicle tags and 1 motorcycle tag a year for $1 each; those vehicles are exempt from ad valorem and privilege taxes
    • At 70% not permanent, bring an updated VA benefits letter at every renewal. An unremarried surviving spouse can keep or request the tags
    • Apply at your county tax collector: Official Air Force benefits summary (Mississippi)
    What to gather (2)
    • VA benefits letter showing your rating
    • Proof you own the vehicle or motorcycle

    Official source: Official Air Force benefits summary (Mississippi) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • No Hunting or Fishing License Needed

    • Mississippi resident who is totally service-connected disabled by VA
    • You are not required to buy a hunting or fishing license
    • Carry proof of your age, Mississippi residency and disability status whenever you hunt or fish
    • Nothing to file; carry your proof: MDWFP hunting license rules · fishing license rules
    What to gather (2)
    • VA letter showing a total service-connected disability
    • Mississippi driver's license (proof of age and residency)

    Official source: fishing license rules · Official source: MDWFP hunting license rules · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Military Retired Pay Not Taxed

    • Military retirees who have met their retirement plan's requirements
    • Mississippi does not tax retirement income such as military retired pay, or VA payments
    • Military Survivor Benefit Plan annuities are not taxed either
    • Leave it out of taxable income when you file: Dept. of Revenue income tax FAQ · Air Force benefits summary
    What to gather (2)
    • Your Form 1099-R
    • Your Mississippi resident income tax return

    Official source: Air Force benefits summary · Official source: Dept. of Revenue income tax FAQ · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • No Firearm Permit License Fee

    • Veteran with a service-connected disability (Mississippi resident, with some exceptions to the residency rule)
    • The $80 license fee for an individual firearm permit is waived, for new permits and renewals
    • The $32 FBI background check fee still applies
    • Apply with the Department of Public Safety: DPS permit instructions (PDF) · MSVA state benefits
    What to gather (2)
    • Copy of your Veteran Health Identification Card (VHIC)
    • Permit application with "Yes" checked on line C-3

    Official source: DPS permit instructions (PDF) · Official source: MSVA state benefits · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • 15% Off State Park Camping and Lodging

    • Veterans (any rating); the discount also covers people 62+, people with disabilities and active service members
    • 15% off the camping and lodging rate at Mississippi state parks
    • Not on holidays or during special events
    • Ask for the discount when you book; show proof and ID at check-in: MDWFP state park fees
    What to gather (2)
    • Proof of veteran status
    • Government-issued ID

    Official source: MDWFP state park fees · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Records for VA Claims

    • Mississippi resident veterans and their claimants (such as spouses and dependents)
    • Free birth and death certificates, and free copies of marriage licenses, divorce and adoption decrees, when needed for a VA dependency, disability or survivors claim
    • Your county chancery clerk records your discharge (DD214) for free and gives free certified copies
    • Ask the State Bureau of Vital Statistics or your county chancery or circuit clerk: MSVA state benefits
    What to gather (2)
    • Your claim details (what the record is for)
    • DD214 to record

    Official source: MSVA state benefits · Last verified 2026-11-03
