Maryland Disabled Veteran Benefits by Rating (2026)

8 Maryland benefits to look into, with the rating each one starts at. Use the checker to filter by your rating.

Maryland state benefits

Last verified 2026-10-30

  • 100% Disabled Veteran Property Tax Exemption

    100% + P&T
    • Maryland resident and veteran (not dishonorably discharged) rated by VA as 100% service-connected, permanent (SDAT also accepts permanently unemployable); the home must be your legal residence
    • Full exemption from property tax on your principal residence and lot (occupied by no more than 2 families)
    • You can apply before you buy: within 15 business days SDAT sends a letter saying whether it is preliminarily approved or denied. For refunds of past taxes, check the box and apply within 3 years of the calendar year VA granted 100% P&T (no refunds before 2018-19)

    Official source: SDAT exemptions · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Surviving Spouse Property Tax Exemption

    Other rules
    • Unremarried surviving spouse of a veteran rated 100% service-connected permanent (before or after death). If the home wasn't already exempt, the veteran must have been domiciled in Maryland at death
    • Or the unremarried spouse of a service member who died in the line of duty (Surviving Spouse of Military Casualty). The home must have been owned at the death, or bought within 2 years of it if either of you was domiciled in Maryland at the death
    • You must own and live in the home. Carries over to a later home up to the prior exemption amount

    Official source: SDAT exemptions · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Disabled Veteran Plates and Registration

    50%+
    • 100% rated: free DV plates (with or without the wheelchair symbol) and permanent registration, no other fees
    • 50 to 99% (or IU at that level): free DV plates, but you still pay annual registration fees and surcharge
    • Law exempts a vehicle of a veteran VA rates totally disabled or with loss of use of a hand, arm or leg, or blindness, from registration fees

    Official source: MVA military plates · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Free Lifetime Hunting License

    100%+
    • Maryland resident who is a 100% service-connected disabled veteran, deemed unemployable by VA, or a former POW
    • Free lifetime license, not transferable. Includes the bow, muzzleloader and sika deer stamps and furbearer permit. You also need a hunter safety certificate (or a hunting license issued before July 1, 1977)
    • Migratory bird stamps and the Big Game Harvest Record are still needed each year. Maryland-resident Purple Heart recipients: 50% off hunting and fishing licenses

    Official source: DNR hunting licenses · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Free Lifetime Fishing License

    100%+
    • Same rule as item 4: 100% service-connected, deemed unemployable by VA, or a former POW; Maryland resident
    • Free lifetime license covering the nontidal angler's license, trout stamp, Chesapeake Bay sport fishing and recreational oyster licenses
    • Not transferable. Maryland-resident Purple Heart recipients get 50% off licenses instead

    Official source: DNR fishing licenses · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Free State Park Day Use for Veterans

    All veterans
    • Any veteran, resident or out-of-state; no disability rating needed
    • Day-use entry fee waived year-round for you; if the park charges per vehicle, the vehicle fee is waived
    • Camping and other facility fees still apply. Others in your car pay unless there is a per-vehicle charge

    Official source: DNR military one-stop · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Military Retirement Income Subtraction

    Other rules
    • Under 55 on Dec 31: subtract the first $12,500 of military retirement income
    • 55 or older: subtract the first $20,000. Includes reserve and Guard retirement and death benefits
    • Age 65+ or totally disabled: you may also qualify for the pension exclusion. VA disability pay is federal tax-free

    Official source: DVMF retirement tax · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Conroy Scholarship for Your Family

    100% + P&T
    • Child, stepchild or unremarried surviving spouse of a service member with a service-connected 100% permanent disability (or who died from service)
    • Veterans with a 25%+ rating who have used up federal GI benefits can qualify too (15 awards a year)
    • Up to $14,086 for 2026-27 (capped at tuition and fees); 5 years full time. Age 16+. You must be a Maryland resident when you apply, or have been when the disability or death occurred. Subject to funding; renewals get priority

    Official source: MHEC Conroy · Last verified 2026-10-30

Federal benefits to look into

  • CHAMPVA: health coverage for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    It's for family members, not the veteran: the spouse or dependent child of a veteran rated P&T from a service-connected disability. It's a cost-sharing program: VA shares the cost of covered care with the family. Not free. Family members eligible for TRICARE can't get CHAMPVA.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DEA (Chapter 35): school money for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    Chapter 35 / DEA education benefits are available to the spouse and children of a veteran who is P&T due to a service-connected disability. For school or training that started on or after Aug 1, 2018, DEA pays up to 36 months of benefits.

    Official source: VA: DEA · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Social Security: faster disability decisions

    100% + P&T

    SSA expedites disability claims from veterans rated 100% P&T. Say "veteran rated 100% P&T" (or enter "Veteran 100% P&T" in the online Remarks). It doesn't guarantee approval; SSA's own rules still apply.

    Official source: SSA: Veterans · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Dental insurance for CHAMPVA families (VADIP)

    P&T

    CHAMPVA covers dental only in limited medical cases; enrollees can buy dental insurance at reduced cost through VADIP.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Your Benefit Summary Letter (proof of your rating)

    All veterans

    You can download your Benefit Summary Letter (sometimes called an award letter) on VA.gov.

    Official source: VA: Download VA letters · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • VA dental care (Class IV)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Per VA, you qualify for any needed dental care if you have a service-connected disability rated 100% disabling, or you're paid at the 100% rate because of TDIU. A temporary 100% rating does not qualify.

    Official source: VA: Dental care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DoD ID card and base privileges (100%)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    A 100% disabled veteran (DAV) and eligible family members can get a DoD Uniformed Services ID card. It gives commissary, exchange and MWR privileges and base access. It is not TRICARE.

    Official source: eCFR: 32 CFR part 161 · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Base retail access for all service-connected veterans

    Any rating

    All service-connected veterans can use commissaries, exchanges and MWR retail facilities, in person and online, per VA's matrix.

    Official source: VA: Derivative benefits matrix · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Federal student loan discharge (TPD)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Veterans whom VA has determined have a service-connected disability rated 100% or are rated totally disabled based on individual unemployability (TDIU) can have eligible federal student loans discharged.

    Official source: Federal Student Aid: TPD discharge · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • No VA home loan funding fee

    10%+

    Veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability are exempt from the VA home loan funding fee, at any compensable rating, not just 100%.

    Official source: VA: Funding fee · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • America the Beautiful Access Pass

    Other rules

    A free, lifetime pass for U.S. citizens or permanent residents with a permanent disability. It doesn't have to be 100% or service-connected.

    Official source: NPS: Access Pass · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Military Lifetime Pass (federal lands)

    All veterans

    Veterans (and Gold Star families) can get a free lifetime pass to federal recreation lands. No disability is needed.

    Official source: NPS: Veterans free access · Last verified 2026-11-01
