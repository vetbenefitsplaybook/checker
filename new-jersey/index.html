New Jersey Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

New Jersey state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-01

  • 100% Disabled Veteran Property Tax Exemption

    • Honorably discharged veteran with active duty service, a legal New Jersey resident, with a VA certification of a 100% permanent and total service-connected disability from active duty, who owns and lives in the home
    • Full exemption from property taxes on the home you own and live in as your main residence
    • File D.V.S.S.E. and proofs with your local tax assessor: Division of Taxation page · Form D.V.S.S.E.
    What to gather (3)
    • Form D.V.S.S.E.
    • DD-214
    • Deed and NJ driver's license or voter registration

    Official source: Division of Taxation page · Official source: Form D.V.S.S.E. · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Surviving Spouse Property Tax Exemption

    • Surviving spouse, civil union or domestic partner of an honorably discharged veteran who was rated 100% P&T, or of a service member who died on active duty
    • The veteran or service member was a New Jersey resident at death; you are a legal New Jersey resident and have not remarried or formed a new civil union or domestic partnership
    • Same full exemption on the home you own and live in as your main residence
    • File D.V.S.S.E. and proofs with your local tax assessor: Division of Taxation page
    What to gather (3)
    • Form D.V.S.S.E.
    • Death certificate and marriage, civil union or partnership certificate
    • Deed

    Official source: Division of Taxation page · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • $250 Veteran Property Tax Deduction

    • Honorably discharged veteran with active duty service (Reservists and Guard only if called to active duty; active duty for training doesn't count)
    • Legal New Jersey resident who owns the property, all as of October 1 of the year before the tax year
    • $250 off your annual property tax bill. An unremarried surviving spouse or partner may also qualify
    • File Form V.S.S. with your local assessor or tax collector: Division of Taxation page
    What to gather (4)
    • Form V.S.S.
    • DD-214
    • Deed
    • NJ driver's license or voter registration

    Official source: Division of Taxation page · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • $6,000 Veteran Income Tax Exemption

    • Veteran honorably discharged or released under honorable circumstances from active duty
    • A $6,000 exemption on your NJ income tax return each year; a spouse or civil union partner who is also a qualifying veteran gets one too on a joint return
    • Does not pass to a surviving spouse (except for the tax year of the veteran's death)
    • Send the form and DD-214 once (online with code VET, by mail to PO Box 440, or fax 609-633-8427), then claim it on your return: Division of Taxation page
    What to gather (2)
    • DD-214 showing character of service
    • Veteran Income Tax Exemption Submission Form (first year only)

    Official source: Division of Taxation page · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Military Pension Not Taxed

    • U.S. military pension and military survivor's benefit payments are not taxed by New Jersey
    • No age or rating requirement
    • Federal civil service pensions are still taxable, even if they include credit for military service
    • Leave it off your New Jersey income tax return: Division of Taxation military page
    What to gather (1)
    • Your 1099-R for military retired pay or survivor benefits

    Official source: Division of Taxation military page · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Free Hunting and Fishing Licenses

    • Honorably discharged veteran with a VA-recognized service-connected disability of any degree
    • New Jersey resident with NJ as your primary home for at least 6 months
    • Mail the certification form and copies to NJ Fish and Wildlife (about 3 weeks): Disabled Veteran Certification form
    What to gather (4)
    • Conservation ID (CID) from NJ's online license system
    • VA letter or card showing a service-connected disability
    • DD-214
    • NJ driver's license or state ID

    Official source: Disabled Veteran Certification form · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Wheelchair-Symbol Plates and Placard

    • Veteran certified by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs as permanently 100% disabled
    • Plates for one vehicle you own, operate or lease, and/or a placard
    • Plates renew yearly; disability recertification every 3 years
    • Mail SP-41 to the MVC Special Plate Unit within 60 days of certification (609-633-8857): Form SP-41
    What to gather (3)
    • Form SP-41 with Section E signed by a VA representative
    • Copy of your vehicle registration (for plates)
    • NJ driver's license

    Official source: Form SP-41 · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • No Sales Tax on a VA-Funded Vehicle

    • Disabled veteran who received funds or a loan from the VA or NJDVA to buy a car
    • The amount the VA or NJDVA paid is not subject to sales tax
    • Any amount you pay out of pocket is still taxed
    What to gather (2)
    • VA or NJDVA paperwork showing the vehicle funding
    • Purchase contract

    Official source: Division of Taxation military tax benefits · Last verified 2026-11-01

Other cleared points

  • Deadline: the $250 deduction looks at your status as of October 1 of the year before the tax year. If the assessor denies a property tax claim, appeal to the County Board of Taxation by April 1 (January 15 in Burlington, Gloucester and Monmouth counties). Help: NJ Department of Veterans Affairs Veterans Service Offices, led by trained and accredited VSOs, serve veterans in all 21 counties (find your county office; toll-free 844-671-1019).
