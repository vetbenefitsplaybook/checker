Louisiana Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Where to get help

the Louisiana Department of Veterans Affairs (LDVA) has veterans assistance counselors (VACs) in service offices across the state. They file VA claims free of charge and certify several of the forms below (LDVA office locator; 225-219-5000).

Official source: LDVA office locator · Last verified 2026-11-03

Rating guide

100% or individual unemployability (IU): no property tax on your homestead (item 1) · 90%+ or IU: tuition exemption for your children (item 8) · 70%–99%: next $4,500 of assessed value exempt (item 2) · 50%–69%: next $2,500 exempt (item 2) · 50%+: free DV plate (item 5) · permanent 50%+: free hunting and fishing license (item 6) · Louisiana residents at 50%+: no state park day-use fee (item 7) · Surviving spouses: item 3 · Military retirees: retired pay not taxed (item 4). (page 1 rating guide; each part matches the item it points to)

Last verified 2026-11-03. Official sources are on each benefit below.

Louisiana state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Full Property Tax Exemption at 100% or IU

    • Veteran rated 100% unemployability or totally disabled by VA (service-connected)
    • You own and live in the home and it gets the homestead exemption
    • After the regular homestead exemption, the rest of the assessed value is exempt from property tax (LDVA notes certain municipal assessments may still apply)
    • Apply with your parish assessor: LDVA state benefits · La. Const. art. VII, sec. 21(K)
    What to gather (3)
    • VA benefit letter showing your rating
    • Proof you own and live in the home
    • Any certification form your parish assessor asks for (an LDVA VAC can help)

    Official source: LDVA state benefits · Official source: La. Const. art. VII, sec. 21(K) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Extra Homestead Exemption at 50%–99%

    • Veteran with a VA service-connected rating of 50% to 99%, on a home you own and live in that gets the homestead exemption
    • 70%–99%: the next $4,500 of assessed value is exempt (taxes apply to assessed value over $12,000)
    • 50%–69%: the next $2,500 of assessed value is exempt (taxes apply to assessed value over $10,000)
    • Apply with your parish assessor: LDVA state benefits · Constitution text
    What to gather (2)
    • VA benefit letter showing your rating
    • Homestead exemption on file with the assessor

    Official source: LDVA state benefits · Official source: Constitution text · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Surviving Spouse Property Tax Exemption

    • Surviving spouse of a veteran who had one of the ratings in items 1 or 2
    • Gets the same exemption level the veteran qualified for
    • You must live in and still own the home; it counts even if the exemption was not on the home before the veteran died
    • Apply with your parish assessor: La. Const. art. VII, sec. 21(K)
    What to gather (3)
    • Veteran's VA rating letter
    • Death certificate
    • Proof you own and live in the home

    Official source: La. Const. art. VII, sec. 21(K) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Military Retired Pay Not Taxed

    • Military and other federal retirees
    • Federal retirement benefits, military and nonmilitary, may be excluded from Louisiana taxable income
    • Military Survivor Benefit Plan payments may also be excluded
    • Claim the federal retirement exclusion on your state return: Department of Revenue FAQ
    What to gather (2)
    • Your 1099-R for military retired pay
    • Your Louisiana individual income tax return

    Official source: Department of Revenue FAQ · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Disabled Veteran (DV) License Plate

    • Louisiana resident veteran with a service-connected disability of 50% or more
    • No fee for the plate, and no renewal requirement; it carries the accessibility symbol and the same parking privileges as mobility-impaired plates
    • A free hang tag on request. OMV charges handling ($8), transfer ($3) and replacement ($10) fees
    • Get the LDVA-17 from a VAC, then apply at the Office of Motor Vehicles: OMV disabled veteran plate policy · La. R.S. 47:490.4
    What to gather (2)
    • Affidavit LDVA-17 signed by a veterans affairs service officer (first issue only)
    • Vehicle registration certificate

    Official source: OMV disabled veteran plate policy · Official source: 490.4 · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Hunting and Fishing License at 50%+

    • Veteran with a permanent service-connected disability classification of 50% or more (resident or nonresident application)
    • Free: basic and saltwater fishing; basic, deer, waterfowl and turkey hunting; and the WMA Access Permit
    • Does not include the federal duck stamp. LDVA says the licenses expire each year and must be renewed
    • Mail, email or bring the application to LDWF in Baton Rouge: application (PDF) · LDWF special licenses
    What to gather (3)
    • Disabled Veteran License application
    • Copy of your driver's license or state ID
    • VA benefit letter, or section 5 of the form completed by a veterans service officer

    Official source: application (PDF) · Official source: LDWF special licenses · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • No day-use entrance fee at any Louisiana state park

    • Louisiana resident veteran rated 50% or more, or totally and permanently disabled, or with a limb amputation or a VA auto allowance
    • No day-use entrance fee at any Louisiana state park
    • People in the same private vehicle get in free too
    • Apply to the Office of State Parks for an entrance permit; LDVA verifies eligibility: La. R.S. 56:1694 · LDVA state benefits
    What to gather (2)
    • VA benefit letter
    • LDVA verification of eligibility

    Official source: LDVA state benefits · Official source: 1694 · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Title 29 Tuition Exemption for Your Children

    • Child, age 16 to 25, of a veteran rated 90% to 100% service-connected or found unemployable by VA
    • The veteran must have lived in Louisiana at least 12 months before the child enters school
    • No tuition or school-imposed fees at any Louisiana public postsecondary school. Spouses and survivors of certain deceased, POW or MIA veterans qualify too
    • Apply through a VAC at your parish veterans service office; the certificate goes to your school: LDVA Title 29
    What to gather (1)
    • VA rating letter (90%+ or unemployability)

    Official source: La. R.S. 29:288 · Official source: LDVA Title 29 · Last verified 2026-11-03
