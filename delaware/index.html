Delaware Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Where to get help

the Delaware Office of Veterans Services has VA-accredited Veteran Service Officers who help veterans and families find and apply for federal, state and local benefits, free of charge (Army benefits summary; OVS service officers; 302-739-2792).

Official source: Army benefits summary · Official source: OVS service officers · Last verified 2026-11-03

Rating guide

100% permanent and total (or paid at 100% for unemployability): no non-vocational school district property tax (item 1) · 60%+: free hunting, trapping and fishing (item 2) · Any service-connected disability: DAV plate (item 3) · VA adaptive equipment: no registration fee on one vehicle (item 4) · Military retirees: pension exclusion (item 5) · Disabled veterans: up to 10 hiring points (item 6) · Delaware resident veterans: half-price park pass (item 7) · Wartime paraplegia, rated totally disabled: $3,000 a year (item 8). (page 1 rating guide; each part matches the item it points to)

Delaware state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Disabled Veterans School Tax Credit

    • Veteran paid 100% VA disability compensation for a service-connected, permanent and total disability (100% rating or individual unemployability)
    • Credit for 100% of the non-vocational school district property tax on your primary residence; you need 3 years of Delaware domicile
    • Can't be combined with the Senior School Property Tax Credit; pay each year's tax bill in full to keep it
    • File with your county by April 30; no need to reapply unless you move: Dept. of Finance · FAQ · Application (PDF)
    What to gather (3)
    • Disabled Veterans School Tax Credit application
    • VA documentation of your disability
    • Driver's license or state ID, and a copy of your Social Security card

    Official source: Dept. of Finance · Official source: FAQ · Official source: Application (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Hunting, Trapping and Fishing at 60%+

    • Honorably discharged veteran certified by VA with a 60% or more service-connected disability
    • A license exemption to hunt, trap and fish without charge
    • Hunter, turkey or trapper education rules still apply where required
    • Send it to the Division of Fish and Wildlife (allow up to 4 weeks): Application (PDF) · Exemptions page
    What to gather (3)
    • Veterans with Disabilities Exempt Application
    • VA letter certifying your discharge and 60%+ rating
    • Copy of your driver's license (and education cards if you have them)

    Official source: Exemptions page · Official source: Application (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Disabled Veteran (DAV) License Plate

    • Veteran with a VA-certified service-connected disability, or a disabling injury in a war or conflict certified by the Department of Defense
    • For a passenger vehicle or a truck with a 3/4-ton or smaller rated capacity; a one-time $10 fee, with no fee beyond the regular registration fee
    • Doesn't allow parking in disability spaces (a wheelchair-symbol version needs separate disability proof)
    • Take it to any DMV office; plates come by mail in about 6 to 8 weeks: Form MV549 (PDF) · 21 Del. C. § 2138
    What to gather (3)
    • Form MV549, DAV License Plate Application
    • VA letter stating you have a service-connected disability
    • $10 fee and your registration details

    Official source: 21 Del. C. § 2138 · Official source: Form MV549 (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • No Registration Fee, Adapted Vehicle

    • Veteran with a disability who is eligible for VA adaptive equipment benefits (such as power steering, power brakes or lifts)
    • No registration fee on one vehicle at a time
    • Covers equipment that helps you get into and out of the vehicle
    • Ask at a DMV office when you register: DMV veterans services · Army benefits summary
    What to gather (2)
    • Documentation from VA showing you qualify for adaptive equipment
    • Vehicle title and registration paperwork

    Official source: DMV veterans services · Official source: Army benefits summary · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Military Pension Exclusion

    • Delaware resident military retirees receiving a U.S. military pension, at any age; if you're 60 or older, you need at least 3 years of Delaware domicile (5 if your domicile began on or after Jan 1, 2027)
    • Currently up to $12,500 of military pension excluded from Delaware income tax
    • Rises to $15,000 for tax year 2027, $20,000 for 2028 and $25,000 for 2029 and later (Senate Bill 219, signed August 17, 2026)
    • Claim the pension exclusion on your Delaware return: SB 219 (Laws of Del. 85:426) · 30 Del. C. § 1106 · Army benefits summary
    What to gather (2)
    • Your Form 1099-R
    • Your Delaware resident income tax return

    Official source: 30 Del. C. § 1106 · Official source: 426) · Official source: Army benefits summary · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • State Job Hiring Preference

    • Honorably discharged veteran who meets the job's qualifications
    • Up to 10 points added to your score for disabled veterans (5 for other veterans); first entry to merit jobs only
    • Not for promotions or layoff retention; some spouses and unremarried widows or widowers can claim 5 points
    • Get forms to Human Resources by 4:30 p.m. the business day after the posting closes: State jobs: veterans' preference · OVS veterans benefits
    What to gather (2)
    • DD214
    • VA letter showing your disability

    Official source: veterans' preference · Official source: OVS veterans benefits · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Half-Price State Park Pass

    • A Delaware resident who has honorably served, or is honorably serving, in the U.S. armed forces, including the Delaware National Guard, and who does not qualify for the separate free 12-month pass
    • Annual entrance fee of one-half the fee set for Delaware residents, for each motor vehicle the resident owns and has registered in Delaware
    • Bring proof of service, vehicle ownership, and vehicle registration

    Official source: 7 Del. C. § 4701(a)(5)e · Last verified 2026-11-09

  • $3,000 Pension for Paraplegic Veterans

    • Delaware resident veteran with paraplegia (no voluntary control over either leg), listed by VA as totally disabled for it
    • The disability must be a direct result of wartime service (or hostilities as a UN member)
    • Pays $3,000 a year in monthly installments; tell the Pension Board if your status changes
    • Contact the Office of Pensions (pensionoffice@delaware.gov; 302-739-4208): 20 Del. C. § 1001 · OVS veterans benefits
    What to gather (3)
    • VA letter showing total disability from paraplegia
    • DD214 showing wartime service
    • Proof of Delaware residency

    Official source: 20 Del. C. § 1001 · Official source: OVS veterans benefits · Last verified 2026-11-03
