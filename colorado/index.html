Colorado Disabled Veteran Benefits by Rating (2026)

8 Colorado benefits to look into, with the rating each one starts at. Use the checker to filter by your rating.

Colorado state benefits

Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Disabled Veteran Property Tax Exemption

    100% + P&T
    • Honorably discharged veteran rated 100% permanent by VA, or paid at 100% for individual unemployability
    • 50% of the first $200,000 of actual value of your owner-occupied primary residence exempt; owner and occupant as of Jan 1
    • One exemption per home. An unremarried surviving spouse of a veteran who had it can keep it. Report rating changes within 60 days

    Official source: DPT exemption · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Disabled Veteran License Plates

    50%+
    • Honorably discharged veteran with a 50%+ service-connected disability, loss of use of one or both hands or feet, or vision loss in both eyes
    • No fee for one set of Disabled Veteran (or Disabled Woman Veteran) plates
    • The plate does not give disabled parking privileges

    Official source: DMV DV plate checklist · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • No Registration Fee or Ownership Tax (1 Vehicle)

    50%+
    • Same rule as item 2: 50%+ service-connected, loss of use of a hand or foot, or blindness
    • No annual registration fee on one vehicle at a time, and that vehicle is exempt from specific ownership tax (Class B/C, under 16,000 lb)
    • DV plate holders qualify for the ownership tax exemption. Moves to your next vehicle when you replace it

    Official source: DMV DV plate checklist · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Free Lifetime Small Game & Fishing License

    50%+
    • Colorado resident veteran with a 50%+ combined service-connected rating, or a Purple Heart recipient
    • Free Lifetime Small Game and Fishing Combination License
    • Counts as a qualifying license for the big game draws. Hunter education still required (born 1949 or later)

    Official source: CPW disabled veterans · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Free Independence State Parks Pass

    50%+
    • Colorado resident veteran paid by VA for 50%+, loss of use of a hand or foot, or vision loss in both eyes; or a Purple Heart recipient
    • Free pass, valid 12 months, transferable between vehicles (you must be present). Apply once, then renew each year
    • DV or Purple Heart plates also get free park entry with no pass. Age 64+ pass holders: $3/night camping discount Sun to Thu

    Official source: CPW specialty passes · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Military Retirement Subtraction

    Other rules
    • Under 55 at year end: subtract up to $15,000 of military retired pay (tax years 2022 to 2028)
    • 55 and older: use the pension subtraction instead, up to $20,000 (55 to 64) or $24,000 (65+), less any Social Security subtraction
    • VA disability compensation is federal tax-free

    Official source: DOR retired servicemembers · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Veterans' Preference for State Jobs

    Other rules
    • Veteran separated under honorable conditions who served during a war or in a campaign with an authorized campaign badge; extra preference if you receive VA compensation or disability retired pay for a line-of-duty disability
    • Applies when you are hired into the state personnel system
    • No preference for promotions, including promotions also open to outside applicants

    Official source: Colorado veterans preference · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Gold Star Spouse Property Tax Exemption

    Other rules
    • Surviving spouse who has not remarried (required on the state application form), of a service member who died in the line of duty (DoD death gratuity paid), or of a veteran whose death was service-connected, if you receive VA DIC
    • Same benefit as item 1: 50% of the first $200,000 of your primary residence exempt
    • You must own and live in the home as of Jan 1. Added by voters as Amendment E (2022), from tax year 2023

    Official source: DPT exemption · Last verified 2026-10-30

Federal benefits to look into

  • CHAMPVA: health coverage for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    It's for family members, not the veteran: the spouse or dependent child of a veteran rated P&T from a service-connected disability. It's a cost-sharing program: VA shares the cost of covered care with the family. Not free. Family members eligible for TRICARE can't get CHAMPVA.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DEA (Chapter 35): school money for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    Chapter 35 / DEA education benefits are available to the spouse and children of a veteran who is P&T due to a service-connected disability. For school or training that started on or after Aug 1, 2018, DEA pays up to 36 months of benefits.

    Official source: VA: DEA · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Social Security: faster disability decisions

    100% + P&T

    SSA expedites disability claims from veterans rated 100% P&T. Say "veteran rated 100% P&T" (or enter "Veteran 100% P&T" in the online Remarks). It doesn't guarantee approval; SSA's own rules still apply.

    Official source: SSA: Veterans · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Dental insurance for CHAMPVA families (VADIP)

    P&T

    CHAMPVA covers dental only in limited medical cases; enrollees can buy dental insurance at reduced cost through VADIP.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Your Benefit Summary Letter (proof of your rating)

    All veterans

    You can download your Benefit Summary Letter (sometimes called an award letter) on VA.gov.

    Official source: VA: Download VA letters · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • VA dental care (Class IV)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Per VA, you qualify for any needed dental care if you have a service-connected disability rated 100% disabling, or you're paid at the 100% rate because of TDIU. A temporary 100% rating does not qualify.

    Official source: VA: Dental care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DoD ID card and base privileges (100%)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    A 100% disabled veteran (DAV) and eligible family members can get a DoD Uniformed Services ID card. It gives commissary, exchange and MWR privileges and base access. It is not TRICARE.

    Official source: eCFR: 32 CFR part 161 · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Base retail access for all service-connected veterans

    Any rating

    All service-connected veterans can use commissaries, exchanges and MWR retail facilities, in person and online, per VA's matrix.

    Official source: VA: Derivative benefits matrix · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Federal student loan discharge (TPD)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Veterans whom VA has determined have a service-connected disability rated 100% or are rated totally disabled based on individual unemployability (TDIU) can have eligible federal student loans discharged.

    Official source: Federal Student Aid: TPD discharge · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • No VA home loan funding fee

    10%+

    Veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability are exempt from the VA home loan funding fee, at any compensable rating, not just 100%.

    Official source: VA: Funding fee · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • America the Beautiful Access Pass

    Other rules

    A free, lifetime pass for U.S. citizens or permanent residents with a permanent disability. It doesn't have to be 100% or service-connected.

    Official source: NPS: Access Pass · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Military Lifetime Pass (federal lands)

    All veterans

    Veterans (and Gold Star families) can get a free lifetime pass to federal recreation lands. No disability is needed.

    Official source: NPS: Veterans free access · Last verified 2026-11-01
