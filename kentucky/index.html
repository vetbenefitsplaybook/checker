Kentucky Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Kentucky state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Homestead Exemption for Total Disability

    • Homeowner classified as totally disabled under a federal program (such as the VA) on January 1, receiving disability payments for the whole year
    • $49,100 off your home's assessed value (2025–2026 amount); you must own and live in the home
    • A veteran with a service-connected total disability documents it once and does not have to reapply every year. One exemption per home
    • File with your county PVA: Form 62A350 · DOR page
    What to gather (3)
    • Form 62A350 (homestead/disability application)
    • VA letter showing total disability and payments
    • Proof you own and live in the home

    Official source: Form 62A350 · Official source: DOR page · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Disabled Sportsman's License

    • Kentucky resident veteran at least 50% disabled from a service-connected disability
    • Valid through the end of February each year. You still need the HIP survey and a Federal Duck Stamp for waterfowl
    • Get disability authorization from KDFWR first, then buy the license: KDFWR page
    What to gather (1)
    • VA letter showing 50%+, your current Kentucky address and the last 4 digits of your SSN

    Official source: KDFWR page · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Free Disabled Veteran Plate and Registration

    • Kentucky resident veteran declared 100% service-connected by the VA, or who got VA help to buy a vehicle
    • No registration fee for the plate; the clerk still collects any usage or property taxes
    • Disabled veteran plates expire July 31
    • Apply at your county clerk: Form TC 96-217
    What to gather (3)
    • Form TC 96-217 (notarized)
    • VA authorization letter
    • Usual vehicle registration documents

    Official source: Form TC 96-217 · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Disabled Veteran Plate at 50%–99%

    • Veteran the VA rates 50% to 99% service-connected
    • Fee is for the plate; regular registration costs may still apply
    • Apply at your county clerk: KRS 186.162
    What to gather (3)
    • VA letter showing your rating
    • DD-214
    • Vehicle registration

    Official source: KRS 186.162 · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Tuition Waiver for Spouse and Children (100%)

    • Spouse (any age) or child, stepchild or orphan under 26 of a veteran rated 100% service-connected, or permanently and totally disabled for pension with wartime service
    • Waives tuition only (not fees, books or housing) at Kentucky state-supported schools, up to 45 months
    • Apply to KDVA after you are accepted: KDVA tuition waiver page
    What to gather (4)
    • Birth or marriage certificate
    • VA disability award letter
    • DD-214
    • School acceptance

    Official source: KDVA tuition waiver page · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Tuition Waiver for Survivors

    • Waives tuition at Kentucky state-supported schools, up to 45 months
    • Apply to KDVA after you are accepted: KDVA tuition waiver page
    What to gather (4)
    • Death certificate
    • Birth or marriage certificate
    • DD-214
    • Proof of service-connected death, if applicable

    Official source: KDVA tuition waiver page · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Free State Park Stays at 100%

    • Kentucky resident veteran 100% disabled from a service-connected incident (VA or DoD)
    • Up to 3 free nights a year, 3 nights max per visit, in a lodge room, cottage or campsite, subject to availability
    • Memorial Day–Labor Day and October: Sunday–Thursday stays only. Book by calling the park no more than 10 days ahead
    • Call the park to reserve: Kentucky State Parks deals
    What to gather (2)
    • VA disability letter
    • Proof of Kentucky residency (both required on arrival)

    Official source: Kentucky State Parks deals · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Pension Income Exclusion

    • Anyone with taxable pension or retirement income, including military retired pay
    • Exclude up to $31,110 on Schedule M
    • Military retirees with pension over $31,110 may exclude more by completing Schedule P
    • Claim it on your Kentucky return: DOR military page
    What to gather (2)
    • 1099-R for military retired pay
    • Schedule M and, if needed, Schedule P

    Official source: DOR military page · Last verified 2026-11-01

Other cleared points

  • Deadline: for the homestead exemption you must be classified totally disabled on January 1 and keep that status for the whole tax period; apply with your county property valuation administrator (PVA). Help: KDVA Veterans Benefits Field Representatives across Kentucky help free of charge (find your representative; KDVA 502-564-9203). Official source: find your representative
