New York Disabled Veteran Benefits by Rating (2026)

8 New York benefits to look into, with the rating each one starts at. Use the checker to filter by your rating.

New York state benefits

Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Alternative Veterans Exemption: Extra Exemption Equal to Half Your Rating

    Any rating
    • Veteran who served during a defined period of war (or received an expeditionary medal) with a VA or DoD service-connected disability rating
    • Extra exemption equal to one-half of your rating applied to assessed value (a 40% rating = 20%), on top of the 15% wartime and 10% combat-zone parts
    • Each part is capped by the dollar limits your municipality sets; it applies to municipal and school taxes (school only if the district adopted it), not special ad valorem levies or special assessments
    • Must be your primary residence. An unremarried surviving spouse who holds title and lives there can keep it

    Official source: NY Tax: alternative veterans exemption · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Full Property Tax Exemption for Veterans Rated P&T (Where Offered)

    P&T
    • Veteran with a permanent and total disability as a result of military service who meets all the alternative veterans exemption rules (item 1), including an honorable discharge
    • A local option: a county, city, town, village or school district may offer a full exemption from property taxes, special ad valorem levies and special assessments
    • Must be the veteran's primary residence

    Official source: NY Tax overview · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • $5 Hunting, Fishing & Trapping Licenses (40%+)

    40%+
    • New York resident military veteran with a 40% or more service-related disability
    • Your first hunting, fishing or trapping license each calendar year costs $5; the others that year are free
    • Free bowhunting and muzzleloading privileges (base hunting license required) and preference for Deer Management Permits

    Official source: DEC free/reduced fee licenses · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Access Pass: Free State Park Entry (40%+)

    40%+
    • New York resident veteran with a 40% or greater service-connected disability
    • Free vehicle entry to most state parks and DEC day-use areas, free golf greens fees and pool entry, and discounted camping and cabins at most State Park and DEC campgrounds
    • You must be present when the pass is used. Some concessionaire-run places don't accept it

    Official source: NY State Parks Access Pass · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • No Registration Fees on a VA-Allowance Vehicle

    Other rules
    • Disabled veteran who got the vehicle under the federal VA automobile allowance (Title 38, Chapter 39)
    • One vehicle only: a passenger car, suburban, or auto truck or light delivery car of 20,000 lbs or less unladen, used only for passengers
    • A rating alone does not qualify. If you replace the vehicle, new paperwork is needed

    Official source: NY DMV military & veterans · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Military Pension: Exempt from NY Income Tax

    Other rules
    • Military retirement pension paid to you as a retiree, or to your beneficiary
    • Exempt from New York State, New York City and Yonkers income taxes
    • VA benefits excluded from federal income are also not taxed by New York

    Official source: NY Tax: military and veterans · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Blind Annuity: $145.29 a Month (2026)

    Other rules
    • Legally blind wartime veteran (20/200 or worse in the better eye with correction, or a field of 20 degrees or less) who lives in and is domiciled in New York
    • Blindness does not need to be service-connected. Generally 90+ days of active duty (fewer if discharged for a service-connected disability)
    • An unremarried surviving spouse of an eligible veteran can apply. The 2026 rate is $145.29 a month

    Official source: Apply for Blind Annuity · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • MERIT Scholarship for Severely Disabled Service Members & Families

    Other rules
    • Child, spouse or financial dependent of a service member who became severely and permanently disabled from injury or illness while performing military duties (combat or not), and the member too
    • A doctor must find the disability prevents any work for pay; the member must have been a NY resident when disabled (also covers those who died or are missing in action)
    • Covers cost of attendance up to the SUNY average; no income limit; full-time study (12+ credits a term)

    Official source: HESC MERIT Scholarship · Last verified 2026-10-30

Federal benefits to look into

  • CHAMPVA: health coverage for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    It's for family members, not the veteran: the spouse or dependent child of a veteran rated P&T from a service-connected disability. It's a cost-sharing program: VA shares the cost of covered care with the family. Not free. Family members eligible for TRICARE can't get CHAMPVA.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DEA (Chapter 35): school money for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    Chapter 35 / DEA education benefits are available to the spouse and children of a veteran who is P&T due to a service-connected disability. For school or training that started on or after Aug 1, 2018, DEA pays up to 36 months of benefits.

    Official source: VA: DEA · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Social Security: faster disability decisions

    100% + P&T

    SSA expedites disability claims from veterans rated 100% P&T. Say "veteran rated 100% P&T" (or enter "Veteran 100% P&T" in the online Remarks). It doesn't guarantee approval; SSA's own rules still apply.

    Official source: SSA: Veterans · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Dental insurance for CHAMPVA families (VADIP)

    P&T

    CHAMPVA covers dental only in limited medical cases; enrollees can buy dental insurance at reduced cost through VADIP.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Your Benefit Summary Letter (proof of your rating)

    All veterans

    You can download your Benefit Summary Letter (sometimes called an award letter) on VA.gov.

    Official source: VA: Download VA letters · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • VA dental care (Class IV)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Per VA, you qualify for any needed dental care if you have a service-connected disability rated 100% disabling, or you're paid at the 100% rate because of TDIU. A temporary 100% rating does not qualify.

    Official source: VA: Dental care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DoD ID card and base privileges (100%)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    A 100% disabled veteran (DAV) and eligible family members can get a DoD Uniformed Services ID card. It gives commissary, exchange and MWR privileges and base access. It is not TRICARE.

    Official source: eCFR: 32 CFR part 161 · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Base retail access for all service-connected veterans

    Any rating

    All service-connected veterans can use commissaries, exchanges and MWR retail facilities, in person and online, per VA's matrix.

    Official source: VA: Derivative benefits matrix · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Federal student loan discharge (TPD)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Veterans whom VA has determined have a service-connected disability rated 100% or are rated totally disabled based on individual unemployability (TDIU) can have eligible federal student loans discharged.

    Official source: Federal Student Aid: TPD discharge · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • No VA home loan funding fee

    10%+

    Veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability are exempt from the VA home loan funding fee, at any compensable rating, not just 100%.

    Official source: VA: Funding fee · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • America the Beautiful Access Pass

    Other rules

    A free, lifetime pass for U.S. citizens or permanent residents with a permanent disability. It doesn't have to be 100% or service-connected.

    Official source: NPS: Access Pass · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Military Lifetime Pass (federal lands)

    All veterans

    Veterans (and Gold Star families) can get a free lifetime pass to federal recreation lands. No disability is needed.

    Official source: NPS: Veterans free access · Last verified 2026-11-01
