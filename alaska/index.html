Alaska Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Where to get help

the Alaska Office of Veterans Affairs helps veterans and families find and apply for federal, state and local benefits; all services are free of charge (Army benefits summary; 907-334-0874).

Official source: Army benefits summary · Last verified 2026-11-03

Rating guide

50% or more: $150,000 property tax exemption, free DV plate and registration, free hunt, fish and trap card (items 1 to 3) · Any service-connected rating: free state park camping pass, half-price ferry fares (items 4, 5) · VA disability compensation (or discharged for a service-connected disability): 10% hiring preference (item 6) · Veterans: 25% off state land (item 7) · Everyone: free help applying (item 8). (page 1 rating guide; each part matches the item it points to)

Last verified 2026-11-03. Official sources are on each benefit below.

Alaska state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-03

  • $150,000 Property Tax Exemption

    • Alaska resident, discharged other than dishonorably, with a service-connected disability rated 50% or more by VA or your branch, where your city or borough levies a property tax
    • Home is your primary residence; first $150,000 of assessed value is exempt from municipal property tax
    • A widow or widower (not remarried) 60 or older can keep it; your city or borough may also require PFD eligibility
    • Apply to your city or borough assessor; ask for its deadline: AS 29.45.030 (e) and (i) · Office of Veterans Affairs
    What to gather (3)
    • Your municipality's exemption application (written application required)
    • VA letter showing your rating
    • DD214

    Official source: Office of Veterans Affairs · Official source: AS 29.45.030 (e) and (i) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free DV Plate and Registration

    • Veteran with a VA-certified combined service-connected disability of 50% or more
    • One vehicle registered without charge; Disabled Veteran plate is $0 to issue and $0 to renew
    • Plate without parking privileges needs only the VA letter; the wheelchair-logo plate also needs Form 861
    • Apply through DMV: DMV license plates · Disability plates · AS 28.10.181 (d)
    What to gather (3)
    • Form 803 plate application
    • VA certification of 50% or more combined
    • Form 861 if you want disabled parking

    Official source: DMV license plates · Official source: Disability plates · Official source: AS 28.10.181 (d) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Hunt, Fish and Trap Card (50%+)

    • Alaska resident (Fish and Game definition), discharged under honorable conditions, certified 50% disabled or more from service
    • A free permanent ID card to hunt, sport fish and trap
    • Void if you claim residency benefits in another state (license, voter registration, homestead exemption)
    • Apply online while in Alaska; send the letter to adfg.license@alaska.gov: ADF&G military licenses
    What to gather (3)
    • Online application
    • Letter of disability certification
    • Free harvest record card for limited fisheries

    Official source: ADF&G military licenses · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free State Park Camping Pass

    • Veteran with a service-connected disability
    • One free camping pass for developed state park campgrounds (2023 to 2027 pass now available)
    • Not for group sites, boat launch or day use; you must be in the campsite each night
    • Apply in person at the Anchorage (907-269-8400) or Fairbanks (907-451-2705) DNR Public Information Center: DAV camping pass
    What to gather (2)
    • VA ID card marked service-connected, or a current-year VA letter, plus photo ID
    • Vehicle information

    Official source: DAV camping pass · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Half-Price Alaska Ferry Fares

    • U.S. veteran with a service-connected disability
    • 50% off the adult passenger fare between Alaskan ports; an attendant also gets 50% if a physician certifies the need
    • Not for vehicles, cabins or meals; book by phone or at a terminal; pass lasts 2 years
    • Send to dot.amhs.customer@alaska.gov or fax 907-465-8824: Pass application (PDF) · Terms (PDF)
    What to gather (2)
    • Disability Discount Pass application
    • Proof of your service-connected disability

    Official source: Terms (PDF) · Official source: Pass application (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • 10% State Hiring Preference

    • Disabled veteran: entitled to VA compensation, or discharged because of a service-connected disability
    • 10% of the points added to a passing score on a scored assessment (5% for other veterans), or a guaranteed interview when the assessment isn't scored, for open competitive jobs
    • Applies to state jobs open to all Alaska residents
    • Apply through Workplace Alaska; it flags 10-point veterans: Veteran preference · AS 39.25.159
    What to gather (2)
    • DD214
    • VA letter showing compensation

    Official source: Veteran preference · Official source: AS 39.25.159 · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • 25% Off State Land (Once)

    • Alaska resident for at least 1 year before the sale, 18 or older, with an honorable or general discharge
    • At least 90 days of active duty, unless cut short by a service-connected disability or an early separation after an overseas tour
    • A one-time 25% discount on state land you buy at auction or over the counter (some development costs excluded)
    • Send proof when DNR asks (within 30 days for over-the-counter buys): Veteran's land discount
    What to gather (3)
    • DD214 showing dates and discharge
    • Notarized Veteran's Discount Application
    • Proof of Alaska residency

    Official source: Veteran's land discount · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Help With Your Claims

    • Veterans, service members and their families
    • Helps you find and apply for federal, state and local benefits; all services are free of charge
    • Can help you get the VA letter items 1 to 6 need
    • Call 907-334-0874 or 1-888-248-3682: Office of Veterans Affairs · Army benefits summary
    What to gather (3)
    • DD214
    • Any VA letters you already have
    • List of the items above you want

    Official source: Army benefits summary · Official source: Office of Veterans Affairs · Last verified 2026-11-03
