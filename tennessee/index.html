Tennessee Disabled Veteran Benefits by Rating (2026)

8 Tennessee benefits to look into, with the rating each one starts at. Use the checker to filter by your rating.

Tennessee state benefits

Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Property Tax Relief for Disabled Veterans (2026)

    P&T
    • Veteran who owns and lives in the home as a primary residence, with: a service-connected P&T rating; or service-connected paraplegia/paralysis, loss or loss of use of 2+ limbs, or legal blindness; or 100% P&T from POW service
    • The state pays back tax on up to $175,000 of market value (2026)
    • Some unremarried surviving spouses also qualify (form F-16S)

    Official source: TDVS property tax relief · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • No County Wheel Tax (100% P&T or Former POW)

    100% + P&T
    • Veteran with a 100% permanent, total service-connected disability, or any former POW, as determined by VA
    • Exempt from the county motor vehicle privilege tax (the "wheel tax")

    Official source: TDVS wheel tax exemption · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Free Disabled Veteran License Plate

    100% + P&T
    • Tennessee resident veteran paid VA compensation for service-connected loss or loss of use of one or both feet or hands, serious vision loss in both eyes, or a 100% permanent and total rating
    • Your first plate is free (2 free if issued before May 19, 2014). A second plate costs the regular registration fee plus half the specialty-plate fee; more plates cost the regular fee plus the cost of making the plate. County fees may apply

    Official source: TN Revenue VR-13 · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • $10 Hunting & Fishing License for Life

    30%+ or P&T
    • Tennessee resident veteran who is 100% P&T from a service-connected cause, or 30%+ disabled by reason of war service
    • A one-time $10 fee buys a combo hunt/fish license plus supplements, valid for the rest of your life
    • WMA, special and federal permits are extra

    Official source: TDVS licenses · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • State Park Camping & Cabin Discounts

    All veterans
    • 100% total service-connected resident veteran: 50% off camping and overnight cabin fees
    • Any Tennessee-resident veteran: 25% off cabins and camping year-round (50% Dec–Feb), 25% off The Lodges, 20% off golf, 15% off select gift shop items. Premium sites excluded
    • A free night of camping on Veterans Day (call 1-888-867-2757)

    Official source: TDVS park discounts · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • No State Income Tax on Pay or Pensions

    All veterans
    • Tennessee has no individual income tax on wages, pensions or Social Security, so military retired pay isn't taxed by the state
    • The Hall tax on interest and dividends was fully repealed from January 1, 2021
    • VA disability compensation is federal tax-free

    Official source: TN Revenue HIT-18 · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Veterans' Preference for State Jobs

    All veterans
    • Honorably discharged veteran applying for a Preferred Service state job who meets the minimum qualifications
    • Also: spouses of veterans with a 100% service-connected or P&T rating, and unremarried surviving spouses of those who died in the line of duty, who are Tennessee voters or have lived in Tennessee for 2 years
    • A hiring preference applied by law, not a guarantee of a job

    Official source: TN DOHR veterans preference · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • No Sales Tax on Vehicle Adaptive Equipment

    Other rules
    • Honorably discharged veteran with a service-connected disability under 38 U.S.C. 3901
    • Adaptive equipment needed to drive safely is exempt from sales and use tax. This covers the equipment, not the vehicle

    Official source: TN Revenue VTR-36 · Last verified 2026-10-30

Federal benefits to look into

  • CHAMPVA: health coverage for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    It's for family members, not the veteran: the spouse or dependent child of a veteran rated P&T from a service-connected disability. It's a cost-sharing program: VA shares the cost of covered care with the family. Not free. Family members eligible for TRICARE can't get CHAMPVA.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DEA (Chapter 35): school money for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    Chapter 35 / DEA education benefits are available to the spouse and children of a veteran who is P&T due to a service-connected disability. For school or training that started on or after Aug 1, 2018, DEA pays up to 36 months of benefits.

    Official source: VA: DEA · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Social Security: faster disability decisions

    100% + P&T

    SSA expedites disability claims from veterans rated 100% P&T. Say "veteran rated 100% P&T" (or enter "Veteran 100% P&T" in the online Remarks). It doesn't guarantee approval; SSA's own rules still apply.

    Official source: SSA: Veterans · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Dental insurance for CHAMPVA families (VADIP)

    P&T

    CHAMPVA covers dental only in limited medical cases; enrollees can buy dental insurance at reduced cost through VADIP.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Your Benefit Summary Letter (proof of your rating)

    All veterans

    You can download your Benefit Summary Letter (sometimes called an award letter) on VA.gov.

    Official source: VA: Download VA letters · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • VA dental care (Class IV)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Per VA, you qualify for any needed dental care if you have a service-connected disability rated 100% disabling, or you're paid at the 100% rate because of TDIU. A temporary 100% rating does not qualify.

    Official source: VA: Dental care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DoD ID card and base privileges (100%)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    A 100% disabled veteran (DAV) and eligible family members can get a DoD Uniformed Services ID card. It gives commissary, exchange and MWR privileges and base access. It is not TRICARE.

    Official source: eCFR: 32 CFR part 161 · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Base retail access for all service-connected veterans

    Any rating

    All service-connected veterans can use commissaries, exchanges and MWR retail facilities, in person and online, per VA's matrix.

    Official source: VA: Derivative benefits matrix · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Federal student loan discharge (TPD)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Veterans whom VA has determined have a service-connected disability rated 100% or are rated totally disabled based on individual unemployability (TDIU) can have eligible federal student loans discharged.

    Official source: Federal Student Aid: TPD discharge · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • No VA home loan funding fee

    10%+

    Veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability are exempt from the VA home loan funding fee, at any compensable rating, not just 100%.

    Official source: VA: Funding fee · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • America the Beautiful Access Pass

    Other rules

    A free, lifetime pass for U.S. citizens or permanent residents with a permanent disability. It doesn't have to be 100% or service-connected.

    Official source: NPS: Access Pass · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Military Lifetime Pass (federal lands)

    All veterans

    Veterans (and Gold Star families) can get a free lifetime pass to federal recreation lands. No disability is needed.

    Official source: NPS: Veterans free access · Last verified 2026-11-01
