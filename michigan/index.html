Michigan Disabled Veteran Benefits by Rating (2026)

8 Michigan benefits to look into, with the rating each one starts at. Use the checker to filter by your rating.

Michigan state benefits

Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Disabled Veterans Property Tax Exemption (100% P&T, IU or SAH)

    100% + P&T
    • Michigan resident, honorably discharged, who is VA-rated permanently and totally disabled from service and paid at the 100% rate
    • Or rated individually unemployable, or has received VA specially adapted housing assistance
    • Your homestead is exempt from property taxes (you own it and live in it)
    • An unremarried surviving spouse keeps it, including on a home bought later

    Official source: MCL 211.7b · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Veterans' Homestead Property Tax Credit (MI-1040CR-2)

    10%+
    • Veteran with a service-connected disability (or the surviving spouse) who owns or rents a Michigan homestead
    • Michigan resident at least six months of the year
    • Credit up to $1,900, based on your property taxes, taxable value and filing category; none if total household resources are over $71,500
    • Even if you owe no income tax, the credit can come as a refund (MVAA)

    Official source: MVAA 100% benefits · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Disabled Veteran License Plate

    50%+
    • Permanent DV plate: honorable discharge plus a 100% combined rating, or under 100% but rated 100% unemployable. No registration fees and no yearly renewal (a non-expiring registration)
    • Standard DV plate: a 50% to 90% rating; regular registration fees apply and it expires on your birthday
    • One DV plate per applicant; passenger vehicle, pickup or van, not motorcycles or motor homes. A surviving spouse can transfer the plate
    • A DV plate isn't a disability parking plate: you need a placard (or Form MV-110) for that

    Official source: SOS veteran plates · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Free State Park Entry (Recreation Passport)

    50%+
    • Vehicles with a Michigan Disabled Veteran (permanent or standard), Ex-POW or Medal of Honor plate
    • Free entry to facilities that require a Recreation Passport
    • Your vehicle must have the matching specialty license plate (item 3)

    Official source: MVAA recreation page · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Free Hunting & Fishing Licenses (100% P&T or IU)

    100% + P&T
    • Michigan resident veteran rated by VA permanently and totally disabled from service and paid at the 100% rate (for disabilities other than blindness)
    • Or rated individually unemployable by VA
    • Free for any license that doesn't need a separate application (licenses that need a lottery aren't free)

    Official source: DNR license information · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Military Retired Pay Not Taxed

    Other rules
    • Military retirement benefits from the U.S. Armed Forces are exempt from Michigan income tax
    • Only the retiree, or a surviving spouse after death or disability, can claim the subtraction

    Official source: Treasury military retirement FAQ · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Children of Veterans Tuition Grant (T&P)

    100% + P&T
    • Natural or adopted child (older than 16 and under 26) of a Michigan veteran VA-rated totally and permanently disabled from service (or who died or is MIA)
    • Up to $2,800 a year toward undergraduate tuition, at least half-time, with a 2.25 GPA
    • The student has to meet Michigan residency rules and file a FAFSA every year

    Official source: Children of Veterans Tuition Grant · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Michigan Veterans Trust Fund Emergency Grants

    Other rules
    • Veteran with an honorable (or general under honorable) discharge and 180 days of active duty during wartime, or 180 days of active duty with an Armed Forces/Marine Corps Expeditionary Medal
    • Or at least one day of wartime service and separated for a disability incurred in the line of duty. Peacetime veterans with at least 180 days of service and an honorable discharge can apply during the year they turn 65
    • For an unforeseen, temporary financial emergency a grant will resolve; a key factor is whether you can manage the bill after the grant

    Official source: Michigan Veterans Trust Fund · Last verified 2026-10-30

Federal benefits to look into

  • CHAMPVA: health coverage for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    It's for family members, not the veteran: the spouse or dependent child of a veteran rated P&T from a service-connected disability. It's a cost-sharing program: VA shares the cost of covered care with the family. Not free. Family members eligible for TRICARE can't get CHAMPVA.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DEA (Chapter 35): school money for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    Chapter 35 / DEA education benefits are available to the spouse and children of a veteran who is P&T due to a service-connected disability. For school or training that started on or after Aug 1, 2018, DEA pays up to 36 months of benefits.

    Official source: VA: DEA · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Social Security: faster disability decisions

    100% + P&T

    SSA expedites disability claims from veterans rated 100% P&T. Say "veteran rated 100% P&T" (or enter "Veteran 100% P&T" in the online Remarks). It doesn't guarantee approval; SSA's own rules still apply.

    Official source: SSA: Veterans · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Dental insurance for CHAMPVA families (VADIP)

    P&T

    CHAMPVA covers dental only in limited medical cases; enrollees can buy dental insurance at reduced cost through VADIP.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Your Benefit Summary Letter (proof of your rating)

    All veterans

    You can download your Benefit Summary Letter (sometimes called an award letter) on VA.gov.

    Official source: VA: Download VA letters · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • VA dental care (Class IV)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Per VA, you qualify for any needed dental care if you have a service-connected disability rated 100% disabling, or you're paid at the 100% rate because of TDIU. A temporary 100% rating does not qualify.

    Official source: VA: Dental care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DoD ID card and base privileges (100%)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    A 100% disabled veteran (DAV) and eligible family members can get a DoD Uniformed Services ID card. It gives commissary, exchange and MWR privileges and base access. It is not TRICARE.

    Official source: eCFR: 32 CFR part 161 · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Base retail access for all service-connected veterans

    Any rating

    All service-connected veterans can use commissaries, exchanges and MWR retail facilities, in person and online, per VA's matrix.

    Official source: VA: Derivative benefits matrix · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Federal student loan discharge (TPD)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Veterans whom VA has determined have a service-connected disability rated 100% or are rated totally disabled based on individual unemployability (TDIU) can have eligible federal student loans discharged.

    Official source: Federal Student Aid: TPD discharge · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • No VA home loan funding fee

    10%+

    Veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability are exempt from the VA home loan funding fee, at any compensable rating, not just 100%.

    Official source: VA: Funding fee · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • America the Beautiful Access Pass

    Other rules

    A free, lifetime pass for U.S. citizens or permanent residents with a permanent disability. It doesn't have to be 100% or service-connected.

    Official source: NPS: Access Pass · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Military Lifetime Pass (federal lands)

    All veterans

    Veterans (and Gold Star families) can get a free lifetime pass to federal recreation lands. No disability is needed.

    Official source: NPS: Veterans free access · Last verified 2026-11-01
