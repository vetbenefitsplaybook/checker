North Dakota Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Where to get help

trained County and Tribal Veterans Service Officers help veterans and families find and apply for federal, state and local benefits, free of charge (Army benefits summary; Find a service officer).

Official source: Find a service officer · Last verified 2026-11-03

Rating guide

100% (or paid at 100% for unemployability): DAV plate with no registration fee, no excise tax on up to two vehicles, free tuition for dependents (items 2, 3, 7) · 50% or more: property tax credit, $10 hunting and $10 fishing licenses, free lifetime park entry (items 1, 4, 5, 6) · Any service-connected rating: 10 hiring points (item 8). (page 1 rating guide; each part matches the item it points to)

Last verified 2026-11-03. Official sources are on each benefit below.

North Dakota state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Disabled Veterans Property Tax Credit

    • Veteran discharged under honorable conditions (or retired) with a 50% or more service-connected rating, or paid at 100% for unemployability
    • Credit equals your rating applied to the first $9,000 of taxable value of your homestead (100% = $9,000); income and assets don't matter
    • Portable if you move; a surviving spouse can qualify (100% credit if receiving DIC)
    • File with your local assessor or county director of tax equalization by April 1: Tax Commissioner page · Guideline (PDF) · Application (PDF)
    What to gather (3)
    • Application for Disabled Veterans Property Tax Credit
    • DD214 showing honorable discharge
    • VA certificate showing your disability percentage

    Official source: Tax Commissioner page · Official source: Application (PDF) · Official source: Guideline (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • DAV Plate With No Registration Fee

    • Veteran rated 100% service-connected, or paid at 100% for unemployability, or eligible under Public Law 79-663 (VA auto grant)
    • No annual license fee on up to two vehicles under 26,000 lbs; DAV plates may park in disability spots with no extra placard
    • An unremarried surviving spouse receiving DIC keeps the exemption for one vehicle
    • Send the signed form and letter to the Motor Vehicle Division: NDDVA DAV plates page
    What to gather (2)
    • VA certification letter (100% or Public Law 663 eligibility)
    • SFN 2872 Application for Certificate of Title

    Official source: NDDVA DAV plates page · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • No Vehicle Excise (Sales) Tax

    • Qualified disabled veteran with DAV plates (100%, IU at 100%, or VA auto grant)
    • Excise (sales) tax exempt on up to two vehicles licensed with DAV plates at a time
    • A vehicle bought with a VA auto grant is exempt; if you already paid, you can get a refund
    • Claim it when you title the vehicle: NDDVA DAV plates page
    What to gather (2)
    • VA certification letter
    • Title application for the vehicle

    Official source: NDDVA DAV plates page · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • $10 Combined Hunting License

    • North Dakota resident disabled veteran with a 50% or more service-connected rating
    • One $10 license covers the general game, habitat stamp, small game and fur-bearer licenses
    • Lottery licenses (such as deer) are separate
    • Apply through Game and Fish (Specialty License): Specialty applications · N.D.C.C. 20.1-03-12(46)
    What to gather (2)
    • Resident Disabled Veteran application for licenses
    • VA letter showing your rating

    Official source: Specialty applications · Official source: N.D.C.C. 20.1-03-12(46) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • $10 Resident Fishing License

    • Resident disabled veteran with a 50% or more service-connected rating, or IU that brings you to at least 50%
    • Fishing license for $10 (regular resident price $25)
    • No $2 aquatic nuisance surcharge on this license
    • Apply through Game and Fish (Specialty License): Specialty applications · N.D.C.C. 20.1-03-12(6)
    What to gather (2)
    • Resident Disabled Veteran application for licenses
    • VA letter showing your rating

    Official source: Specialty applications · Official source: N.D.C.C. 20.1-03-12(6) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Lifetime State Park Entry

    • North Dakota resident veteran with a 50% or more service-connected rating (also former POWs and spouses of deceased qualifying veterans)
    • Free lifetime entrance for you and those in your vehicle at any state park or recreation area
    • Veterans with lower service-connected ratings can buy an annual permit for $28
    • Mail to ND Parks and Recreation, 604 E Boulevard Ave, Dept 750, Bismarck, ND 58505: Application (PDF) · Permits page
    What to gather (3)
    • Lifetime park entrance permit application (SFN 61591)
    • VA Summary of Benefits letter (no medical details)
    • Proof of ND address (or a copy of your ND ID)

    Official source: Application (PDF) · Official source: Permits page · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Tuition for Dependents

    • Child, stepchild, spouse, widow or widower of a veteran rated 100% service-connected (or IU at 100%), or who was killed or died of service-connected causes
    • No tuition or fees toward a bachelor's degree or certificate at a ND state college (flight costs excluded)
    • Residency rules apply; the student must be accepted first
    • Ask the campus financial aid or veterans office: NDDVA tuition waiver page
    What to gather (3)
    • VA letter showing the veteran's 100% rating
    • Proof of relationship and North Dakota residency
    • Acceptance letter from the campus

    Official source: NDDVA tuition waiver page · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Public Hiring Preference

    • Disabled veteran (any VA service-connected rating) applying to a state or local government job
    • 10 points on a 100-point competitive exam (5 for other veterans); first preference in non-competitive hiring
    • A disability can't disqualify you unless it keeps you from doing the job
    • Claim it with proof on each public job application: NDDVA veterans' preference · N.D.C.C. ch. 37-19.1 (PDF)
    What to gather (2)
    • DD214
    • VA letter showing your service-connected rating

    Official source: N.D.C.C. ch. 37-19.1 (PDF) · Official source: NDDVA veterans' preference · Last verified 2026-11-03
