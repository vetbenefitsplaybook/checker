Oklahoma Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Oklahoma state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-01

  • 100% Disabled Veteran Property Tax Exemption

    • Head of household honorably discharged from active service in the U.S. Armed Forces or Oklahoma National Guard, and an Oklahoma resident
    • VA-certified 100% permanent service-connected disability, or receiving compensation at the 100% rate
    • Exempts the full fair cash value of your homestead. A surviving spouse may also claim it
    • File Form 998 with the county assessor where the home is: Form 998 · Tax Commission exemptions
    What to gather (4)
    • OTC Form 998
    • Current VA benefits award letter showing 100%
    • DD-214
    • Recorded deed

    Official source: Form 998 · Official source: Tax Commission exemptions · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Sales Tax Exemption Card

    • Oklahoma resident, honorably discharged, receiving VA compensation at the 100% rate for a permanent service-connected disability
    • No sales tax on up to $25,000 a year of purchases; you must register in the Oklahoma Veterans Registry first
    • An unremarried surviving spouse may qualify for up to $1,000 a year
    • Register with ODVA, then apply on OkTAP and show the card when you buy: ODVA Veterans Registry · Tax Commission page
    What to gather (2)
    • VA letter certifying compensation at the 100% rate
    • Proof of Oklahoma Veterans Registry registration

    Official source: Tax Commission page · Official source: ODVA Veterans Registry · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Military Retired Pay Not Taxed

    • Each person may exclude 100% of retirement benefits from any component of the U.S. Armed Forces
    • No age or rating requirement
    • Enter it on Schedule 511-A, line 4 (Military Retirement): Form 511 instructions
    What to gather (2)
    • Your 1099-R for military retired pay
    • Form 511, Schedule 511-A

    Official source: Form 511 instructions · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • No-Cost Driver License and ID

    • Honorably discharged veteran receiving VA compensation at the 100% rate for a permanent disability
    • Driver license or state ID issued or renewed at no cost
    • Commercial licenses follow federal FMCSA rules, so this may not cover a CDL
    • Bring your proof to a Service Oklahoma location: Service Oklahoma military page
    What to gather (2)
    • Original VA letter proving 100% disability compensation, or your OTC sales tax exemption card
    • Your usual license documents

    Official source: Service Oklahoma military page · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Vehicle Excise Tax Exemption

    • Veteran with a 100% DAV sales tax exemption card or VA letter
    • No motor vehicle excise tax on 1 vehicle bought every 3 years
    • The 3 years run from the purchase date
    • Show your card or letter when you title the vehicle: Service Oklahoma fees page
    What to gather (2)
    • 100% DAV sales tax exemption card, or VA letter with raised seal
    • Title and purchase papers

    Official source: Service Oklahoma fees page · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • $25 Lifetime Hunting and Fishing License

    • A legal Oklahoma resident who has lived in the state at least six months and intends to remain, with VA certification of a disability, and registered with the Oklahoma Department of Veterans Affairs veterans registry
    • Lifetime combination hunting and fishing license: $25 if the disability is 60 percent or more, and $200 if it is less than 60 percent
    • Buy it from the State Wildlife Conservation Director

    Official source: 29 O.S. § 4-114 · Last verified 2026-11-09

  • Free State Park Admission

    • Any honorably discharged veteran who currently lives in Oklahoma
    • Free admission to all state-owned or state-operated parks and museums
    • No rating required
    • Show your ID and DD-214 at the park or museum: OKSTEP state benefits
    What to gather (2)
    • Oklahoma driver's license or state photo ID
    • DD-214 or similar proof of honorable discharge

    Official source: OKSTEP state benefits · Last verified 2026-11-01

Other cleared points

  • Deadline: for the property tax exemption you must own and live in the home on January 1, with your deed recorded by February 1, of the first year you claim it. Help: the Oklahoma Department of Veterans Affairs (ODVA) can help you get the VA certification letters these benefits ask for (ODVA benefits page; 405-523-4000, toll-free 888-655-2838). Official source: ODVA benefits page
