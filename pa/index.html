Pennsylvania Disabled Veteran Benefits by Rating (2026)

8 Pennsylvania benefits to look into, with the rating each one starts at. Use the checker to filter by your rating.

Pennsylvania state benefits

Last verified 2026-10-27

  • Disabled Veterans' Real Estate Tax Exemption

    100%+ or P&T
    • PA resident with a discharge under conditions other than dishonorable (honorable or general; an other-than-honorable discharge can count if VA has found you eligible for benefits)
    • Wartime service as VA defines it (the Persian Gulf War period runs from Aug 2, 1990 with no end date set), or certain awards such as an expeditionary medal (43 Pa. Code § 5a.7)
    • VA rates you 100% permanent, or TDIU, or service-connected blindness, paraplegia or loss of 2+ limbs
    • The home is your main residence, owned by you alone or with your spouse
    • Financial need: presumed if the applicant's income is $114,637 or less (joint return: half counts). VA disability pay isn't counted.

    Official source: DMVA Real Estate Tax Exemption · Last verified 2026-10-27

  • Property Tax/Rent Rebate

    Other rules
    • Homeowners and renters
    • Age 65+, a widow(er) 50+, or permanently disabled and 18+. A VA letter stating you're 100% disabled is accepted proof.
    • Household income $48,110 or less (2025 claims). Federal veterans' disability payments aren't counted.
    • Standard rebate up to $1,000

    Official source: how to apply · Last verified 2026-10-27

  • Severely Disabled Veteran Plate + Free Registration

    100%+
    • A 100% service-connected rating, or a lower service-connected rating with a condition listed on MV-145V (e.g., can't walk 200 feet without stopping to rest)
    • Lets the vehicle use disability parking spaces when it's being used for you
    • Free registration for one passenger car or truck of 9,000 lbs or less if you're 100% service-connected or paid at 100% for TDIU (standard passenger registration is $48/yr)
    • Plate fee: $14. The regular Disabled Veteran plate has no special parking privileges.

    Official source: PennDOT disability plates page · Last verified 2026-10-27

  • Veterans Designation on Your License or ID

    All veterans
    • Veterans (including Reserve and National Guard) with a DD-214 or equivalent and a discharge under conditions other than dishonorable
    • No fee for the designation; you pay the normal license/ID issue, renewal or duplicate fee
    • Stays on at every renewal. It doesn't entitle you to any discount by itself.

    Official source: PennDOT Veterans Designation · Last verified 2026-10-27

  • Veterans' Temporary Assistance (VTA)

    All veterans
    • Discharged under conditions other than dishonorable after active duty (not just training), or died in service, or has a VA-certified service-connected disability; or a surviving dependent (unmarried spouse, or certain children or dependent parents)
    • PA resident
    • A sudden, temporary hardship you didn't cause (job loss, sickness, disaster, etc.)
    • Up to $1,600 in a 12-month period for food, shelter, fuel and clothing. You don't need a 100% rating.

    Official source: DMVA VTA · Last verified 2026-10-27

  • Blind Veterans' Pension · Amputee & Paralyzed Veterans' Pension

    Other rules
    • $150 a month
    • You were a PA resident when you entered the military, with a discharge under conditions other than dishonorable (honorable or general; an other-than-honorable discharge can count if VA has found you eligible for benefits)
    • Blind: service-connected vision loss rated 90%–100% permanently disabling by VA (eye diagnostic codes 6000–6091)
    • Amputee/paralyzed: service-connected loss, or loss of use, of 2+ limbs, VA-rated 40% or more in each

    Official source: Blind pension page · Last verified 2026-10-27

  • Free Notarization for Military Service Papers

    All veterans
    • Veterans, and the surviving spouses, orphans and parents of veterans
    • Covers affidavits for pension papers and other papers connected with military service

    Official source: 51 Pa.C.S. § 9101 · Last verified 2026-10-27

  • Veteran Small-Business Startup Fee Waiver

    All veterans
    • Veterans (discharge other than dishonorable, including Reserve/Guard) and reservists
    • Small business: independently owned, 100 or fewer employees, owned and controlled by veterans/reservists
    • Waives startup filing fees, e.g., $125 LLC Certificate of Organization, $70 fictitious name. Not ongoing or renewal fees.

    Official source: PA DOS fee exemption (PDF) · Last verified 2026-10-27

Federal benefits to look into

  • CHAMPVA: health coverage for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    It's for family members, not the veteran: the spouse or dependent child of a veteran rated P&T from a service-connected disability. It's a cost-sharing program: VA shares the cost of covered care with the family. Not free. Family members eligible for TRICARE can't get CHAMPVA.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DEA (Chapter 35): school money for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    Chapter 35 / DEA education benefits are available to the spouse and children of a veteran who is P&T due to a service-connected disability. For school or training that started on or after Aug 1, 2018, DEA pays up to 36 months of benefits.

    Official source: VA: DEA · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Social Security: faster disability decisions

    100% + P&T

    SSA expedites disability claims from veterans rated 100% P&T. Say "veteran rated 100% P&T" (or enter "Veteran 100% P&T" in the online Remarks). It doesn't guarantee approval; SSA's own rules still apply.

    Official source: SSA: Veterans · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Dental insurance for CHAMPVA families (VADIP)

    P&T

    CHAMPVA covers dental only in limited medical cases; enrollees can buy dental insurance at reduced cost through VADIP.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Your Benefit Summary Letter (proof of your rating)

    All veterans

    You can download your Benefit Summary Letter (sometimes called an award letter) on VA.gov.

    Official source: VA: Download VA letters · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • VA dental care (Class IV)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Per VA, you qualify for any needed dental care if you have a service-connected disability rated 100% disabling, or you're paid at the 100% rate because of TDIU. A temporary 100% rating does not qualify.

    Official source: VA: Dental care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DoD ID card and base privileges (100%)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    A 100% disabled veteran (DAV) and eligible family members can get a DoD Uniformed Services ID card. It gives commissary, exchange and MWR privileges and base access. It is not TRICARE.

    Official source: eCFR: 32 CFR part 161 · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Base retail access for all service-connected veterans

    Any rating

    All service-connected veterans can use commissaries, exchanges and MWR retail facilities, in person and online, per VA's matrix.

    Official source: VA: Derivative benefits matrix · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Federal student loan discharge (TPD)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Veterans whom VA has determined have a service-connected disability rated 100% or are rated totally disabled based on individual unemployability (TDIU) can have eligible federal student loans discharged.

    Official source: Federal Student Aid: TPD discharge · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • No VA home loan funding fee

    10%+

    Veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability are exempt from the VA home loan funding fee, at any compensable rating, not just 100%.

    Official source: VA: Funding fee · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • America the Beautiful Access Pass

    Other rules

    A free, lifetime pass for U.S. citizens or permanent residents with a permanent disability. It doesn't have to be 100% or service-connected.

    Official source: NPS: Access Pass · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Military Lifetime Pass (federal lands)

    All veterans

    Veterans (and Gold Star families) can get a free lifetime pass to federal recreation lands. No disability is needed.

    Official source: NPS: Veterans free access · Last verified 2026-11-01
