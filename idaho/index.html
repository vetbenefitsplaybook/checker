Idaho Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Rating guide

100% (or paid at 100% for unemployability): up to $1,500 off property tax (item 1) · 100% rating: free DV plates (item 3) · Idaho residents who are 100% disabled with 100% service-related disabilities: free day-use and a free campsite (item 4) · 40%+: reduced hunting and fishing fees (item 5) · Any VA disability rating: special big game tag drawing (item 6) · Wartime veterans rated 10%+: income-based property tax cut and retirement deduction at any age (items 2, 7) · 10%+: hiring points (item 8). (page 1 rating guide; each part matches the item it points to)

Last verified 2026-11-03. Official sources are on each benefit below.

Idaho state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Up to $1,500 Off Property Tax at 100%

    • VA rates you 100% service-connected, or pays you at the 100% rate for individual unemployability, as of January 1
    • Cuts the tax on your home and up to one acre by up to $1,500, with no income limit; a surviving spouse can keep it on the same home until remarrying
    • You must own and live in the home before April 15, with a current homeowner's exemption. Reapply yearly unless you're permanent and total
    • Get the application from your county assessor or tax.idaho.gov and file it with your county assessor, January 1 to April 15: Tax Commission veteran benefit · Pub. 650 (PDF)
    What to gather (2)
    • Current VA letter confirming 100% or unemployability as of January 1
    • Application (from your assessor or tax.idaho.gov)

    Official source: Tax Commission veteran benefit · Official source: Pub. 650 (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Property Tax Reduction (Circuit Breaker)

    • Idaho homeowner who is a wartime veteran with a VA service-connected rating of 10% or more (or a VA pension), or who is a widow(er), age 65+, or a former POW, among others
    • Cuts the tax on your home and up to one acre by $250 to $1,500, based on income
    • Income limit for 2027 (your 2026 income): $40,030 after medical expenses (it changes yearly). You can take it as well as item 1
    • Apply every year with your county assessor, January 1 to April 15: Tax Commission property tax reduction · Pub. 135 (PDF)
    What to gather (3)
    • Proof of income and of medical and funeral expenses paid
    • VA disability letter
    • Proof you own the home

    Official source: Tax Commission property tax reduction · Official source: Pub. 135 (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Disabled Veteran Plates

    • Veteran with a 100% rating letter from VA
    • Plate fees and registration don't apply; limit of one set of Disabled Veteran plates
    • Choose a plate with or without the wheelchair symbol; plates can't be personalized
    • Mail to ITD Special Plates, PO Box 7129, Boise, ID 83707, or email specialplates@itd.idaho.gov: ITD 3397 (PDF) · IDVS state benefits
    What to gather (3)
    • ITD Form 3397
    • VA letter showing your 100% rating
    • Vehicle VIN and Idaho title number

    Official source: ITD 3397 (PDF) · Official source: IDVS state benefits · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free State Park Day Use and Campsite

    • Idaho resident veterans who are 100% disabled with 100% service-related disabilities
    • A free campsite while camping in Idaho State Parks, and free day-use access

    Official source: Idaho Division of Veterans Services · Last verified 2026-11-09

  • Reduced Hunting and Fishing Fees (40%+)

    • A resident with a service-connected veterans disability benefit of 40 percent or more may buy the disabled-person combination and fishing licenses. The fee is the disabled-person fee in Idaho Code 36-416
    • Idaho Code 36-416 also sets reduced Disabled American Veteran deer, elk, black bear, and turkey tags, and reduced Disabled American Veteran archery and muzzleloader permits, for residents and nonresidents
    • Nonresident Disabled American Veteran tags are limited to holders of the nonresident disabled American veteran hunting license
    • Once the department has the disability determination on file, it does not have to be proved again each year

    Official source: Idaho Code § 36-406 · Official source: Idaho Code § 36-416 · Last verified 2026-11-09

  • Disabled Veterans Special Big Game Tag

    • Idaho Fish and Game makes five free tags available each year for a disabled veteran sponsored by a qualified organization
    • Tags may be used for deer, elk, pronghorn, moose, black bear, or mountain lion, except controlled hunts with fewer than five tags
    • The tag is free, once in a lifetime, and the holder does not need a hunting license
    • Apply January 2 through January 31 of the hunt year. Idaho Division of Veterans Services screens applicants. The top two candidates it sponsors receive tags, and Fish and Game issues the other three to candidates sponsored by other qualified organizations
    • Send the application to IDVS Central Support Office, 351 Collins Road, Boise, ID 83702, or use the form linked from the state page

    Official source: Idaho Division of Veterans Services · Last verified 2026-11-09

  • Military Retirement Deduction

    • Retired service members (and unremarried surviving spouses) who are disabled at any age, age 62+, or under 62 and working
    • Disabled includes a wartime veteran with a service-connected rating of 10% or more
    • For 2025, up to $48,216 single or $72,324 joint, minus Social Security benefits
    • Claim it when you file: Tax Commission retirement deduction · Form 39R (PDF)
    What to gather (3)
    • Your DFAS 1099-R
    • VA rating letter (if claiming as disabled)
    • Form 39R, Part B, line 8

    Official source: Tax Commission retirement deduction · Official source: Form 39R (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • State Job Hiring Preference

    • Veterans discharged under honorable conditions with a service-connected rating of 10% or more, or Purple Heart
    • 10 points added to your score for state jobs (initial hire only); at 30%+, an interview if you're a qualified applicant on the register (employers don't have to interview more than 10 people in total)
    • Unremarried widow(er)s may claim the 10 points
    • Claim it when you apply for a state job: IDVS state benefits
    What to gather (2)
    • DD214
    • VA letter showing your rating

    Official source: IDVS state benefits · Last verified 2026-11-03
