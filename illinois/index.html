Illinois Disabled Veteran Benefits by Rating (2026)

8 Illinois benefits to look into, with the rating each one starts at. Use the checker to filter by your rating.

Illinois state benefits

Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Standard Homestead Exemption for Veterans with Disabilities (30%+)

    30%+
    • Illinois veteran with a VA-certified service-connected rating who receives disability compensation
    • 30% to 49%: $2,500 off equalized assessed value (EAV) · 50% to 69%: $5,000 off · 70%+: the first $250,000 of EAV is exempt
    • For your primary residence (any commercial-use part is excluded; renting it out over 6 months counts as commercial)
    • An unremarried surviving spouse keeps it while living there (and some surviving spouses qualify on their own, including DIC recipients)

    Official source: IDOR exemption guide (PIO-74) · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Specially Adapted Housing Exemption: Up to $100,000

    Other rules
    • Veteran with a disability whose home was bought, built or adapted with VA specially adapted housing funds (or donated adaptations when VA approved SAH funds)
    • Up to $100,000 off the assessed value; also available on a mobile home
    • Lasts while the veteran, spouse or unremarried surviving spouse lives there
    • You can't take this and the item 1 exemption in the same tax year

    Official source: IDOR exemption guide (PIO-74) · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Free Hunting & Fishing (10%+)

    10%+
    • Veteran receiving 10% or more service-connected compensation, or a VA total disability pension
    • No hunting or fishing license purchase required, whatever your state of residency
    • You must carry the IDVA Disability Affidavit and show it when asked while hunting or fishing

    Official source: IDVA permits page · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Free Camping at State Parks

    100%+
    • Illinois resident veteran receiving 100% service-connected compensation (or military disability retired pay, not TDRL)
    • Or with permanent loss of use of a leg, an arm or both, or unable to move without crutches or a wheelchair; former POWs also qualify
    • No camping fee at IDNR parks any day of the week (the $10 utility fee still applies at sites with utilities)
    • Your spouse, minor children and grandchildren under 18 qualify when camping with you

    Official source: IDVA permits page · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Disabled Veteran Plates: No Registration Fee (50%+)

    50%+
    • Veteran with a VA service-connected rating of 50% or more: special plates with no registration fee for one vehicle (car, motorcycle or truck up to 8,000 lb)
    • A veteran whose service-connected disability qualifies for a disability plate (certified by a physician, PA or APRN) can get plates with the access symbol, also with no registration fee
    • Renewals need eligibility proof again unless the disability is permanent

    Official source: 625 ILCS 5/3-609 · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Military Retired Pay Not Taxed

    Other rules
    • Illinois lets you subtract the federally taxed part of government retirement and government disability pay, including military plans
    • Applies to military retired pay included in your federal adjusted gross income

    Official source: IDOR Publication 120 · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Family Education Benefits (100% P&T)

    100% + P&T
    • Veteran rated by VA 100% permanent and totally disabled from service-connected causes (including by unemployability), or MIA, POW, or died of service-connected causes
    • MIA/POW Scholarship: the spouse or child gets full tuition and mandatory fees at Illinois state-supported colleges for the equivalent of 4 years (120 points)
    • Illinois residency rule: resident when entering service, within 6 months after, or living in Illinois when you apply after at least 15 straight years here since entering active duty
    • Educational Opportunity Grant: $250 a year per child aged 10 to 18 at an Illinois school or home school (veteran 100% P&T, including IU, or MIA/POW/service-connected death); apply every year

    Official source: MIA/POW Scholarship · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Illinois Veteran Grant (IVG): Tuition and Fees

    Other rules
    • Honorably discharged veteran (or honorably serving) with at least one full year of active duty (or served in a foreign country in a time of hostilities)
    • Lived in Illinois within 6 months before entering service and came back within 6 months of discharge, or live in Illinois now after 15 straight years here since leaving active duty
    • Pays tuition and mandatory fees at Illinois state-supported colleges, universities and community colleges

    Official source: IDVA Illinois Veteran Grant · Last verified 2026-10-30

Federal benefits to look into

  • CHAMPVA: health coverage for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    It's for family members, not the veteran: the spouse or dependent child of a veteran rated P&T from a service-connected disability. It's a cost-sharing program: VA shares the cost of covered care with the family. Not free. Family members eligible for TRICARE can't get CHAMPVA.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DEA (Chapter 35): school money for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    Chapter 35 / DEA education benefits are available to the spouse and children of a veteran who is P&T due to a service-connected disability. For school or training that started on or after Aug 1, 2018, DEA pays up to 36 months of benefits.

    Official source: VA: DEA · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Social Security: faster disability decisions

    100% + P&T

    SSA expedites disability claims from veterans rated 100% P&T. Say "veteran rated 100% P&T" (or enter "Veteran 100% P&T" in the online Remarks). It doesn't guarantee approval; SSA's own rules still apply.

    Official source: SSA: Veterans · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Dental insurance for CHAMPVA families (VADIP)

    P&T

    CHAMPVA covers dental only in limited medical cases; enrollees can buy dental insurance at reduced cost through VADIP.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Your Benefit Summary Letter (proof of your rating)

    All veterans

    You can download your Benefit Summary Letter (sometimes called an award letter) on VA.gov.

    Official source: VA: Download VA letters · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • VA dental care (Class IV)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Per VA, you qualify for any needed dental care if you have a service-connected disability rated 100% disabling, or you're paid at the 100% rate because of TDIU. A temporary 100% rating does not qualify.

    Official source: VA: Dental care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DoD ID card and base privileges (100%)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    A 100% disabled veteran (DAV) and eligible family members can get a DoD Uniformed Services ID card. It gives commissary, exchange and MWR privileges and base access. It is not TRICARE.

    Official source: eCFR: 32 CFR part 161 · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Base retail access for all service-connected veterans

    Any rating

    All service-connected veterans can use commissaries, exchanges and MWR retail facilities, in person and online, per VA's matrix.

    Official source: VA: Derivative benefits matrix · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Federal student loan discharge (TPD)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Veterans whom VA has determined have a service-connected disability rated 100% or are rated totally disabled based on individual unemployability (TDIU) can have eligible federal student loans discharged.

    Official source: Federal Student Aid: TPD discharge · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • No VA home loan funding fee

    10%+

    Veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability are exempt from the VA home loan funding fee, at any compensable rating, not just 100%.

    Official source: VA: Funding fee · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • America the Beautiful Access Pass

    Other rules

    A free, lifetime pass for U.S. citizens or permanent residents with a permanent disability. It doesn't have to be 100% or service-connected.

    Official source: NPS: Access Pass · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Military Lifetime Pass (federal lands)

    All veterans

    Veterans (and Gold Star families) can get a free lifetime pass to federal recreation lands. No disability is needed.

    Official source: NPS: Veterans free access · Last verified 2026-11-01
