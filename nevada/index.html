Nevada Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Where to get help

The Nevada Department of Veterans Services (NDVS) has VA-accredited Veterans Service Officers who help file claims and appeals at no cost (find an NDVS office; Las Vegas 702-224-6025, Reno 775-321-4880).

Official source: find an NDVS office · Last verified 2026-11-03

Rating guide

60%+ permanent service-connected: property tax or vehicle tax exemption (items 1, 2) · 50%+: $15 hunting and fishing license (item 5) · Any permanent service-connected rating: annual state park permit (item 6) · Receiving VA compensation for a qualifying disability: DV plates (item 4) · Surviving spouses: item 3 · Everyone: no state income tax (item 7) · Job seekers: guaranteed state job interview (item 8). (page 1 rating guide; each part matches the item it points to)

Last verified 2026-11-03. Official sources are on each benefit below.

Nevada state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Disabled Veteran Property Tax Exemption at 60%+

    • Nevada resident, honorably discharged, with a permanent service-connected disability of 60% or more
    • 2026–27 amounts off your assessed value: 60–79%: $18,200 · 80–99%: $27,300 · 100%: $36,400 (adjusted each year)
    • You can't also take the regular veterans exemption. The assessor mails a renewal form each year
    • File the affidavit with your county assessor: sample 2026/27 affidavit (Washoe, PDF) · Clark County exemptions
    What to gather (3)
    • VA letter showing the percentage of your permanent service-connected disability
    • DD214 or other proof of honorable discharge
    • Signed affidavit (assessor's form)

    Official source: Clark County exemptions · Official source: sample 2026/27 affidavit (Washoe, PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Or Use It on Your Vehicle Tax

    • Same 60%+ veterans as item 1 can apply the exemption to the governmental services tax when they register a vehicle
    • Whatever you use on vehicles reduces what is left for your property tax (same 60–79%, 80–99%, 100% tiers)
    • You may transfer the vehicle exemption to your current spouse
    • Claim it when you register or renew at the DMV: DMV veteran exemptions · NDVS tax exemption
    What to gather (3)
    • VA rating letter
    • Proof of honorable discharge
    • Annual affidavit filed with the DMV

    Official source: DMV veteran exemptions · Official source: NDVS tax exemption · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Surviving Spouse Exemption

    • You were married to and living with the veteran for the 5 years before death, have not remarried, and live in Nevada
    • Same exemption amount the veteran qualified for
    • File the affidavit with your county assessor: Clark County exemptions · NRS 361.091
    What to gather (2)
    • Veteran's VA rating letter and discharge papers
    • Surviving spouse affidavit

    Official source: Clark County exemptions · Official source: NRS 361.091 · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Disabled Veteran License Plates

    • Veteran who receives VA compensation for a service-connected disability rated 100% (or combined ratings adding up to 100%), or for a service-connected permanent disability that qualifies for disabled-person plates (NRS 482.0962)
    • Plates say DISABLED VETERAN (or DISABLED FEMALE VETERAN or VETERAN WHO IS DISABLED) with the access symbol; up to 2 sets
    • No parking fees at state or local meters or lots. Regular registration fees still apply
    • Order in person at a DMV office: DMV veterans plates · NRS 482.377
    What to gather (3)
    • DMV plate application
    • VA proof of your service-connected disability and compensation
    • Current registration, odometer reading and proof of insurance

    Official source: DMV veterans plates · Official source: NRS 482.377 · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • $15 Hunting and Fishing License at 50%+

    • A bona fide Nevada resident with a service-connected disability of 50 percent or more and an honorable discharge or certificate of satisfactory service
    • Annual resident specialty combination hunting and fishing license for $15

    Official source: NRS 502.072 and 502.240 · Last verified 2026-11-09

  • Disabled Veteran Annual State Park Permit

    • Nevada resident with a permanent service-connected disability (any rating) and an honorable discharge
    • 12 months of unlimited park entry, camping and boat launching at all Nevada state parks
    • The permit itself is free by law, but there is a $30 annual administrative fee
    • Apply to Nevada State Parks: annual permits · NRS 407.065
    What to gather (3)
    • VA letter showing a permanent service-connected disability
    • Proof of honorable discharge
    • Proof of Nevada residency

    Official source: annual permits · Official source: NRS 407.065 · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • No State Income Tax

    • All Nevada residents
    • Nevada has no personal income tax, so military retired pay, wages and other personal income are not taxed by the state
    • There is no state personal income tax return to file
    • No state return needed: Nevada Constitution, art. 10, sec. 1(9)
    What to gather (1)
    • Nothing to gather for this one

    Official source: Nevada Constitution, art. 10, sec. 1(9) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Guaranteed State Job Interview

    • Veteran with a service-connected disability who is a qualified applicant for a Nevada classified state job
    • The hiring agency must interview you
    • Veterans also get a preference in state hiring pools
    • Apply for state jobs and show your veteran and disability status: NRS 284.265
    What to gather (3)
    • DD214
    • VA letter showing a service-connected disability
    • Your application for the state job

    Official source: NRS 284.265 · Last verified 2026-11-03
