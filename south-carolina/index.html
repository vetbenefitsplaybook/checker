South Carolina Disabled Veteran Benefits by Rating (2026)

8 South Carolina benefits to look into, with the rating each one starts at. Use the checker to filter by your rating.

South Carolina state benefits

Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Home Fully Exempt from Property Tax (T&P)

    P&T
    • Veteran VA-rated totally and permanently disabled from a service-connected cause
    • Your home and land up to 5 acres are exempt, if you own it (alone or with your spouse) and it has the 4% legal residence rate
    • An unremarried surviving spouse who lives in and owns the home keeps it

    Official source: SCDOR veterans card · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Two Vehicles Exempt from Property Tax

    P&T
    • Same T&P service-connected veteran: 2 private passenger vehicles you own or lease are exempt (a vehicle in your spouse's name counts if your spouse lives with you and it is registered at your address)
    • A surviving spouse keeps the exemption on one vehicle until remarriage

    Official source: SCDOR exemptions · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Free Disabled Veteran License Plate

    P&T
    • Veteran considered totally and permanently disabled from a service-connected disability
    • No fee for this plate (exempt from the regular registration fee). Up to 3 plates in this class
    • A wheelchair version needs a doctor's certification (Form RG-007A)

    Official source: SCDMV Disabled Veteran plate · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Free 3-Year Disability Hunting & Fishing License

    10%+ (receiving VA pay)
    • U.S. citizen living in South Carolina for 365 days in a row before you apply, and receiving VA disability benefits
    • A 3-year combination license at no cost: hunting, big game, WMA, migratory waterfowl and fresh and saltwater fishing. Base deer tags mailed free
    • Renew every 3 years with fresh proof. Optional hard card $6
    • Hunting privileges need hunter education if born after June 30, 1979

    Official source: SCDNR application · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • State Park Discount (At Least 35%)

    P&T
    • South Carolina resident who is disabled (unable to work for 12+ months due to an impairment) or is a veteran VA-classified permanently and totally disabled
    • By law, park discounts for these residents must be at least 35% (S.C. Code 51-3-65)

    Official source: S.C. Code 51-3-60 · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Military Retired Pay Fully Deducted

    Other rules
    • You may deduct all military retirement income in your SC taxable income, at any age (tax year 2022 and later)
    • A surviving spouse receiving it gets the same deduction
    • VA disability compensation is federal tax-free: VA.gov

    Official source: SC Revenue Ruling 22-11 · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Free College Tuition for Your Children

    P&T
    • Child, 26 or younger, of a wartime veteran who served honorably and is P&T disabled as determined by VA (from any cause), or who was killed in action, died in service or from a service-connected cause, was a POW, is MIA, or received the Medal of Honor or Purple Heart
    • Free tuition (a tuition waiver) for an undergraduate degree at SC state-supported colleges, universities and technical schools. Residency rules apply; updated by 2026 Act 135

    Official source: SCDVA education · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Interview Preference for State Jobs

    All veterans
    • Veteran who served on active duty (not just training), was discharged under honorable conditions, and meets the job's minimum requirements
    • Agencies must interview at least one qualified veteran for every full-time position posted. A physical impairment that doesn't stop you doing the job can't disqualify you

    Official source: SC State HR memo · Last verified 2026-10-30

Federal benefits to look into

  • CHAMPVA: health coverage for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    It's for family members, not the veteran: the spouse or dependent child of a veteran rated P&T from a service-connected disability. It's a cost-sharing program: VA shares the cost of covered care with the family. Not free. Family members eligible for TRICARE can't get CHAMPVA.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DEA (Chapter 35): school money for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    Chapter 35 / DEA education benefits are available to the spouse and children of a veteran who is P&T due to a service-connected disability. For school or training that started on or after Aug 1, 2018, DEA pays up to 36 months of benefits.

    Official source: VA: DEA · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Social Security: faster disability decisions

    100% + P&T

    SSA expedites disability claims from veterans rated 100% P&T. Say "veteran rated 100% P&T" (or enter "Veteran 100% P&T" in the online Remarks). It doesn't guarantee approval; SSA's own rules still apply.

    Official source: SSA: Veterans · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Dental insurance for CHAMPVA families (VADIP)

    P&T

    CHAMPVA covers dental only in limited medical cases; enrollees can buy dental insurance at reduced cost through VADIP.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Your Benefit Summary Letter (proof of your rating)

    All veterans

    You can download your Benefit Summary Letter (sometimes called an award letter) on VA.gov.

    Official source: VA: Download VA letters · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • VA dental care (Class IV)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Per VA, you qualify for any needed dental care if you have a service-connected disability rated 100% disabling, or you're paid at the 100% rate because of TDIU. A temporary 100% rating does not qualify.

    Official source: VA: Dental care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DoD ID card and base privileges (100%)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    A 100% disabled veteran (DAV) and eligible family members can get a DoD Uniformed Services ID card. It gives commissary, exchange and MWR privileges and base access. It is not TRICARE.

    Official source: eCFR: 32 CFR part 161 · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Base retail access for all service-connected veterans

    Any rating

    All service-connected veterans can use commissaries, exchanges and MWR retail facilities, in person and online, per VA's matrix.

    Official source: VA: Derivative benefits matrix · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Federal student loan discharge (TPD)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Veterans whom VA has determined have a service-connected disability rated 100% or are rated totally disabled based on individual unemployability (TDIU) can have eligible federal student loans discharged.

    Official source: Federal Student Aid: TPD discharge · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • No VA home loan funding fee

    10%+

    Veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability are exempt from the VA home loan funding fee, at any compensable rating, not just 100%.

    Official source: VA: Funding fee · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • America the Beautiful Access Pass

    Other rules

    A free, lifetime pass for U.S. citizens or permanent residents with a permanent disability. It doesn't have to be 100% or service-connected.

    Official source: NPS: Access Pass · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Military Lifetime Pass (federal lands)

    All veterans

    Veterans (and Gold Star families) can get a free lifetime pass to federal recreation lands. No disability is needed.

    Official source: NPS: Veterans free access · Last verified 2026-11-01
