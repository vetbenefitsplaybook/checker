Utah Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Where to get help

the Utah Department of Veterans and Military Affairs (UDVMA) offers free help with VA claims, applications and appeals through accredited Veterans Service Officers (UDVMA regional offices; 801-326-2372).

Official source: UDVMA regional offices · Last verified 2026-11-03

Rating guide

10%+: property tax exemption that grows with your rating (items 1, 2) · 20%+: discounted hunting and fishing licenses (item 5) · Any service-connected rating: free state park Honor Pass, 10% state hiring preference (items 6, 8) · Disabled veterans certified by UDVMA or a military entity: DV plate (item 7) · Surviving spouses and minor orphans: item 3 · Military retirees: income tax credit (item 4). (page 1 rating guide; each part matches the item it points to)

Last verified 2026-11-03. Official sources are on each benefit below.

Utah state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Disabled Veteran Property Tax Exemption at 10%+

    • Veteran disabled in military service with a rating of at least 10%
    • Exempts up to $535,459 of your home's taxable value, scaled to your rating and unemployability (for example, 10% of the maximum at a 10% rating); the cap adjusts yearly
    • No exemption below 10%
    What to gather (3)
    • Proof of military service (DD214)
    • VA letter showing your disability rating
    • County application form

    Official source: Tax Commission Pub 36 · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Or Apply It to Your Vehicles

    • Same 10%+ veterans as item 1
    • Ask your county how to split it between your home and vehicles
    What to gather (1)
    • Same application, proof of service and VA rating letter as item 1

    Official source: Tax Commission Pub 36 · Official source: UDVMA benefit booklet (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Surviving Spouse and Minor Orphans

    • Unmarried surviving spouse or minor orphans of a veteran disabled in military service
    • Same exemption as items 1 and 2
    • Application needs proof of service and proof of disability or death
    What to gather (2)
    • Veteran's DD214
    • VA rating letter or proof of death

    Official source: Tax Commission Pub 36 · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Military Retirement Income Tax Credit

    • Utah taxpayers who received taxable military retirement pay, including survivors receiving it
    • Nonrefundable credit equal to Utah's income tax rate times your taxable military retirement pay: 4.5% on 2025 returns, 4.45% for 2026 (S.B. 60)
    • Can't be combined with the general Retirement Credit (code 18)
    • Claim it on TC-40A, Part 3, using code AJ: Tax Commission instructions
    What to gather (3)
    • Your 1099-R for military retired pay
    • Military Retirement Credit worksheet
    • Utah TC-40 and TC-40A

    Official source: Tax Commission instructions · Official source: S.B. 60 (2026) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Discounted Hunting and Fishing at 20%+

    • Utah resident with a service-connected disability of 20% or more
    • Resident disabled veteran 1-year licenses: fishing $12, hunting $25.50, combination $28.50; multiyear licenses up to 5 years at the same yearly rate
    • Apply once; renewals stay at the discounted rate
    • Apply online with DWR: DWR disabled veteran application · DWR fees
    What to gather (2)
    • Official VA or military letter, statement or card showing a rating of 20% or more
    • Your Utah ID (UtahID account to apply online)

    Official source: DWR disabled veteran application · Official source: DWR fees · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Veterans with Disabilities Honor Pass

    • Utah resident, honorably discharged, with a documented service-connected disability (any rating)
    • Free day-use entry to Utah state parks for you and up to 7 guests in the same vehicle
    • Does not cover camping, golf, OHV rider or other special fees
    What to gather (1)
    • Current VA Summary of Benefits letter

    Official source: Utah State Parks passes · Official source: UDVMA benefit booklet (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Disabled Veteran License Plate

    • Disabled veteran certified by UDVMA or another U.S. military entity (Utah DMV lists no minimum rating)
    • Can be transferred to an unmarried surviving spouse; can be personalized
    • Does not give disabled parking privileges (get a placard separately). DMV plate fees apply
    • Order online, at a DMV office or by mail: DMV Disabled Veteran plate
    What to gather (2)
    • Letter from UDVMA or another military entity certifying your disabled status
    • Current registration

    Official source: DMV Disabled Veteran plate · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • 10% State and Local Hiring Preference

    • Veteran with a service-connected disability (any percentage) or a Purple Heart
    • 10% of the total possible score is added to a passing score for career service jobs with the state, counties, cities and other public employers
    • Claim the preference when you apply: Utah Code 71A-2 (PDF)
    What to gather (3)
    • DD214
    • VA letter showing a service-connected disability
    • Your job application

    Official source: Utah Code 71A-2 (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03
