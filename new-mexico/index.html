New Mexico Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Where to get help

the New Mexico Department of Veterans Services (NMDVS) has State Benefits offices that specialize in state benefits; if you don't live near one, its Field Services offices help (NMDVS state benefits; 1-866-433-8387).

Official source: NMDVS state benefits · Last verified 2026-11-03

Rating guide

Any permanent service-connected rating: property tax exemption equal to your rating (item 1) · Any service-connected rating: $10 hunting and fishing license (item 5) · 50%+: free plates and registration, free museums pass (items 3, 8) · Permanent 100%: no residential property tax (item 1) · 100%: free lifetime hunting and fishing (item 4) · New Mexico resident veterans: veteran exemption (item 2) and free lifetime state park pass (item 7) · Military retirees: up to $30,000 of retired pay not taxed (item 6). (page 1 rating guide; each part matches the item it points to)

Last verified 2026-11-03. Official sources are on each benefit below.

New Mexico state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Disabled Veteran Property Tax Exemption

    • Honorably discharged veteran with a permanent VA service-connected disability (any rating)
    • Exempts a share of your home's taxable value equal to your VA rating, applied after the item 2 veteran exemption; at a permanent 100%, no residential property tax
    • The home must be your principal residence (joint property with your spouse counts). The rating-based amount starts with 2026 tax bills
    • Send Form 1 to NMDVS for a certificate, then take the original certificate to your county assessor: DVS Form 1 (PDF) · Bernalillo County example
    What to gather (4)
    • DVS Form 1 (check Disabled Veteran Tax Exemption)
    • Copy of your DD214
    • VA finding of service-connected disability
    • Proof of NM residency (NM driver's license or ID, voter registration, or latest NM tax return)

    Official source: Bernalillo County example · Official source: DVS Form 1 (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Veteran Exemption and Surviving Spouses

    • NM resident veteran with 90+ days of active duty (or less if discharged for a service-connected disability)
    • $10,000 of taxable value exempt for 2025, adjusted for inflation from 2026 (your county's notice of value shows the current amount); you can claim it on top of the disabled veteran exemption
    • Unremarried surviving spouse: keeps both exemptions, even if the veteran never applied for the disabled one
    • Same process as item 1: certificate from NMDVS, then your county assessor: DVS Form 1 (PDF) · NMDVS state benefits
    What to gather (3)
    • DVS Form 1 (check Veteran Tax Exemption)
    • DD214 and proof of NM residency
    • Surviving spouse: marriage certificate, veteran's death certificate, and VA finding of disability or service-related death

    Official source: NMDVS state benefits · Official source: DVS Form 1 (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Disabled Veteran Plates and Registration

    • Veteran with a 50% or higher disability rating
    • Plates for up to 2 vehicles, motorcycles included, with no fee, including the regular registration fee
    • If you also qualify for a disabled person plate, you can add the wheelchair symbol
    • Mail form MVD10353 to MVD Vehicle Services Bureau, P.O. Box 1028, Santa Fe, NM 87504-1028 (a vehicle not yet titled in New Mexico goes to an MVD field office first): MVD military and veteran plates · NMDVS state benefits
    What to gather (3)
    • Form MVD10353
    • VA letter showing your rating
    • Vehicle title or registration

    Official source: NMDVS state benefits · Official source: MVD military and veteran plates · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Lifetime Hunting and Fishing License at 100%

    • NM resident who is 100% disabled as a result of military service
    • A free Disabled Veteran Card: fish, hunt small game and get a free deer license
    • The deer license needs a successful draw or written private-landowner permission, and must be requested each license year
    • Send the Disabled Veteran Card application to NMDVS (vet.benefits@dvs.nm.gov, or State Benefits Division, 407 Galisteo St., Room 142, Santa Fe, NM 87501); the Department of Wildlife issues the card after NMDVS verifies your disability: NMDOW military and disability licenses · NMDVS state benefits
    What to gather (3)
    • Disabled Veteran Card application
    • VA letter showing your 100% rating
    • Proof of NM residency

    Official source: NMDOW military and disability licenses · Official source: NMDVS state benefits · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • $10 Hunting and Fishing License (Any Rating)

    • NM resident disabled veteran who doesn't meet the 100% rule
    • A $10 combination game-hunting and fishing license, at any service-connected percentage
    • Habitat Management and Access Validation and a Habitat Stamp may also be required
    • Buy online, by phone (1-888-248-6866) or at a license vendor: NMDOW military and disability licenses
    What to gather (2)
    • Proof of your VA disability
    • NM driver's license or ID

    Official source: NMDOW military and disability licenses · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Up to $30,000 of Military Retired Pay Not Taxed

    • Armed forces retiree (retired by years of service or disability with lifetime benefits), or the surviving spouse of one
    • Exempts up to $30,000 of armed forces retired pay from NM income tax
    • Married filing jointly: each qualifying retiree can claim $30,000
    • Claim it when you file: PIT-ADJ instructions (PDF)
    What to gather (2)
    • Your DFAS 1099-R
    • Schedule PIT-ADJ, line 24, attached to Form PIT-1

    Official source: PIT-ADJ instructions (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Lifetime State Park Pass

    • New Mexico resident veterans (any rating)
    • Free day-use, camping and utility fees at all NM state parks; you can still book campsites
    • Not covered: reservation fees ($3 to $10), group sites, cabins, yurts and special facilities. The pass isn't transferable
    • Apply to NMDVS (walk-ins can get the pass the same day): State Parks: for veterans · pass FAQ (PDF)
    What to gather (3)
    • DVS Form 15 application
    • Copy of your DD214
    • Proof of NM residency

    Official source: for veterans · Official source: pass FAQ (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free State Monuments and Museums Pass

    • NM veteran rated 50% or higher service-connected
    • A free pass to New Mexico state monuments and museums
    • For your personal use only
    • Apply through NMDVS: NMDVS state benefits
    What to gather (2)
    • Monuments and Museums Pass application (from NMDVS)
    • VA letter showing your rating

    Official source: NMDVS state benefits · Last verified 2026-11-03
