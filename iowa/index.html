Iowa Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Where to get help

the Iowa Department of Veterans Affairs (IDVA) points veterans to their County Veterans Service Office for personal help with the state programs below (IDVA state benefits; IDVA 515-252-4698).

Official source: IDVA state benefits · Last verified 2026-11-03

Rating guide

100% permanent, or P&T based on unemployability paid at 100%: no property tax on your homestead (item 1) · Permanent 100%: no driver's license fee (item 6) · Any service-connected rating: $7 lifetime hunting or fishing license (item 4) · VA-funded vehicle: free DV plate, no registration fee (item 5) · Surviving spouses and DIC recipients: item 2 · Military retirees: retired pay not taxed (item 3) · Most veterans: $4,000 property exemption, Veterans Trust Fund (items 7, 8). (page 1 rating guide; each part matches the item it points to)

Last verified 2026-11-03. Official sources are on each benefit below.

Iowa state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Disabled Veteran Homestead Tax Credit

    • Veteran with a permanent 100% service-connected rating, or P&T based on individual unemployability paid at the 100% rate
    • Credit equals 100% of the property tax on your homestead. For applications received on or after July 1, 2026, it covers the homestead up to ½ acre
    • You must own and live in the home on July 1, claim Iowa residency, and live there at least 6 months a year (military or nursing home stays excepted)
    • File with your county assessor by July 1: Iowa Dept. of Revenue credits
    What to gather (3)
    • Form 54-049
    • Current VA Benefits Paid letter
    • DD214

    Official source: Iowa Dept. of Revenue credits · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Surviving Spouse Homestead Credit

    • Surviving spouse of a veteran who received the item 1 credit: keeps it while living in the home and not remarried
    • Surviving spouse receiving DIC: qualifies too, even after remarriage
    • Same 100% credit on the homestead
    • File with your county assessor by July 1: Iowa Dept. of Revenue credits · IDVA state benefits
    What to gather (3)
    • Form 54-049
    • VA DIC letter or the veteran's credit record
    • Death certificate

    Official source: IDVA state benefits · Official source: Iowa Dept. of Revenue credits · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Military Retired Pay Not Taxed

    • Retirees of the Armed Forces, reserves or National Guard, any age, with or without a disability
    • Military retired pay from DFAS (or a similar source) is exempt from Iowa income tax; IDVA says survivor benefits are too
    • Civil service (CSRS/FERS) retirement through OPM does not count for this exemption
    • Deduct it on IA 1040, Schedule 1, line 4: Dept. of Revenue instructions
    What to gather (2)
    • Your DFAS 1099-R
    • Iowa IA 1040 and Schedule 1

    Official source: Dept. of Revenue instructions · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • $7 Lifetime Hunting or Fishing License

    • Iowa resident (90 days) who served on active federal service and is entitled to a service-connected rating (any percentage)
    • Lifetime hunting and/or fishing license for $7; lifetime fur harvester license $7
    • One extra any-deer tag for any firearm season ($33). Yearly fish and wildlife fees still apply
    • Mail or email the form to DNR Customer Service (515-725-8200): DNR application (PDF) · Iowa Code ch. 483A
    What to gather (4)
    • DNR Form 542-1412
    • DD214 showing honorable or general (under honorable conditions)
    • Current VA letter showing a service-connected rating
    • Hunter safety certificate if born after Jan. 1, 1972

    Official source: DNR application (PDF) · Official source: Iowa Code ch. 483A · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free DV Plate for a VA-Funded Vehicle

    • Seriously disabled Iowa resident veteran whose vehicle was bought with a VA automobile grant (38 U.S.C. 3901–3904)
    • No cost for the DV plate and no annual registration fee for that vehicle
    • Free parking at city lots and meters. Does not allow parking in disability spaces (get a placard separately)
    • Apply at your county treasurer's office: Iowa DOT DV plate · Iowa Code 321.105
    What to gather (2)
    • DD214
    • Documentation confirming federal funding for the vehicle

    Official source: Iowa DOT DV plate · Official source: Iowa Code 321.105 · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • No Driver's License Fee at Permanent 100%

    • Veteran with a permanent service-connected rating of 100%
    • No fee for a Class C operator license or a motorcycle license (Class M or endorsement)
    • Show proof once; the waiver carries over to future renewals
    • Show the letter when you get or renew your license: Iowa DOT fee waiver
    What to gather (1)
    • VA Summary of Benefits letter showing your combined rating (paper or electronic)

    Official source: Iowa DOT fee waiver · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Military Service Property Tax Exemption

    • Exempts up to $4,000 of taxable value (assessment years 2023 and later)
    • You can't take this exemption in the same year you take the item 1 homestead credit
    • File with your county assessor by July 1 (once per property): Iowa Dept. of Revenue credits · Iowa Code ch. 426A
    What to gather (2)
    • Form 54-146
    • DD214

    Official source: Iowa Dept. of Revenue credits · Official source: Iowa Code ch. 426A · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Iowa Veterans Trust Fund Help

    • Can help with unemployment due to service-related causes; vision, hearing and dental care; medical equipment; prescriptions; counseling
    • Also vehicle repairs, housing repairs and emergency transitional housing. The Iowa Veterans Commission approves requests
    • Apply through your County Veterans Service Office: IDVA state benefits
    What to gather (1)
    • Application from your county VSO

    Official source: IDVA state benefits · Last verified 2026-11-03
