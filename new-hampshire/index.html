New Hampshire Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Where to get help

the NH Division of Veterans Services has trained Veterans Services Officers who help veterans and families find and apply for federal, state and local benefits, free of charge (Army benefits summary; 603-624-9230).

Official source: Army benefits summary · Last verified 2026-11-03

Rating guide

Permanent and total: $700 to $5,000 property tax credit, free DV plate with no state registration fee, free driver's license, $10 lifetime hunt and fish license, children's free tuition (items 1, 4, 5, 6, 8) · P&T with a VA-adapted home: no property tax on the home (item 2) · 80% to 99% (for more than 2 years): half-price lifetime hunt and fish license (item 6) · Discharged for a service-connected disability: veterans' tax credit (item 3) · Any rating: free state park day use (item 7). (page 1 rating guide; each part matches the item it points to)

Last verified 2026-11-03. Official sources are on each benefit below.

New Hampshire state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-03

  • $700 to $5,000 Property Tax Credit (P&T)

    • Veteran with an honorable or general discharge (or an uncharacterized discharge for a service-connected disability) who has a total and permanent service-connected disability, or a double amputee or paraplegic from service; or the surviving spouse
    • A $700 yearly credit off the tax on your home; your town may adopt $701 up to $5,000
    • The town's higher amount replaces the other veterans' credits (items 2 and 3); it applies to your principal home only
    • File with your town or city assessors by April 15 (one-time permanent application): Form PA-29 (PDF) · RSA 72:35
    What to gather (3)
    • Form PA-29 (permanent application)
    • VA certification that you're rated totally and permanently disabled from service connection
    • DD214

    Official source: 35 · Official source: Form PA-29 (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • No Property Tax on a VA-Adapted Home

    • Veteran who owns a home specially adapted through a VA SAH or SHA grant (or bought with proceeds from selling one)
    • And is 100% permanent and total (including unemployability), a double amputee or paraplegic, or blind in both eyes (5/200 or less) from service
    • The homestead is exempt from all property tax; a surviving spouse can keep it
    • File with your town or city assessors by April 15: Form PA-29 (PDF) · RSA 72:36-a
    What to gather (3)
    • Form PA-29
    • Proof of your service-connected disability
    • Proof of the VA adaptation grant

    Official source: 36-a · Official source: Form PA-29 (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Veterans' Tax Credit if Discharged for Disability

    • NH resident who was discharged from the armed forces because of a service-connected disability, or the surviving spouse (veterans with 90+ days of service in a listed war or conflict also qualify)
    • A $50 yearly credit off your home's tax; towns may adopt $51 to $750, rising to up to $1,000 from April 1, 2027
    • Resident of NH for at least one year before April 1
    • File with your town or city assessors by April 15: Form PA-29 (PDF) · RSA 72:28
    What to gather (2)
    • Form PA-29
    • DD214 showing discharge for disability

    Official source: 28 · Official source: Form PA-29 (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Disabled Veteran Plate, No State Registration Fee

    • Veteran VA rates permanently and totally disabled from service, or an amputee or paraplegic who received a vehicle from the U.S. government
    • One set of special plates (with the accessibility symbol) for one vehicle at no charge, and no state registration fee; the town or city registration permit fee is waived for the first set of plates only if you served in a war or armed conflict listed in RSA 72:28
    • You may take free regular veteran plates instead, but then lose the free parking that the accessibility plate allows
    • Apply through the NH Division of Motor Vehicles: RSA 261:86 · RSA 261:141 · RSA 261:157
    What to gather (3)
    • VA letter showing permanent and total
    • DD214
    • Vehicle title or registration

    Official source: 141 · Official source: 157 · Official source: 86 · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Driver's License

    • Veteran VA rates permanently and totally disabled from a service-connected disability
    • Or a disabled veteran who received a motor vehicle from the U.S. government because of amputation or paralysis
    • No fee is charged for the driver's license
    • Ask at a DMV office when you get or renew your license: RSA 263:42 (para. III)
    What to gather (2)
    • VA letter showing permanent and total
    • Your current license or ID

    Official source: 42 (para. III) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • $10 Lifetime Hunt and Fish License (P&T)

    • NH resident veteran, discharged other than dishonorably, totally and permanently disabled from service
    • A perpetual special veteran's fishing, hunting and archery license; one-time $10 administrative fee
    • Rated 80% to 99% for more than 2 years: a lifetime license at half the regular lifetime price (Fish and Game may also approve it if rated for 2 years or less)
    • Apply to NH Fish and Game: RSA 214:13 · Army benefits summary
    What to gather (3)
    • Fish and Game disabled veteran license application
    • VA letter showing your rating
    • DD214

    Official source: 13 · Official source: Army benefits summary · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free State Park Day Use (Any Rating)

    • New Hampshire veteran with a service-connected disability of any percentage
    • No day-use admission fee at state parks: one vehicle, one parking space
    • Not for camping, the Flume Gorge, Cannon tram, metered parking or other paid activities; guests pay
    • Show your proof at the park entrance: NH State Parks passes
    What to gather (2)
    • NH Disabled Veteran plate, or a VA letter certifying a service-connected disability
    • NH driver's license

    Official source: NH State Parks passes · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Tuition for Children (P&T)

    • Child (under 27) of a veteran with a 100% total and permanent service-connected rating, discharged other than dishonorably
    • Veteran lived in NH on entering service or when rated, and lives in NH (or did at death)
    • No tuition at University System of NH schools and community colleges; the student must file the FAFSA, use other gift aid first, and use any Post-9/11 GI Bill benefits first
    • Apply with the USNH tuition waiver application (certified by UNH Military and Veteran Services) or to the Community College System of NH: USNH waiver policy · CCSNH tuition waivers
    What to gather (4)
    • VA letter showing 100% total, permanent and service-connected
    • DD214
    • FAFSA
    • Proof of NH residency

    Official source: CCSNH tuition waivers · Official source: USNH waiver policy · Last verified 2026-11-03
