Missouri Disabled Veteran Benefits by Rating (2026)

8 Missouri benefits to look into, with the rating each one starts at. Use the checker to filter by your rating.

Missouri state benefits

Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Property Tax Credit ("Circuit Breaker")

    100%+
    • You or your spouse is a veteran who became 100% disabled as a result of military service, and you paid property tax (or rent) on your Missouri home
    • A 100% service-connected veteran doesn't count VA payments as income
    • 2025 claims (filed in 2026): owners up to $1,100 (renters $750); income limit $30,000 single / $34,000 married if you owned all year
    • 2026 claims (filed in 2027), HB 594 (2025): owners up to $1,550 (renters $1,055); limits rise to $42,200 single / $48,000 married owners

    Official source: DOR property tax credit · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Full Home Exemption for Former POWs

    Other rules
    • Missouri citizen who is a former prisoner of war with a total service-connected disability
    • The home you use as a homestead is exempt from property tax

    Official source: Mo. Const. art. X, sec. 6 · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Free Disabled Veteran License Plates

    100%+
    • Missouri resident, honorably discharged, paid VA compensation for a total service-connected disability (or a disability at the VA auto-grant level)
    • One set is free: no specialty, transfer, processing or replacement fees (late renewal penalty still applies). Extra sets pay regular fees
    • Parking in disabled spots needs the wheelchair symbol (form DOR-1776)

    Official source: DOR Disabled Veteran plates · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Fish & Hunt Without a Permit (60%+ or POW)

    60%+
    • Honorably discharged veteran (resident or not) with a service-related disability of 60% or more, or a former POW
    • Fish and hunt small game without a permit. Still buy deer, turkey, elk and bear permits, the Migratory Bird permit, Duck Stamp, Conservation Order permit, and the trout permit or daily tag where required. Trapping still needs a permit
    • Nonresidents in this group pay resident prices

    Official source: MDC veteran benefits · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Military Retired Pay Not Taxed

    Other rules
    • Your total military retirement benefits included in federal income can be subtracted from Missouri income
    • VA disability compensation is federal tax-free
    • Survivor Benefit Plan annuities don't qualify for this subtraction (they may qualify for the public pension exemption)

    Official source: DOR Military Reference Guide · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Veterans' Preference for State Merit Jobs

    All veterans
    • Veterans, disabled veterans, spouses of disabled veterans and certain unmarried surviving spouses get preference in merit-system exams and, when all other relevant job-related factors are equal, in hiring
    • Honorably discharged veterans also get credit for related military training

    Official source: RSMo 36.220 · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Returning Heroes Tuition Cap ($50/Credit Hour)

    Other rules
    • Combat veteran (shown by combat theater service, combat medals, or imminent danger/hostile fire pay) discharged under honorable conditions who lives in or can vote in Missouri
    • Undergraduate tuition capped at $50 per credit hour at public colleges (keep a 2.5 GPA). Graduate tuition capped at 30% of tuition and fees
    • Use it within 10 years of your last discharge (20 years for graduate study)

    Official source: RSMo 173.900 · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Wartime Veteran's Survivors Grant (Family)

    Other rules
    • Spouse, or child under 25, of a post-9/11 Missouri combat veteran who died or was injured in combat, or became 80% disabled from combat injuries
    • Pays tuition (up to the Mizzou rate), up to $2,000 per semester for room and board and up to $500 for books. Limited to 25 students a year

    Official source: MDHEWD survivors grant · Last verified 2026-10-30

Federal benefits to look into

  • CHAMPVA: health coverage for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    It's for family members, not the veteran: the spouse or dependent child of a veteran rated P&T from a service-connected disability. It's a cost-sharing program: VA shares the cost of covered care with the family. Not free. Family members eligible for TRICARE can't get CHAMPVA.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DEA (Chapter 35): school money for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    Chapter 35 / DEA education benefits are available to the spouse and children of a veteran who is P&T due to a service-connected disability. For school or training that started on or after Aug 1, 2018, DEA pays up to 36 months of benefits.

    Official source: VA: DEA · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Social Security: faster disability decisions

    100% + P&T

    SSA expedites disability claims from veterans rated 100% P&T. Say "veteran rated 100% P&T" (or enter "Veteran 100% P&T" in the online Remarks). It doesn't guarantee approval; SSA's own rules still apply.

    Official source: SSA: Veterans · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Dental insurance for CHAMPVA families (VADIP)

    P&T

    CHAMPVA covers dental only in limited medical cases; enrollees can buy dental insurance at reduced cost through VADIP.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Your Benefit Summary Letter (proof of your rating)

    All veterans

    You can download your Benefit Summary Letter (sometimes called an award letter) on VA.gov.

    Official source: VA: Download VA letters · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • VA dental care (Class IV)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Per VA, you qualify for any needed dental care if you have a service-connected disability rated 100% disabling, or you're paid at the 100% rate because of TDIU. A temporary 100% rating does not qualify.

    Official source: VA: Dental care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DoD ID card and base privileges (100%)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    A 100% disabled veteran (DAV) and eligible family members can get a DoD Uniformed Services ID card. It gives commissary, exchange and MWR privileges and base access. It is not TRICARE.

    Official source: eCFR: 32 CFR part 161 · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Base retail access for all service-connected veterans

    Any rating

    All service-connected veterans can use commissaries, exchanges and MWR retail facilities, in person and online, per VA's matrix.

    Official source: VA: Derivative benefits matrix · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Federal student loan discharge (TPD)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Veterans whom VA has determined have a service-connected disability rated 100% or are rated totally disabled based on individual unemployability (TDIU) can have eligible federal student loans discharged.

    Official source: Federal Student Aid: TPD discharge · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • No VA home loan funding fee

    10%+

    Veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability are exempt from the VA home loan funding fee, at any compensable rating, not just 100%.

    Official source: VA: Funding fee · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • America the Beautiful Access Pass

    Other rules

    A free, lifetime pass for U.S. citizens or permanent residents with a permanent disability. It doesn't have to be 100% or service-connected.

    Official source: NPS: Access Pass · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Military Lifetime Pass (federal lands)

    All veterans

    Veterans (and Gold Star families) can get a free lifetime pass to federal recreation lands. No disability is needed.

    Official source: NPS: Veterans free access · Last verified 2026-11-01
