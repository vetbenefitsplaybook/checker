Minnesota Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Rating guide

100% permanent and total (P&T): $300,000 home value exclusion, vehicle registration, title and sales tax exemptions, MN GI Bill for your spouse and children (items 1, 6, 8) · 70%+: $150,000 home value exclusion (item 1) · 100% service-connected: free deer, small game and permanent fishing licenses (item 4) · Any service-connected rating: free state park permit (item 5) · Military retirees: retired pay subtraction (item 3). (page 1 rating guide; each part matches the item it points to)

Last verified 2026-11-01. Official sources are on each benefit below.

Minnesota state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Disabled Veteran Home Value Exclusion

    • Honorably discharged veteran with a VA service-connected rating of 70% or more, who owns and lives in a homesteaded Minnesota home
    • $150,000 of your home's market value is excluded from property tax at 70%+; $300,000 at 100% P&T
    • If the veteran doesn't own a homestead, a VA-approved primary family caregiver's homestead may qualify instead
    • Apply to your county assessor by December 31: Dept. of Revenue page
    What to gather (3)
    • DD-214 or other discharge papers
    • VA letter verifying your disability rating
    • Your county's application form

    Official source: Dept. of Revenue page · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Surviving Spouse Home Value Exclusion

    • Surviving spouse of an honorably discharged veteran rated 100% P&T, a spouse awarded DIC, or the spouse of a service member who died of a service-connected cause on active duty
    • You hold title to the homestead and live there; the $300,000 exclusion lasts until you remarry or sell or transfer the home
    • A one-time move to a home of equal or lower value can keep the exclusion. A spouse of a service member who died on active duty must first apply within 2 years of the death
    • Apply to your county assessor by December 31: Dept. of Revenue page
    What to gather (3)
    • Veteran's DD-214 (or DD-1300 / DD-2064)
    • VA letter showing the 100% P&T rating or your DIC award
    • Proof of date of death

    Official source: Dept. of Revenue page · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Military Retired Pay Subtraction

    • Military retirement pay that is taxable on your federal return: active-component retired pay, reserve-component retired pay, and Survivor Benefit Plan payments
    • The full amount is subtracted from Minnesota taxable income; no age or rating requirement
    • You can't also claim the Credit for Past Military Service. Pay only from dual-status technician service doesn't qualify
    • Claim it on Schedule M1M with your Minnesota return: Dept. of Revenue page
    What to gather (2)
    • Your 1099-R for military retired pay
    • Schedule M1M

    Official source: Dept. of Revenue page · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Free Deer, Small Game and Fishing Licenses

    • Minnesota resident veteran with a 100% service-connected disability as defined by the VA
    • Free small game license and one free deer license (firearm, archery or muzzleloader) each year; free permanent angling license (no trout stamp needed)
    • A federal duck stamp is still required to hunt waterfowl (age 16+). A permanent card can serve as yearly proof for the deer and small game licenses
    • Get hunting licenses from any license agent; the permanent angling license comes only from the DNR License Center in St. Paul: DNR military and veteran page · angling form
    What to gather (3)
    • VA proof of your 100% service-connected disability
    • Driver's license or state ID
    • DNR permanent angling license application

    Official source: angling form · Official source: DNR military and veteran page · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Free State Park Permit

    • Veteran with a service-connected disability at any rating, as determined by the VA
    • Free annual state park vehicle permit
    • Bring proof each year
    • Show your proof at any Minnesota state park: DNR military and veteran page
    What to gather (1)
    • VA award or determination letter, VA health care ID card, DoD privilege card, or a Minnesota Disabled Veteran license plate

    Official source: DNR military and veteran page · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Vehicle Registration, Title and Sales Tax Exemptions

    • Veteran with a VA 100% total and permanent service-connected rating (DVS also accepts a benefits letter showing TDIU as proof)
    • No registration tax on up to 2 passenger vehicles, one-ton pickups, motorcycles or RVs; title fee exemption for vehicles bought on or after Jan. 1, 2024; sales tax exemption for vehicles bought on or after July 1, 2024
    • You must be the vehicle's owner, lessee or trust grantor. Personalized plate fees and plate contributions still apply
    • Submit proof once at a DVS registration office, or mail it to DVS, Suite 187, 445 Minnesota St, St. Paul, MN 55101: DVS exemptions page
    What to gather (2)
    • VA benefits, award or decision letter showing 100% (or TDIU)
    • Vehicle title or purchase papers

    Official source: DVS exemptions page · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • No Sales Tax on a VA-Funded Vehicle

    • Veteran who bought a vehicle with funding from the VA
    • The VA-funded purchase may be exempt from Minnesota vehicle sales tax
    • Separate from item 6, so it can help at ratings below 100%
    • Bring the VA form or letter to DVS when you title the vehicle: DVS exemptions page
    What to gather (1)
    • VA Form 21-4502 (all 3 sections) or VA Form 10-1394 (all 4 sections), or a VA letter naming the vehicle and the grant

    Official source: DVS exemptions page · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Minnesota GI Bill for Your Family

    • Spouse or child of a veteran with a VA 100% total and permanent service-connected rating, or surviving spouse or child of a service member who died from service
    • The student must be eligible for federal Chapter 33 (Fry) or Chapter 35 (DEA) benefits, be a Minnesota resident, be under 62 and attend a Minnesota school
    • Up to $5,000 a year and $15,000 lifetime. Reapply each year and file a FAFSA
    • Apply online through MDVA or ask an MDVA education coordinator: MDVA Minnesota GI Bill
    What to gather (3)
    • VA Certificate of Eligibility for Chapter 33 or 35
    • FAFSA
    • Proof of Minnesota residency

    Official source: MDVA Minnesota GI Bill · Last verified 2026-11-01

Other cleared points

  • Deadline: apply for the property tax exclusion with your county assessor by December 31 for taxes payable the next year (Minn. Stat. 273.13, subd. 34). Help: County Veterans Service Officers (CVSOs) work in all 87 Minnesota counties and can identify and recommend benefits; MDVA's LinkVet line is 1-888-546-5838 (MDVA benefits brochure).
