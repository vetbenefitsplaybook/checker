Alabama Disabled Veteran Benefits by Rating (2026)

8 Alabama benefits to look into, with the rating each one starts at. Use the checker to filter by your rating.

Alabama state benefits

Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Homestead Exemption: No Property Tax

    P&T
    • Veteran (or anyone) who is permanently and totally disabled; no age or income limit
    • Your principal residence (a single-family home you own and live in) and up to 160 adjacent acres are exempt from all state, county and city property taxes
    • From Oct 1, 2026: VA-rated P&T veterans skip yearly re-verification. 100% P&T buyers can get a tentative certificate within 20 days, and settlement agents and loan closing officers may not count the tax in debt-to-income

    Official source: DOR homestead · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Specially Adapted Housing Home Exemption

    Other rules
    • Veteran whose home was acquired with a VA specially adapted housing grant (Public Law 702)
    • Home exempt from property tax regardless of value
    • Continues for your unremarried surviving spouse while they own and live in it (the Code says "widow")

    Official source: ADVA Laws Affecting Veterans · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Disabled Veteran Plate: No Registration Fee

    10%+
    • Alabama resident with a 10%+ service-connected rating: first plate exempt from registration and plate issuance fees
    • 51%+ (or special driving controls): extra plates $5 a year each. Under 51%: full fees on extra plates
    • You still pay ad valorem (vehicle property) tax. The DV plate itself is optional for the fee break

    Official source: DOR DV plate · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Disabled Veteran Hunting License

    40%+ (lifetime); 50%+ annual; $3.65 at 100% P&T
    • Alabama resident (90+ days) rated 100% service-connected, permanent and total: 100% Disabled Military Veteran's Appreciation Hunting License, $3.65 (includes Wildlife Heritage privileges)
    • Rated 50% or more: the 50% Disabled Military Veterans Appreciation Hunting license (half the all-game price), $18.20
    • 40%+: may buy a lifetime hunting license instead. Bait privilege is extra; night feral swine/coyote license $1.10 at 100%

    Official source: Outdoor Alabama licenses · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Discounted Fishing License

    20%+
    • Alabama resident (90+ days) veteran certified by VA as 20% or more disabled
    • Discounted Disabled Military Veteran Appreciation fishing license
    • Or buy a lifetime freshwater and/or saltwater version (20%+); price depends on whether you are 50+

    Official source: ADVA Laws Affecting Veterans · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Military Retired Pay Not Taxed

    Other rules
    • All U.S. military retirement pay and survivor benefits are exempt from Alabama income tax
    • No age or rating requirement; also exempt from county and city income taxes
    • Combat-zone pay is also exempt. VA disability benefits are also exempt from Alabama income tax

    Official source: DOR's exempt-income list · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • Free State Park Admission

    All veterans
    • Alabama resident honorably discharged veterans and active duty: free admission on days the park is open to the public
    • Nonresident active and retired military: free on state and federal holidays
    • Covers admission only; camping and other fees still apply

    Official source: ADVA Laws Affecting Veterans · Last verified 2026-10-30

  • G.I. Dependent Scholarship

    40%+
    • Children, stepchildren and spouses of a veteran rated 40%+ service-connected (or who died of service, POW or MIA), honorably discharged after 90+ days (with exceptions). The veteran must have lived in Alabama 1 year before entering service and 2 years before you apply (or other options), or 5 years before you apply if 100% P&T. The student must live in Alabama.
    • Children: 5 years (10 semesters). Spouse or unremarried widow(er): 5 years at 100% P&T, 3 years at 40-90%
    • Tuition up to $400 per credit hour, plus up to $1,000 a semester for books and fees. Children generally must start before age 26 (30 in some cases)

    Official source: ADVA G.I. Dependent Scholarship · Last verified 2026-10-30

Federal benefits to look into

  • CHAMPVA: health coverage for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    It's for family members, not the veteran: the spouse or dependent child of a veteran rated P&T from a service-connected disability. It's a cost-sharing program: VA shares the cost of covered care with the family. Not free. Family members eligible for TRICARE can't get CHAMPVA.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DEA (Chapter 35): school money for your spouse and kids

    P&T

    Chapter 35 / DEA education benefits are available to the spouse and children of a veteran who is P&T due to a service-connected disability. For school or training that started on or after Aug 1, 2018, DEA pays up to 36 months of benefits.

    Official source: VA: DEA · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Social Security: faster disability decisions

    100% + P&T

    SSA expedites disability claims from veterans rated 100% P&T. Say "veteran rated 100% P&T" (or enter "Veteran 100% P&T" in the online Remarks). It doesn't guarantee approval; SSA's own rules still apply.

    Official source: SSA: Veterans · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • Dental insurance for CHAMPVA families (VADIP)

    P&T

    CHAMPVA covers dental only in limited medical cases; enrollees can buy dental insurance at reduced cost through VADIP.

    Official source: VA: CHAMPVA care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Your Benefit Summary Letter (proof of your rating)

    All veterans

    You can download your Benefit Summary Letter (sometimes called an award letter) on VA.gov.

    Official source: VA: Download VA letters · Last verified 2026-10-24

  • VA dental care (Class IV)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Per VA, you qualify for any needed dental care if you have a service-connected disability rated 100% disabling, or you're paid at the 100% rate because of TDIU. A temporary 100% rating does not qualify.

    Official source: VA: Dental care · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • DoD ID card and base privileges (100%)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    A 100% disabled veteran (DAV) and eligible family members can get a DoD Uniformed Services ID card. It gives commissary, exchange and MWR privileges and base access. It is not TRICARE.

    Official source: eCFR: 32 CFR part 161 · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Base retail access for all service-connected veterans

    Any rating

    All service-connected veterans can use commissaries, exchanges and MWR retail facilities, in person and online, per VA's matrix.

    Official source: VA: Derivative benefits matrix · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Federal student loan discharge (TPD)

    100% (or paid at 100% for TDIU)

    Veterans whom VA has determined have a service-connected disability rated 100% or are rated totally disabled based on individual unemployability (TDIU) can have eligible federal student loans discharged.

    Official source: Federal Student Aid: TPD discharge · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • No VA home loan funding fee

    10%+

    Veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability are exempt from the VA home loan funding fee, at any compensable rating, not just 100%.

    Official source: VA: Funding fee · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • America the Beautiful Access Pass

    Other rules

    A free, lifetime pass for U.S. citizens or permanent residents with a permanent disability. It doesn't have to be 100% or service-connected.

    Official source: NPS: Access Pass · Last verified 2026-11-01

  • Military Lifetime Pass (federal lands)

    All veterans

    Veterans (and Gold Star families) can get a free lifetime pass to federal recreation lands. No disability is needed.

    Official source: NPS: Veterans free access · Last verified 2026-11-01
