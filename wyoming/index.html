Wyoming Disabled Veteran Benefits (2026)

State benefits to look into, each with its official source. This page is not a rating filter. Federal benefits are in the checker.

Where to get help

State Veteran Service Officers help veterans and families learn about and apply for federal and state benefits; call the Wyoming Veterans Commission at 800-833-5987 (Find a service officer).

Official source: Find a service officer · Last verified 2026-11-03

Rating guide

100% disabled: free lifetime bird, small game and fishing license (item 4) · 50% or more (VA compensation): DV plate with no registration fee, free lifetime fishing license, free lifetime park pass, donated big game licenses (items 3, 5, 6, 7) · 10% or more: 10% hiring advantage (item 8) · Any compensable service-connected rating: $6,000 property tax exemption (items 1, 2). (page 1 rating guide; each part matches the item it points to)

Last verified 2026-11-03. Official sources are on each benefit below.

Wyoming state benefits

Last verified 2026-11-03

  • $6,000 Veterans Property Tax Exemption

    • Disabled veteran with a compensable service-connected disability certified by VA or a branch of service
    • Wyoming resident for at least 3 years; exempts $6,000 of assessed value a year on your principal residence
    • An unremarried surviving spouse can continue the exemption
    • File with your county assessor by the fourth Monday in May, every year: DOR tax relief · Assessor contacts
    What to gather (3)
    • Sworn claim form from your county assessor
    • DD214
    • VA letter showing your service-connected rating

    Official source: Assessor contacts · Official source: DOR tax relief · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Use the Exemption on Vehicle Registration

    • Veteran who qualifies for item 1
    • Per the Department of Revenue, the exemption may be applied to the property tax on your primary residence or to motor vehicle registration
    • Filed after the May deadline? You can still claim it that year, but only to lower vehicle registration fees
    • Ask your county assessor, then use it at the county treasurer when you register: DOR tax relief · W.S. Title 39 (PDF)
    What to gather (2)
    • Approved veterans exemption claim
    • Vehicle registration renewal notice

    Official source: DOR tax relief · Official source: W.S. Title 39 (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Disabled Veteran Plate, No Registration Fee

    • Wyoming resident who receives VA compensation for a 50% or more service-connected disability
    • One plate is free and exempt from registration fees; a second DV plate (motorcycle or multipurpose vehicle only) pays regular fees
    • DV plates do not allow parking in disability spaces; you need a separate placard
    • Apply at your county treasurer at least 30 days before your registration expires: WYDOT DV plates · MV-100B (PDF)
    What to gather (3)
    • Application for Disabled Veteran Plates (MV-100B)
    • VA letter showing your rating and compensation
    • Proof of Wyoming residency if your treasurer asks

    Official source: MV-100B (PDF) · Official source: WYDOT DV plates · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Bird, Small Game and Fish License (100%)

    • Honorably discharged Wyoming resident veteran rated 100% disabled by VA
    • No charge lifetime license for game birds, small game and fishing; no conservation stamp needed under any Wyoming license
    • Valid as long as you stay a Wyoming resident
    • Apply at Game and Fish headquarters in Cheyenne or a regional office, or by mail (allow 15 business days): 100% information (PDF) · Application (PDF)
    What to gather (2)
    • Disabled Veteran License/Permit Application
    • VA letter on letterhead certifying 100% disability

    Official source: 100% information (PDF) · Official source: Application (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Lifetime Fishing License (50%+)

    • Wyoming resident receiving VA compensation for a 50% or more service-connected disability
    • No charge lifetime fishing license; no conservation stamp needed for fishing
    • Proof of residency required
    • Apply at Game and Fish headquarters or a regional office, or by mail: 50% information (PDF) · Licensing for veterans
    What to gather (3)
    • Disabled Veteran License/Permit Application
    • VA letter on letterhead certifying 50% or more compensation
    • Wyoming driver license or ID number

    Official source: Licensing for veterans · Official source: 50% information (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Free Lifetime State Park Pass

    • Wyoming resident veteran receiving VA compensation for a 50% or more service-connected disability
    • Free day use and camping at state parks and recreation areas, and free entry to state historic sites, for you and everyone in your vehicle
    • Valid as long as you keep Wyoming residency; reduced-rate annual passes for other household vehicles
    • Apply online at Reserve.Wyoming.gov, or mail or email to Wyoming State Parks, Cheyenne: Application (PDF) · Pass information (PDF)
    What to gather (3)
    • Lifetime Veteran's Pass application
    • Wyoming driver license or state ID
    • VA letter showing 50% or more

    Official source: Application (PDF) · Official source: Pass information (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • Donated Big Game Licenses

    • Veteran receiving VA compensation for a 50% or more service-connected disability
    • Hunters can surrender unused big game licenses to be reissued free to disabled veterans
    • You must be selected and sponsored by a nonprofit that provides hunts for disabled veterans
    • The sponsoring nonprofit applies to the Game and Fish License Section: Donation information (PDF) · Licensing for veterans
    What to gather (2)
    • Sponsorship by a qualifying nonprofit
    • VA proof of 50% or more compensation

    Official source: Licensing for veterans · Official source: Donation information (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03

  • 10% Public Hiring Advantage

    • Honorably discharged veteran who has lived in Wyoming for 1 year or more and applies for a first job with a public employer
    • Scored hiring: 5% advantage, or 10% with a service-connected disability of 10% or more
    • Disabled veterans get preference over able-bodied veterans; not for current public employees
    • Claim it on each state, county, city, school district or UW application: W.S. Title 19 (PDF)
    What to gather (2)
    • DD214
    • VA letter showing your service-connected rating

    Official source: W.S. Title 19 (PDF) · Last verified 2026-11-03
